# Mathematical research workflow

The user's priority is generalizable mathematics: uniform formulas,
structural explanations, exact hypotheses and counterexamples.

- When completing research work, suggest a next step and explain its goal,
  why it advances the program, and what would count as progress. Avoid
  extending a case calculation merely because more cases can be computed.
- Read the [research ledger](RESEARCH_LEDGER.md) when choosing or reporting
  mathematical extensions. Update it when a substantial result, limitation,
  proof status or research question changes. Preserve stable entry IDs.
- Organize new case calculations around the general question they test.
  A completed table is valuable evidence with finite scope, not a uniform
  theorem or a novelty claim.
- Keep published inputs, repository deductions, finite checks, conditional
  arguments and open questions separate. Supporting proof notes and
  PROOF_STATUS.md retain their authority; the ledger is their index.
- State the representation, character lattice, source normalization,
  parabolic and coefficient ring. Distinguish integral torus sources,
  Levi invariants and embedded Borel images. Torus degree-one generation
  does not settle the other two stages.
- Distinguish characteristic-zero proofs from specialized fiber lengths,
  source critical loci from target branch loci, and the degree of a
  particular Springer map from minimum Cayley degree.
- An open question here may already be answered in the literature. Record
  literature checks actually performed; absence of a located result does
  not establish priority.
- For documentation changes, use the existing documentation verifier for
  examples and local links. Refresh the release audit when its bound files
  change. Do not repeat unchanged runtime tests without a relevant reason.
