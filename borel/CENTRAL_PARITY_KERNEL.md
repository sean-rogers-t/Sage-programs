# Central parity and the entire integral Borel kernel

Status, 21 October 2026: sufficient all-degree criteria, an all-rank type C
application, a type A application, and a counterexample on Spin7/B.
These address a general question beyond the completed E7 image tables.
There is no necessary-and-sufficient root-data classification here and
no claim of literature priority.

Update, 22 October 2026: the [odd spin extension](SPIN_PARITY_EXTENSION.md)
proves that this failure does **not** extend to every Spin(2n+1)/B.
Spin9/B has an explicit all-degree stable presentation; complete independent
certificates also give stability in ranks 5 and 6, and first failures in
ranks 7 and 8 at degrees 11 and 16. In every rank, degree-four failure
occurs exactly at n=3. An arbitrary-rank classification remains unresolved.

## 1. The exact question

Let G be simply connected complex semisimple, X=X(T) its character
lattice, Q its root lattice, R=Z_(2), S=Sym_R(X_R), and

\[
 0\ne\chi:X/Q\longrightarrow\mathbb F_2.
\]

The quotient X_R/Q_R need not itself have order two; a nontrivial
mod-two character is enough to define the derivative. On S/2S let
partial_chi be the derivation determined by partial_chi(lambda)=chi(lambda)
for linear forms lambda. It lowers polynomial degree by one, squares
to zero, and commutes with W because chi kills every root.

For retained nodes I put

\[
 A_I=S^{W_I},\quad K_I=\ker(\beta^{P_I}:A_I\longrightarrow H^{2*}(G/P_I;R)),
 \quad N_I=\beta^{P_I}(A_I).
\]

The actual reduction bar(A_I)=A_I/2A_I injects into S/2S. It need not
be the full modular invariant ring. Before asking about the kernel,
one must establish

\[
 \partial_\chi(\overline A_I)\subseteq\overline A_I.          \tag{1}
\]

The desired kernel condition is

\[
 \partial_\chi((K_I+2A_I)/2A_I)\subseteq(K_I+2A_I)/2A_I.     \tag{2}
\]

This means the derivative descends to **N_I/2N_I**. Checking only that
beta(partial(f)) vanishes in ambient H/2H is weaker when N_I is
nonprimitive. No representation is chosen in this question: the sources
are full, unscaled weight sources. For a Springer character source,
the separate lattice/scaling theorem must first identify it with the
appropriate Sym(Lambda_rho) source.

## 2. Sufficient integral presentation and parabolic hypotheses

**Cofree-source criterion.** Suppose integral homogeneous polynomials
r_1,...,r_s satisfy:

1. Over Q they generate the full-flag Borel kernel, and S/(r_1,...,r_s)
   is R-torsion-free. Thus their ideal is already the actual integral
   full-flag kernel; no additional 2-saturation is needed.
2. Each r_j belongs to A_I. The polynomial source S is free over A_I
   with a basis containing 1.
3. The actual reduced source satisfies (1), and partial_chi(bar(r_j))
   belongs to the ideal generated by the bar(r_j) in S/2S.

Then (2) holds in **every degree**.

**Proof.** Torsion-freeness and the rational kernel identify the full
integral kernel with J=(r_j). The free decomposition with unit vector
gives J intersect A_I=(r_j)A_I, so this is K_I. The same decomposition
after reduction modulo two shows contraction of bar(J) to bar(A_I)
is generated there by the bar(r_j). Hypothesis 3 and the Leibniz rule
therefore prove (2). A presentation verified only over Q does not
establish hypothesis 1. Monic integral presentations or other flatness
proofs can establish it without checking degrees one at a time.

There is also an **image-descent criterion**, already used in the E7
degree-eleven note: if the full-flag derivative descends, (1) holds,
and N_I is primitive in N_empty, then it descends to N_I/2N_I. This
follows by its injection into N_empty/2N_empty and compatibility with
source differentiation. A common integral fiber basis supplies this
primitivity in the earlier good-Levi applications. These are sufficient
conditions, not necessary ones.

## 3. Type C: every rank and every standard parabolic

**Theorem.** For G=Sp_(2n)(C), every n>=1 and every standard P_I,
the nontrivial character X/Q -> F2 defines a derivative preserving the
entire actual integral Borel kernel in every degree.

**Proof.** Use the actual weight lattice X=Z epsilon1 plus ... plus
Z epsilonn. The root lattice consists of vectors of even coefficient
sum, so chi(epsilon_j)=1. The signed permutation Weyl group has
integral invariant generators

\[
 r_j=e_j(\epsilon_1^2,\ldots,\epsilon_n^2),\qquad1\le j\le n.
\]

The classical integral Borel presentation is S/(r_1,...,r_n), with
torsion-free quotient. This published presentation and the description
of the signed-permutation invariants are recalled in
[Tamvakis, introduction and section 1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.1329).
Every r_j is killed by partial_chi modulo two, term by term.

A standard Levi Weyl group consists of ordinary permutation blocks
and a possibly empty signed tail. Its **full integral invariant ring**
A_I is polynomial on the elementary functions in each ordinary block
and the elementary functions of squares in the tail. Sign invariance
forces even tail exponents over the torsion-free ring R; ordinary
symmetric polynomial theory then proves this assertion integrally.

On an ordinary block of size b,

\[
 \partial_\chi(e_j)=(b-j+1)e_{j-1};                          \tag{3}
\]

on every squared-tail generator the derivative is zero. Thus (1) holds
for the **actual** reduced invariants, without assuming modular
invariants lift.

Finally S is free over A_I with a basis containing 1: use Artin bases
on the ordinary blocks. On a signed tail, first take the squarefree
monomial basis of R[y] over R[y^2], then an ordinary Artin basis of
R[y^2] over its symmetric functions. Their products give the required
free basis over R. All hypotheses of the criterion are now proved.

This theorem is an elementary deduction from published presentations.
It does not require a fixed odd degree-one weight, a stable root
complement, or a quadratic lift h^2=B. Those stronger conditions enter
the separate E7-style root-image multiplicity formula, not kernel
stability itself. No assertion about type C root-image Smith factors
follows from derivative stability alone.

## 4. Type A: an all-degree sufficient parabolic condition

**Theorem.** For G=SL_(2r)(C), with any r>=1, the central character
chi(x_i)=1 preserves the entire integral Borel kernel for every standard
parabolic having at least one odd Levi block.

Here X_R has generators x_1,...,x_(2r), sum x_i=0. The full-flag
kernel is generated integrally by e2,...,e_(2r), with torsion-free
quotient. See [Buch, section 2.3](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~asbuch/papers/qhpartial.pdf).
The derivative is well defined modulo e1 since partial(e1)=2r=0
in F2, and (3) gives stability of the full Chern ideal.

For completeness, the odd-block condition supplies the required actual
source presentation without modular averaging. In R[x_1,...,x_(2r)],
let E=sum x_i and choose an odd block of size b. Replace x_i in that
block by x_i-E/b and leave the other variables unchanged. Together
with E the new variables give the equivariant splitting
R[x]=Sym_R(X_R)[E]. Coefficientwise invariants therefore identify A_I
with the ordinary block symmetric ring modulo E. The block Artin basis
descends modulo E to a free S over A_I with unit basis vector. Formula
(3) preserves that actual source, so the criterion applies.

Unlike the earlier quadratic-image theorem, r need not be odd. For
2r divisible by four, chi is still a quotient character, but X_R/Q_R
has order larger than two. This result concerns kernel stability,
not an extension of the earlier root-image multiplicity formula.
No necessity of the odd-block condition, or classification of all-even
type A parabolics, is asserted here.

## 5. A genuine failure: Spin7/B in degree four

An index-two root lattice, W-equivariance, and a fixed odd weight are
**insufficient**, even for the full flag. Take G=Spin7 (type B3), P=B.
In fundamental-weight coordinates u=omega1,v=omega2,z=omega3,

\[
 \epsilon_1=u,\quad\epsilon_2=v-u,\quad\epsilon_3=2z-v,
 \qquad\chi(u)=\chi(v)=0,\quad\chi(z)=1.
\]

Thus X_R/Q_R=F2, X_R=Ru plus Rv plus Rz and
Q_R=Ru plus Rv plus R(2z). The fixed-odd-weight condition holds,
and the full-flag source has a stable complement trivially. Set

\[
 q=\tfrac12\sum_i\epsilon_i^2
   =u^2-uv+v^2-2vz+2z^2,\qquad r_4=\sum_{i<j}\epsilon_i^2\epsilon_j^2.
\]

The integral quartic

\[
 F=-u^4+2u^3v-2u^2v^2+uv^3+u^2vz-uv^2z+z^4               \tag{4}
\]

belongs to the actual integral kernel, because the exact identity

\[
 4F=r_4+q(-5u^2+5uv-v^2+2vz+2z^2)                         \tag{5}
\]

puts it in the rational Borel ideal. The integral target is torsion-free,
so the rational-kernel intersection is precisely the integral kernel.
But its derivative is

\[
       \partial_\chi(\overline F)=u^2v+uv^2.                \tag{6}
\]

In degree three the entire integral kernel is
q*(Ru plus Rv plus Rz): rationally there is only the quadratic relation,
and q is monic in u, so integral monic division proves the intersection
statement. Modulo two q=u^2+uv+v^2. Division of (6) by this polynomial
has quotient v and nonzero remainder v^3. Thus (6) does **not** belong
to (K_3+2S_3)/2S_3. Condition (2) fails.

This is a written counterexample for an actual group and actual flag,
not an abstract algebra model or an uncompleted computational search.
It demonstrates why stability of rational basic relations does not
survive integral saturation automatically. The primary BGG computation
even finds the derivative's ambient Schubert coefficients all even
(4,-2,4), so an ambient H/2H test would miss this obstruction.

The unit quadratic-lift hypothesis is absent here: the primitive
quadratic has z^2 coefficient 2, and no relation z^2-B(u,v) can be
integral, since it would require q/2. The example therefore does not
refute a stronger conjecture imposing an actual unit quadratic lift.
Conversely type C shows such a lift is not necessary for kernel stability.
Types B3 and C3 have the same signed-permutation Weyl group; their
different **character lattices** give different answers.

## 6. Independent checks and implications for the program

The [primary check](experiments/central-parity-kernel.json) constructs
actual integer reflection kernels and BGG coefficient kernels before
reducing modulo two. It finds (4) and verifies its failure against the
whole primitive cubic kernel, not a guessed relation list. It also
checks the type C3 full flag, the parabolic with retained nodes {2,3}
and the parabolic with retained nodes {1,2}, through the first degree
beyond each top dimension. Those finite checks corroborate, but do not
prove, the arbitrary-rank theorem.

The [independent native audit](experiments/central-parity-kernel-audit.json)
uses the explicit Cartesian invariants, exact polynomial identity (5),
monic division and its own bit-rank check of the primitive cubic kernel.
It uses no Sage, BGG runtime, or Smith table. It also checks (3) and the
squared-generator identities in up to six variables. The all-rank
argument is the algebraic proof above. Shared mathematical inputs are
the B3 root datum and rational Borel theorem. The positive theorems are
deductions from the cited classical presentations; the checked sources
do not establish priority for our precise formulation or counterexample.

For E7, the previous all-degree image classification already implies
kernel stability on its 112-case family via the quadratic presentation.
That is a consequence of those image proofs. The new abstract sufficient
criteria above do not supply an independent all-degree structural proof
for E7: its extra integral saturation relations still require control.

**Next research target:** determine which root/character lattices admit
a derivative-compatible integral presentation after 2-saturation.
Spin7/B now supplies a concrete obstruction to test any proposed
criterion. A useful sharper test is whether the stable-complement plus
unit-quadratic-lift hypotheses suffice on actual root data, or need an
additional saturation hypothesis. No universal answer to that stronger
question is claimed here.
