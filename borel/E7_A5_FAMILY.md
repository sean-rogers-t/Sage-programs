# E7 comparisons through the odd-index A5+A2 subgroup

This continues [the remaining-parabolic calculation](E7_REMAINING_PARABOLICS.md).
The objects are the full root and weight invariant images M_I and N_I over
R=Z_(2), in polynomial degree d (cohomological degree 2d). Their relative
cokernel is being computed; the ambient cohomology/image cokernel is a
different question. No literature-priority claim is made.

## Published inputs and the new calculation

The ordinary characteristic map, rational coinvariant presentation, Weyl
conjugacy, elementary symmetric invariants, and the integral Schur bases
for type A flag fibers are classical inputs. The calculations below use
these inputs to determine particular full integral root/weight images.
They do not follow from a rational presentation alone.

Keep Bourbaki's E7 numbering. The roots alpha_2,alpha_4,alpha_5,alpha_6,
alpha_7 form A5, and alpha_1,-theta form its orthogonal A2 complement,
where theta has marks (2,2,3,4,3,2,1). Their root lattice has index three
in Q(E7). Write eta_1,eta_2 for the A2 fundamental weights. At two,

\[
 Q(E7)_R=Q(A5)_R\oplus P(A2)_R,
 \qquad X(E7)_R=P(A5)_R\oplus P(A2)_R.
\]

The determinant and actual embeddings are checked in the
[independent lattice/fiber audit](experiments/e7-a5-fiber-transfers-audit.json).
In particular, the complementary A2 characters belong to both local
lattices. This assertion would be false without localization away from
the subsystem's index three.

For standard A5 weights x_1,...,x_6 and A2 weights z_1,z_2,z_3, each with
sum zero, the E7 minimal 56 restricts to the weights

\[
 \{x_i+x_j+x_k:i<j<k\}\ \cup\ \{\pm(x_i-z_j):1\le i\le6,1\le j\le3\}.
\]

This identity is independently checked using a fresh E7 weight orbit.
Set e_j=e_j(x) and q_j=e_j(z). The degree-two trace relation eliminates
e_2=-q_2. The remaining trace relations have degrees 6,8,10,12,14,18
in variables (q_2,q_3,e_3,e_4,e_5,e_6). Their quotient has rank 672
and top polynomial degree 45. This is a maximal-rank subgroup
intermediary, **not** a standard parabolic or a complex 45-dimensional
flag variety. The [moment audit](experiments/e7-a5a2-moments-audit.json)
checks the relations and algebraic independence; the
[image audit](experiments/e7-a5a2-images-audit.json) checks the complete
source images in every degree.

## The two completed transfers

Let T_0(q)=sum m_d q^d for the intermediary's relative cokernel. Every
nonunit relative Smith factor is two. Its coefficients, degrees 0 to 45,
are

```
0,0,0,0,1,0,2,2,2,4,5,4,7,7,8,9,12,8,16,12,15,13,17,
10,18,12,15,10,15,8,13,8,8,6,8,3,5,3,2,2,2,0,1,0,0,0
```

Their sum is 293. For retained sets

\[
 I=\{1,2,4,5,6,7\},\qquad J=\{2,4,5,6,7\},
\]

of types A5+A1 and A5 respectively, the full-image formulas are

\[
 N_I^d/M_I^d\simeq(\mathbb Z/2)^{[q^d]\,([3]_qT_0)},\qquad
 N_J^d/M_J^d\simeq(\mathbb Z/2)^{[q^d]\,([2]_q[3]_qT_0)}.
\]

The first gap is degree four. There are 879 and 1758 nonunit factors
in total. These formulas cover the A5+A1 and A5 standard parabolics.

Here is why the transfer is integral and concerns the full sources.
Put h=eta_2, u=eta_1 and t=u(h-u). Then

\[
 q_2=t-h^2,\quad q_3=-ht,\quad
 h^3+q_2h+q_3=0,\quad u^2-hu+t=0.
\]

The A2 partial fiber source is free on 1,h,h^2 over R[q_2,q_3].
The full fiber source is free on these and u,uh,uh^2. The same
characters and monic relations work for Q and X. Tensoring the A5
invariant source with these free modules is exact over R: one may take
invariants coefficient by coefficient in a common module basis.
The rational coinvariant presentation proves that the fiber basis remains
independent after applying Borel. Therefore each embedded image is the
direct sum of the corresponding shifted intermediary images. Taking
the quotient gives the displayed formulas. This proof does not claim
that taking invariants after Borel is exact.

## A uniform source lemma for the even-block refinements

The following elementary invariant-ring argument supplies complete sources
for the remaining type A refinements. Its role here is a repository
deduction; no assertion of originality is intended.

Let P=R[u,v,a,b,c], with involution

\[
 \sigma(a)=-a,\qquad\sigma(b)=b-ua,\qquad
 \sigma(c)=c-va,\qquad\sigma(u)=u,\quad\sigma(v)=v.
\]

Define

\[
 A=a^2,\quad N_b=b^2-uab,\quad N_c=c^2-vac,
\]
\[
 B=2b-ua,\quad C=2c-va,\quad D=vb-uc,
 \quad E=2bc-a(vb+uc).
\]

Then

\[
 P^\sigma=R[u,v,A,N_b,N_c,B,C,D,E].
\]

As a module over K=R[u,v,A,N_b,N_c], it is generated by
1,B,C,D,E with sole relation vB-uC-2D=0.

For completeness, P is free over K on the eight square-free a,b,c
monomials: reduce c^2, then b^2, then a^2 by their monic relations.
The five displayed invariant columns have exactly the stated kernel.
Indeed, the bc coefficient first forces the E coefficient to vanish;
the b and c coefficients give 2f_B+vf_D=0 and 2f_C-uf_D=0.
Because u,v remain nonzero modulo two, f_D is divisible by two and
the relation follows. The constant coefficient gives f_1=0.

Modulo two their only relation is vB+uC=0. The b,c coefficients force
the coefficient of D to vanish, the ab,ac coefficients force the
coefficient of E to vanish, and the a coefficient has syzygy (v,u)
since u,v are relatively prime. Thus reduction of the module inclusion
into P stays injective: the generated algebra is 2-saturated in P.
After inverting two, a,B,C diagonalize the involution, so the generated
algebra is the full invariant algebra. Any invariant in P has a power
of two times itself in this algebra; saturation removes that power.
This proves the assertion in all degrees.

The ten quadratic products of B,C,D,E reduce to the five module
generators. They and the linear relation give a presentation; all
identities appear in the [source certificate](experiments/a5-even-block-sources.json).

For A5 blocks (4,2), use d_i=x_i-x_6, a=d_5, and
c_j=e_j(d_1,...,d_4). Set s=x_5+x_6 and

\[
 u=-9s,\quad v=c_3-ac_2+a^3,\quad b=c_2,
 \quad c=c_4-a^2c_2/3.
\]

The relation c_1=2a-3s makes this an invertible triangular change over
R. The last-pair transposition becomes precisely sigma. For blocks
(2,2,2), after taking symmetric functions in the first two pairs, let
their root-coordinate sums be u+a,v+a and products b,c. The last-pair
transposition again becomes sigma. Hence the lemma supplies the full
root sources in both cases, with nine generators.

Their mod-two quotients by all positive-degree full A5 root invariants
are finite. The recorded monomials give 51 and 342 generators as modules
over the full A5 root invariant ring, of rational ranks 15 and 90.
Graded Nakayama proves that these are complete source modules, before
Borel. The [independent source audit](experiments/a5-even-block-sources-audit.json)
checks the coordinate actions, identities and module spanning using a
different Groebner order. The full Borel-image calculations are independently audited below.

The latter two rows also cover their three previously audited Weyl

For the integral weight fiber, the recurrence

a_k=e_k-s a_(k-1)-t a_(k-2), a_0=1,

and relations a_5=a_6=0 give the usual Gr(2,6) basis s^i t^j with
 i+j<=4. This is the classical Schur staircase, expressed in elementary
pair variables, and is a common free source basis before Borel. Its
rational independence after Borel follows from the coinvariant
presentation. Hence the full weight images are direct sums of shifted
intermediary images. The root images need the entire 51-generator module;
they are not obtained by assuming this weight basis is a root basis.

Each root generator's matrix is integral in the full weight-image frame.
If its complete image matrix has r_0 unit pivots modulo four and r_1
additional even pivots, r_0+r_1=rank(N) proves that no Smith factor has
2-adic valuation greater than one. Thus m=rank(N)-r_0 gives the full
relative multiplicity. This finite certificate covers every nonzero
cohomological degree, without an unbounded generator guess or an
invariant-lifting assumption after Borel.

## The (2,2,2) family and a common closed formula

Write P_0(q) for the Hilbert polynomial of the 672-dimensional intermediary:

\[
 P_0(q)=
 \frac{\prod_{d\in\{2,6,8,10,12,14,18\}}(1-q^d)}
 {(1-q^2)^2(1-q^3)^2(1-q^4)(1-q^5)(1-q^6)}.
\]

Define U_0=qP_0/(1+q) and C_0=U_0-T_0, with T_0 explicitly given above.
Both are integer polynomials; C_0 has signed coefficients. For a
composition a=(a_1,...,a_k) of three, let

\[
 {n\brack b_1,\ldots,b_k}_v
 =\frac{\prod_{j=1}^n(1-v^j)}
 {\prod_i\prod_{j=1}^{b_i}(1-v^j)},\qquad \sum_i b_i=n.
\]

For the A5 even blocks (2a_1,...,2a_k), together with the complementary
A2, the complete relative multiplicity polynomial is

\[
 \boxed{\quad
 T_a(q)=U_0(q){6\brack 2a_1,\ldots,2a_k}_q
       -C_0(q){3\brack a_1,\ldots,a_k}_{q^4}.
 \quad}                                                     \tag{1}
\]

Reordering the blocks gives Weyl-conjugate systems. Thus the compositions
(3), (2,1), and (1,1,1) cover this entire even-block family. Refining the
complementary A2 to A1 or to the torus multiplies T_a by [3]_q or
[2]_q[3]_q, using the common integral source bases proved above.

In particular, put

\[
 T_{222}=U_0{6\brack2}_q{4\brack2}_q
         -C_0(1+q^4)(1+q^4+q^8).
\]

All its nonunit factors are two. It supplies the final four A5-based
standard diagrams:

| Retained nodes | Levi type | Multiplicity polynomial | Total factors two |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {1,2,3,5,7} | A2+A1^3 | T_222 | 29,982 |
| {1,2,5,7}, {2,3,5,7} | A1^4 | [3]_q T_222 | 89,946 each |
| {2,5,7} | A1^3 | [2]_q[3]_q T_222 | 179,892 |

The [two-implementation comparison](experiments/e7-even-block-222-comparison-audit.json)
checks every mod-four column and multiplicity through top degree 57.
The root source is the full 342-generator module, and the weight fiber
is the common 90-element type A flag basis. This completes twelve
standard diagrams across the A5-based family, accounting for eight
Weyl types. The [polynomial-identity certificate](experiments/e7-even-block-formula.json)
checks (1) against all independently verified tables.

The smaller correction factors are

\[
 {3\brack2,1}_{q^4}=1+q^4+q^8,\qquad
 {3\brack1,1,1}_{q^4}=(1+q^4)(1+q^4+q^8).
\]

They can also be written

\[
 {3\brack a_1,\ldots,a_k}_{q^4}
 =\frac{P_{W(C_3)}(q^2)}{\prod_i P_{W(C_{a_i})}(q^2)},
\]

using the classical C-type Weyl degree formula. This is an exact
halved-block identity in the E7 calculations. A geometric explanation
through A5-to-C3 folding, an integral comparison implementing it, and
an extension to arbitrary rank remain separate research questions.
No literature-priority claim is made for the identity or the image formulas.

## Relation to the Springer maps

The previously proved source classification gives J_56^d=12^d M_I^d
and J_133^d=36^d N_I^d for the normalized minimal and adjoint Springer
maps. Therefore (J_56+J_133)/J_56 is degreewise isomorphic to N_I/M_I,
because multiplication by 3^d is invertible on this two-primary group.
The formulas above make this comparison explicit for twelve further
standard parabolics. They do not classify the entire ambient integral image of
either Springer map.
