# Relative root/weight images for every E7 parabolic

## The complete statement

Let G be the simply connected complex group of type E7, T a maximal
torus, X=X(T) its weight lattice, and Q its root lattice. Use Bourbaki
numbering with edges (1,3),(3,4),(2,4),(4,5),(5,6),(6,7). For a proper
subset I of {1,...,7}, P_I denotes the standard parabolic whose Levi
Weyl group W_I is generated by the retained nodes I. Put R=Z_(2), and
write beta for the rational Borel characteristic map. Define

\[
 M_I^d=\beta^{P_I}((\operatorname{Sym}_R^d Q_R)^{W_I}),\qquad
 N_I^d=\beta^{P_I}((\operatorname{Sym}_R^d X_R)^{W_I}).
\]

These are the images of the **full integral invariant sources**, embedded
in H^(2d)(G/P_I;Q). Polynomial degree d means cohomological degree 2d.

**Theorem (computer-assisted).** For all 127 proper standard parabolics
and every d, there is an explicitly determined nonnegative integer m_I,d
such that

\[
 2N_I^d\subseteq M_I^d\subseteq N_I^d,\qquad
 N_I^d/M_I^d\simeq(\mathbb Z/2)^{m_{I,d}}.
                                                        \tag{1}
\]

Put T_I(q)=sum_d m_I,d q^d and P_W(q)=sum_(w in W) q^(length(w)).
Write [n]_q=1+q+...+q^(n-1). If I omits at least one of 2,5,7, then

\[
             T_I(q)=\frac{qP_{W(E7)}(q)}{(1+q)P_{W_I}(q)}. \tag{2}
\]

This covers 112 parabolics. The other fifteen have the following
polynomials. Symbols T_0,T_42,T_222 are defined below, T_5 is the complete
[E7/P6 table](E7_D5_A1_COMPARISON.md), and T_4 is the complete
[D4+A1 table](E7_D4_A1_COMPARISON.md).

The [machine-readable theorem](experiments/e7-all-parabolics-summary.json)
contains all 127 retained sets, dimensions, cohomology ranks and complete
multiplicity coefficient lists. Each polynomial vanishes above the
dimension of its flag variety. The first nonzero relative degree is one
in 112 cases, two in twelve cases, four in two cases, and eight for D6.
For G/G the comparison is trivial. Since Q and X agree at every odd
prime, (1) also determines the relative group for the global integral
sources; localization of invariant modules commutes with these finite
reflection kernels.

## The even-block formula

The index-three A5+A2 root subsystem gives a common intermediary with
Hilbert polynomial

\[
 P_0(q)=\frac{\prod_{d\in\{2,6,8,10,12,14,18\}}(1-q^d)}
 {(1-q^2)^2(1-q^3)^2(1-q^4)(1-q^5)(1-q^6)}.
\]

It has rational rank 672 and top degree 45. Its relative multiplicity
polynomial T_0 has coefficient list, in degrees 0 through 45,

```
0,0,0,0,1,0,2,2,2,4,5,4,7,7,8,9,12,8,16,12,15,13,17,
10,18,12,15,10,15,8,13,8,8,6,8,3,5,3,2,2,2,0,1,0,0,0.
```

Put U_0=qP_0/(1+q) and C_0=U_0-T_0. The polynomial C_0 has signed
coefficients. For a composition a=(a_1,...,a_k) of three, the complete
formula for the A5 blocks (2a_1,...,2a_k), with complementary A2, is

\[
 T_a(q)=U_0(q){6\brack2a_1,\ldots,2a_k}_q
             -C_0(q){3\brack a_1,\ldots,a_k}_{q^4},       \tag{3}
\]

where the brackets are Gaussian multinomials. Thus T_42=T_(2,1) and
T_222=T_(1,1,1). Refining the common A2 to A1 or to its torus multiplies
by [3]_q or [2]_q[3]_q. Reordering blocks gives Weyl conjugate cases.
The [A5-family proof](E7_A5_FAMILY.md) gives the exact source arguments,
integral transfer bases and independently checked image certificates.

Explicitly, for sum b_i=n,
\({n\brack b_1,\ldots,b_k}_v=
\prod_{j=1}^n(1-v^j)/\prod_i\prod_{j=1}^{b_i}(1-v^j)\).

Equation (3) is an exact formula for this finite E7 family. The smaller
correction factor is also

\[
 {3\brack a_1,\ldots,a_k}_{q^4}
 =P_{W(C_3)}(q^2)/\prod_i P_{W(C_{a_i})}(q^2).
\]

A geometric folding construction explaining this identity, or a theorem
in arbitrary rank, remains open in this program.

A6, D5 and D4 source/image calculations and common integral source bases.
The [D6 calculation](E7_REMAINING_PARABOLICS.md) treats E7/P1 separately.
It proves complete source components in the required range and equality
of root and weight images above that range.

For D5+A1 and D4+A1, the [full-source theorem](E7_LOCAL_SOURCES.md)
proves generation in every degree by 25 and 18 explicit integral

unbounded step. Independent audits verify the actual polynomial
witnesses, monoid closure, nonnegative expressions and Hilbert identities.
The degree-23 D5+A1 correction is necessary before Borel and redundant
after Borel. The complete image calculation therefore gives T_5 exactly.

For the twelve A5-based diagrams, a nine-generator full A5 root source,
an elementary involution lemma for even blocks, and complete 51- and
342-generator source modules establish source completeness. Independent
rational reducers and different mod-four row/column eliminations verify
the embedded images in every degree. Common A2 source bases and four
explicit Weyl conjugacies give the stated transfers.

The D4+A1 calculation uses a rank-ten quadric tower over the P6 quotient,
the full 34-generator D4 weight-source module and the full 18-generator

## Connection to the Springer morphisms

For the normalized degree-one character sources used in this repository,
the earlier lattice classification gives

\[
 \mathcal J_{56}^d=12^d M_I^d,\qquad
 \mathcal J_{133}^d=36^d N_I^d.
\]

The definitions and normalization are those in the
[E7 representation comparison](E7_REPRESENTATION_COMPARISON.md).
Consequently

\[
 (\mathcal J_{56}^d+\mathcal J_{133}^d)/\mathcal J_{56}^d
 \simeq (\mathbb Z/2)^{m_{I,d}}.                         \tag{4}
\]

Indeed the quotient is (M_I^d+3^dN_I^d)/M_I^d after cancelling 12^d,
and multiplication by 3^d is invertible on N_I^d/M_I^d. This makes the
minimal/adjoint relative Springer/Borel image comparison explicit for
every E7 parabolic. The absolute quotient H^(2d)(G/P_I;Z)/N_I^d is a
different problem.

For d>0 with nonzero cohomology, the adjoint image is contained in the
minimal image exactly when m_I,d=0. If m_I,d is positive, the two
normalized images are incomparable: their two-primary difference is
measured by (4), while the factor 3^d obstructs containment in the other
direction. Thus the tables also identify every degree in which the
representation change gives actual containment.

There is also an immediate scalar-comparison corollary. Write
n=rank N_I^d and m=m_I,d. The global relative Smith theorem gives a
basis e_1,...,e_n of N_I^d in which

\[
 M_I^d=\bigoplus_{j=1}^n 2^{\epsilon_j}\mathbb Z e_j,
 \qquad \epsilon_j\in\{0,1\},\quad\sum_j\epsilon_j=m.
\]

For positive integers a,b, compare the sources Sym(aQ) and Sym(bX).
Their degree-d images have sum and intersection generated respectively by

\[
 \gcd(a^d2^{\epsilon_j},b^d)e_j,\qquad
 \operatorname{lcm}(a^d2^{\epsilon_j},b^d)e_j.
\]

Thus the cohomology rank and the single multiplicity m determine all
relative abelian quotient groups of these scalar root/weight images,
without constructing an entire Schubert matrix. This is a formal
consequence of ordinary Smith form, applicable once the character-source
classification identifies a representation with one of these sources.
It does not supply their embedding in an integral Schubert basis.

## Published inputs, deductions and open questions

Borel's rational presentation, Weyl invariant degrees, type A Schur
bases, symmetric-polynomial invariant theory, Nakayama's lemma and Smith
normal form are established inputs. The explicit local sources, their
completeness certificates, embedded relative image lattices and formulas
(1)--(4) are the deductions verified here. Literature priority for these
particular formulas has not been established. Earlier targeted checks
are recorded in the linked notes; this completion makes no new literature
search claim.

The requested finite E7 classification is complete. Structural
explanations of the equal index corrections and halved-block pattern,
analogues for other root data, absolute weight-image cokernels, and
equivariant comparisons remain distinct next problems.
