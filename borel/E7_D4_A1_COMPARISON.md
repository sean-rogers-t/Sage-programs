# The complete D4+A1 relative image formula in E7

Let G be simply connected of type E7, with Bourbaki numbering and retained
nodes I={2,3,4,5,7}. The Levi has type D4+A1; G/P_I has dimension 50
and 7,560 Schubert classes. Let R=Z_(2), Q be the root lattice and X the
weight lattice, and define

\[
 M^d=\beta^{P_I}((\operatorname{Sym}_R^d Q_R)^{W_I}),\qquad
 N^d=\beta^{P_I}((\operatorname{Sym}_R^d X_R)^{W_I}).
\]

The target is rational cohomology containing these full integral-source
image lattices. Degree d denotes cohomological degree 2d.

**Theorem (computer-assisted).** In every degree, 2N^d is contained in
M^d, and N^d/M^d=(Z/2)^(m_d). The coefficients m_0,...,m_50 are

```
0,0,1,2,3,5,8,12,17,22,30,39,48,59,70,84,94,106,119,134,
142,151,159,168,170,169,172,170,166,156,153,141,131,114,110,
94,83,66,61,48,40,27,25,17,13,7,6,3,2,0,1.
```

There are 3,618 nonunit relative factors, all two, and the first gap is
N^2/M^2=Z/2. The [independent full image audit](experiments/e7-d4a1-tower-images-audit.json)
records the degreewise ranks and multiplicities. Equality at all odd
primes follows from Q localized there being X, so this also determines
the global integral relative group.

## Full sources before Borel

Use h=omega_6, w=omega_7 and

\[
 (u_1,...,u_5)=(\omega_1,\omega_3-\omega_1,\omega_4-\omega_3,
 \omega_2+\omega_5-\omega_4,\omega_5-\omega_2),\quad
 Y_i=2u_i-h.
\]

Put y=u_1, b=sum u_i=2omega_5, p=w-omega_5,
r=p(h-b-p), and t=w(h-w)=r+hb/2-b^2/4. D4 acts by even signed
permutations on Y_2,...,Y_5 and fixes h,y,w. The A1 reflection s_7
fixes the D4 variables and sends w to h-w.

The [full local-source theorem](E7_LOCAL_SOURCES.md) proves that the
root invariant source is generated by eighteen explicit elements of
degrees

```
1,1,2,2,4,4,4,6,6,6,8,8,8,9,10,12,14,16.
```

The proof expands in the exact integral coefficient ring
R[h,y,b,c_2,c_3,c_4,r], where c_j=e_j(u_2,...,u_5), and compares the
leading-monomial algebra with the full rational invariant Hilbert series.
The independent all-degree Hilbert identity proves completeness; the
degree-16 component calculation alone would not do so. A
[fresh generator audit](experiments/e7-d4a1-generators-audit.json) checks
the actual character lattice, reflection actions, integral coefficients
and rational invariant preimages.

Let A_4 and A_5 denote the full integral coefficient algebras for the
preceding D4 and D5 actions, without the fixed character w. Their
all-degree completeness and a 34-generator A_5-module for A_4 were
proved in the [earlier D4 note](E7_D4_REFINEMENT.md). The same coefficient
algebras occur here. Since s_7 fixes them and w maps to h-w, the full
weight sources are A_4[t] and A_5[t]. The invariant assertion follows
from the monic rank-two basis 1,w over D[w(h-w)] for any torsion-free
coefficient domain D, and coefficientwise invariance. Hence the same
34 module generators are complete before Borel. They need not form a
free module basis; rational rank ten does not imply integral freeness.

Here is the integral identification behind that reuse. In the earlier
E6 root span, let Omega_j be the E6 fundamental weights. Since its
Cartan determinant is three, they are a basis over R. Map

\[
 (\Omega_1,\Omega_2,\Omega_3,\Omega_4,\Omega_5,\Omega_6)
 \longmapsto
 (\omega_6,\omega_2,\omega_5,\omega_4,\omega_3,\omega_1).
\]

This identifies that local lattice with U=R{omega_1,...,omega_6}.
The E6 opposition permutation carries the old D5 nodes {2,3,4,5,6}
to {1,2,3,4,5} and preserves the D4 node set. The full reflection
matrices intertwine because the relevant target roots have no omega_7
coefficient. The old five u-coordinates map to the reverse of the five
new u-coordinates; old h and y map to new h and y. Thus the actual
invariant rings and their 34-generator module transport before Borel.
Finally X_R=U direct-sum Rw, and s_7 fixes U and sends w to h-w.
The [independent lattice-transfer audit](experiments/e7-d4a1-weight-transfer-audit.json)
checks all five intertwining identities, both node sets, every coordinate
map and the commuting A1 action. Abstract agreement of the Levi types
alone would not justify this integral transfer.

## A rank-ten quadric tower

The audited E7/P6 quotient is the common base. Put

\[
 E_j=e_j(Y_1^2,...,Y_5^2),\quad p_5=\prod_{i=1}^5Y_i,
 \quad x=Y_1=2y-h,\quad p_4=\prod_{i=2}^5Y_i.
\]

The base quadratic relation is E_1=8t-3h^2. Over the rational base
quotient, the D4 tower has relations

\[
 xp_4=p_5,\qquad
 p_4^2=E_4-x^2E_3+x^4E_2-x^6E_1+x^8.
\]

Their consequence is

\[
 x^9=p_5p_4-E_4x+E_3x^3-E_2x^5+E_1x^7.
\]

The usual D5/D4 quadric invariant presentation has rational basis
1,x,...,x^8,p_4. Its relative degrees are 0,1,...,8,4, whose sum of
graded copies of the rank-756 P6 quotient gives rank 7,560 and top
degree 50. Rational independence follows before Borel from the invariant
presentation and hence after imposing the common base relations.

For the lattice computation a different reference frame is used:
1,y,...,y^8,g, where g is an actual integral quartic D4 invariant with
nonzero constant p_4 coefficient. This is a rational frame inside the
weight source. The full weight image is generated by the entire
34-element source module, rather than by an assumed integral quadric
basis. The eighteen full root generators are reconstructed in the same
tower from their exact classical-invariant preimages.

Universal source polynomials are transported before reducing modulo the
new base relations. In particular, old complement powers appearing as
w_old^2 are replaced by -3t. Substitution into old, already reduced
Borel coordinates would be invalid: that old reduction mixes odd powers
of w_old. Both implementations reconstruct the source expressions first.

## Embedded lattice proof and independent verification

In each degree the primary calculation forms the full weight image N
from the 34 source module generators and the root image M recursively
from the eighteen algebra generators. Local Hermite bases give
C=N_basis^(-1) M_basis. Exact checks show that C and 2C^(-1) have
odd denominators. Thus M is contained in N and contains 2N; the
multiplicity is rank(N)-rank(C modulo 2).

The [primary certificate](experiments/e7-d4a1-tower-final.json) stores
the rational tower, actual generator multiplication components and image
bases. It was designed before the all-degree source theorem: its
conservative `full_root_image_proved` flags only use bounded components
and a finer-flag intersection bound. They are superseded by the separate
all-degree source theorem and the full image audit linked above.

The native Python/FLINT audit imports no Sage or primary experiment code.
It reconstructs the tower reducer, every root expression, the 34 weight
module images and all multiplication components. In each degree it
checks that the weight columns are integral and primitive in the stated
weight frame. It reconstructs all products of the root generators with
the previously verified root image. Independent row elimination modulo
four proves that every relative Smith factor has valuation zero or one,
and that the reconstructed products have exactly the stated lattice.
The induction starts at the unit in degree zero and runs through 50.

The proof shares the separately audited P6 base quotient, D4 source
module and source polynomials. It verifies the new arithmetic with a
different implementation; it does not claim disjoint underlying inputs.
The finer-flag intersection comparison is supplementary and can be
strict. Its supplementary transition matrices are not replayed by the
full image audit and are not needed for the theorem. Taking Weyl
invariants after Borel is not used to establish the full root image.

## Springer interpretation and scope

The earlier normalized source theorem gives J_56^d=12^d M^d and
J_133^d=36^d N^d. Consequently
(J_56^d+J_133^d)/J_56^d=(Z/2)^(m_d), as in the
[complete E7 statement](E7_ALL_PARABOLICS.md).

Borel's presentation, even signed permutation invariants, the quadric
tower, local lattice criteria and the Hilbert/Nakayama method are known
inputs. The explicit integral source and embedded image certificates
establish the particular relative formula above. No literature-priority
claim is made. The ambient quotient H^(2d)(G/P_I;Z)/N^d, a structural
formula for these multiplicities, and extensions to other root data
remain separate questions.
