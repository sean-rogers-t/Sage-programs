# E7 D5 refinements: complete integral image comparison

This note proves the two D5 cases left by the
[full-flag/A6 comparison](E7_FULL_FLAG_COMPARISON.md). It uses the full
2-local invariant source, including its extra integral generators.
The proof is computer-assisted, with a separate arithmetic audit. Classical
inputs, the deduction, finite scope and unresolved literature priority
are distinguished below.

## 1. The theorem and its Springer meaning

Let G be simply connected of type E7 over C, with Bourbaki numbering
(edges 1--3, 3--4, 2--4, 4--5, 5--6, 6--7). Write X for its weight
lattice, Q for its root lattice, W for its Weyl group, and R=Z_(2).
The retained simple roots of the parabolic are either

\[
 I=\{2,3,4,5,6\},\qquad I'=\{1,2,3,4,5\}.
\]

Thus these are two-node-omitted refinements of P7, not maximal parabolics.
For either retained set J, define the full unscaled Borel images

\[
 M_J^d=\beta^{P_J}((\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathbb Z}^dQ)^{W_J}),
 \qquad
 N_J^d=\beta^{P_J}((\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathbb Z}^dX)^{W_J}).
\]

Polynomial degree d means cohomological degree 2d. These are embedded
integral images of full rational rank; the quotient below compares the
images to each other, not either image to ambient cohomology.

**Theorem.** For both J=I and J=I', in every degree,

\[
 N_J^d/M_J^d\simeq(\mathbb Z/2)^{m_d},\qquad
 \sum_{d=0}^{43}m_dq^d
 =\frac{qP_{W(E7)}(q)}{(1+q)P_{W(D5)}(q)}
 =q(1+q^5)(1+q^4+q^8)[7]_{q^2}[18]_q.                 \tag{1}
\]

Here [r]_q=1+q+...+q^(r-1),
P_(W(E7))=[2][6][8][10][12][14][18], and
P_(W(D5))=[2][4][5][6][8]. There are 756 nonunit relative factors in total.
The variety has complex dimension 43 and 1512 Schubert classes.
Both images vanish above degree 43. In particular the top relative factor
is 2.

For the normalized Springer morphisms of the minimal 56-dimensional and
adjoint 133-dimensional representations, the
[character-source classification](IRREDUCIBLE_CHARACTER_LATTICES.md) gives

\[
 \mathcal J_{56,J}^d=12^dM_J^d,\qquad
 \mathcal J_{133,J}^d=36^dN_J^d.
\]

Consequently (1) also describes
(\mathcal J_56,J^d+\mathcal J_133,J^d)/\mathcal J_56,J^d.
It supplies the missing root/weight comparison for the Kumar
polynomial-character map beta after (theta_rho^*)^-1 on these flags.
Other irreducible E7 pairs follow from the existing scalar Q/X source
classification and minimum/maximum local valuations. Absolute H/N factors
and arbitrary direct-sum sources are separate questions.

## 2. Common complement and a useful source-completeness criterion

Put K={1,...,6} and V=R alpha1+...+R alpha6. The previously proved lattice
split, whose two basis indices are odd, is

\[
 X_R=V\oplus Rw,\qquad Q_R=V\oplus R(2w),\qquad w=\omega_7.
\]

For A=(Sym_R V)^(W_I), the actual sources are A[w] and A[2w].
Write Omega=C6^-1 alpha for the fundamental weights of this E6 subsystem.
These are 2-local coordinates because det(C6)=3. Set h=Omega1 and

\[
\begin{split}
 u_1&=-\Omega_2+\Omega_3,&u_2&=\Omega_2+\Omega_3-\Omega_4,\\
 u_3&=\Omega_4-\Omega_5,&u_4&=\Omega_5-\Omega_6,&u_5&=\Omega_6,
 \qquad z=\Omega_3.
\end{split}
\]

Then sum(u_i)=2z and (h,z,u1,u2,u3,u4) is an exact integral E6-weight
basis. The subgroup with retained nodes {2,4,5,6} permutes the five u_i
and fixes h,z. Replacing its four variable directions by u_i-u5 has
index 5. Since 5 is a unit in R, centering u_i by 2z/5 and applying the
ordinary symmetric-polynomial theorem identifies its **full** invariant
ring as

\[
 P_R=R[h,z,c_2,c_3,c_4,c_5],\qquad c_j=e_j(u_1,...,u_5).       \tag{2}
\]

In particular, coefficientwise 2-local integrality in (2) is the actual
integrality test for any rational D5 invariant. This avoids treating a
proper rational-generator algebra as the full source.

The following elementary criterion explains the completeness certificate.
If B is a finitely generated graded subalgebra of A, and its reduction
inside P_R/2P_R has the same Hilbert series as A over Q, then B=A.
Indeed A is saturated in P_R: if 2f is invariant then f is invariant.
Thus A_d/2A_d injects into (P_R)_d/2(P_R)_d and has dimension rank_R A_d.
Equality of the two Hilbert series makes B_d surject onto A_d/2A_d.
Nakayama's lemma applied to the finite module A_d/B_d gives equality in
each degree. This is a general source-completeness test, not a new
invariant-theoretic theorem.

## 3. The full integral D5 source

Set x_i=2u_i-h. The D5 Weyl group acts by even signed permutations of
the five x_i and fixes h. Over Q its invariant algebra is polynomial on

\[
 h,\quad e_1(x^2),\quad e_2(x^2),\quad x_1...x_5,
 \quad e_3(x^2),\quad e_4(x^2),                         \tag{3}
\]

with degrees 1,2,4,5,6,8. Formula (3) is used only over Q.
The full R-algebra A is instead generated by ten coefficient-integral
polynomials in (2), with degrees

\[
                 1,2,4,5,6,8,8,9,12,16.                \tag{4}
\]

Their exact polynomials, rational identities in (3), and reduction
relations are recorded in the
[source presentation](experiments/e7-d5-source-algebra.json) and
[image certificate archive](experiments/e7-d5-images-certificates.json.gz).
For example its degree-two generator is h*z-2*z^2+c2, and its degree-four
generator is

\[
 -h^3z+h^2z^2+4hz^3-4z^4-4hzc_2+4z^2c_2
       +3hc_3-4zc_3+2c_4.
\]

The construction uses exact coefficient lattices: generate the rational
invariant component, halve every integral relation among coefficient
columns modulo 2, and repeat until the columns are independent modulo 2.
This gives the full component, since a primitive lattice in a rational
subspace is its full intersection with the ambient coefficient lattice.
The extra columns needed by the smaller generator algebra supply (4).
The recorded bounded component construction is discovery and additional
verification; the following Hilbert certificate proves completeness in
**all** degrees.

Reduce the ten polynomials modulo 2. Eliminate their three remaining
coefficient variables after solving for c2 and c5 and replacing c3 by
b+h^2z+hz^2. A 40-polynomial graph Groebner basis has 20 elimination
relations on the ten generators. Its Hilbert numerator is exactly

\[
 (1-q^8)(1-q^9)(1-q^{12})(1-q^{16}).
\]

Dividing by the product of (1-q^d) for the ten degrees (4) gives

\[
 H(q)=\frac1{(1-q)(1-q^2)(1-q^4)(1-q^5)(1-q^6)(1-q^8)}.
\]

This is the characteristic-zero D5 invariant Hilbert series. The criterion
in Section 2 proves that (4) generates A in all degrees. The ten-generator
algebra is not asserted polynomial. In particular this argument does not
use a nonexistent polynomial source basis at the torsion prime.

This distinction is visible already in degree four of the Cayley-plane
fiber. The degree-two generator restricts to the square of its divisor,
and the displayed degree-four generator restricts to 2h^4-6s, where s is
the degree-four Schubert generator used in the
[E6 image calculation](E6_ROOT_WEIGHT_COMPARISON.md#4-cayley-plane-images-and-their-exact-local-bases).
Thus the full invariant-polynomial image in that fiber degree is
R h^4 + R(2s), rather than the full R h^4 + R s. A common primitive
polynomial fiber basis cannot justify the D5 transfer at 2.

The [independent source audit](experiments/e7-d5-source-audit.json)
checks the actual lattice indices, all ten rational invariant identities,
integer coefficients, both containments of the graph ideal, Buchberger's
criterion and the monomial-ideal Hilbert numerator using ordinary Python.
SymPy only parses polynomials and handles the small lattice matrices.
The invariant and GF(2) arithmetic is separately implemented.

## 4. Complete rational Borel quotient

The common quadratic relation is

\[
 w^2=B,\qquad B=-\frac38(e_1(x^2)+3h^2)
              =-\frac32\alpha^t C_6^{-1}\alpha.
\]

Although the expression in x has denominator 8, B is coefficient-integral
in V: if g2=h*z-2*z^2+c2 is the degree-two source generator, then
B=3(g2-h^2). In particular it belongs to A. Eliminate e1 using this monic w relation. The
rational source becomes Q[h,w,v4,v5,v6,v8], of weights 1,1,4,5,6,8, where
v4=e2(x^2), v5=product(x), v6=e3(x^2), v8=e4(x^2).

The 27 E6 minimal weights in these coordinates are

\[
 h;\quad -h/2\pm x_i/2;\quad
 (h+\sum_i\epsilon_i x_i)/4\quad(\prod_i\epsilon_i=1).
\]

The 56 E7 minimal weights are +/-w and +/-(lambda-w/3) for these 27 lambda.
Their even trace moments in degrees 6,8,10,12,14,18, after the quadratic
reduction, generate the rational Borel ideal. A weighted Groebner basis
has 298 polynomials and 1512 standard monomials, with highest degree 43.

The [independent moment audit](experiments/e7-d5-moments-audit.json)
reconstructs both weight sets from fresh Cartan reflections and proves
all six polynomial identities by exact rational evaluation on unisolvent
sets. The evaluation matrices have full rank over F_101, certifying
unisolvence over Q; this is not approximate sampling. A nonzero exact
seven-by-seven Jacobian of the seven E7 moments proves their algebraic
independence. Their degrees are the basic E7 degrees, so comparison with
the known invariant Hilbert series proves they generate the full rational
W-invariant algebra. Rational Borel theory then gives quotient dimension
|W(E7)|/|W(D5)|=1512. The independent Groebner/dimension checks therefore
identify the entire rational kernel, not just a selected set of relations.

## 5. Complete integral images and formula (1)

Use the ten common full-source generators (4), together with w for N and
2w for M. In every degree 0..43 the
[image calculation](experiments/e7-d5-images.json) supplies rational
coordinate matrices H_N,H_M relative to the standard monomials above.
They are proved to be the complete local images by induction: H_0=(1),
and for every generator g of degree k,

- all columns of H_d^-1*(g H_(d-k)) are in R;
- their combined reduction modulo 2 has full row rank.

These two statements give equality of the proposed and generated image
lattices by Nakayama. The induction covers all degrees because the
rational cohomology is zero above 43. Modular Hermite reduction is only a
way to find the matrices; exact containment and primitive generation
certify them afterwards. No precision assumption supplies a proof step.

The common quadratic gives beta(w)^2=beta(B), with B in A. Hence
N=M+beta(w)M and 2N is contained in M, as already proved for all P7
refinements. Therefore the Smith valuations of H_N^-1 H_M are 0 or 1,
and m_d is its size minus its rank modulo 2. There is no odd-primary
quotient because X/Q has order 2. The exhaustive calculation gives
exactly the coefficients of the positive product in (1).

The [independent image audit](experiments/e7-d5-images-audit.json)
uses its own rational reducer, matrix inverses and bit ranks, with gmpy2
rather than Sage. It checks the weighted Buchberger criterion, all
standard monomials, every multiplication closure and primitive generation
condition, M contained in N, 2N contained in M, and every multiplicity.
It shares the recorded certificates and the same general algorithms;
it does not import the primary scripts or use Sage/Singular arithmetic.
The separately reconstructed moments and source audit justify its inputs.

A further [direct invariant-kernel calculation](experiments/e7-d5-kernel.json)
in degrees 10..13 gives m=13,16,18,21 after 6,8,10,11 coefficient-halving
rounds respectively. This is a separate bounded corroboration using the
previously checked full-flag moment inputs, not an additional independent
source construction.

Finally E6 opposition acts on K by (1 6)(3 5), fixes w, and preserves both
actual lattices and intertwines the characteristic maps after pullback to G/B.
It exchanges I and I'. Thus the
completed calculation proves (1) for both D5 refinements.

## 6. Attribution, scope and remaining problem

The published inputs are rational Borel and Chevalley invariant theory,
the symmetric-polynomial theorem, integral characteristic maps and the
E6 opposition symmetry. The coefficient and image completeness arguments
use elementary DVR linear algebra and Nakayama. The exact ten-generator
source application and the all-degree relative D5 image comparison are
repository deductions with exhaustive certificates; their literature
priority is unresolved.

Integral spin invariant theory is established literature, not a discovery
of this calculation. [Lyons (1998)](https://doi.org/10.1080/00927879808826272)
proves nonpolynomiality for Spin(n), n>=10, and
[Duan, Sections 7--8](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03799) discusses explicit
integral spin invariants. These are related sources; their Spin(10)
lattice must not be silently substituted for this actual Levi lattice.
The recent [Baek preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18188) studies which
spin invariants come from the Chow ring of BG. Its restriction
CH(BG)->CH(BT)^W is different from the present Borel map out of all Levi
invariants. None of these inspected statements is being credited with
formula (1), and this search does not establish its novelty.

The rational quotient method is the Borel calculation underlying the
Kumar Springer-character maps; see
[Kumar Part I](https://kumar.math.unc.edu/papers/kumar76.pdf) and
[Kumar--Xie Part III](https://kumar.math.unc.edu/papers/kumar86.pdf).
The [Duan--Zhao Cayley-plane presentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0511332)
is background for the tempting E6/D5 fiber route, but the proof above
does not assume a common primitive polynomial basis of that fiber at 2.

The [integer-only completion check](experiments/e7-d5-summary.json)
reconstructs the product in (1), checks all ranks and multiplicities,
compares with both earlier degree-nine tables and the direct kernels,
and verifies the remaining-case inventory.

Together with the earlier 109 cases, this settles 111 proper E7
parabolics and 63 of the 64 P7 refinements. Only retained
I={2,3,4,5} (D4) remains in that refinement family. The full integral
source and image-completeness method here is a candidate route for D4;
no D4 all-degree claim follows from the D5 computation. Runtime/API
version remains 0.20.0.
