# Refinements of E7/P7: multiplicities through degree nine

Status, 20 October 2026: a uniform computer-assisted deduction through
polynomial degree 9 for all 64 retained subsets of the E6 Levi. The
[A6 intermediary theorem](E7_FULL_FLAG_COMPARISON.md) now proves the
full-flag polynomial and all-degree factors on 61 of these refinements.
The [D5 completion](E7_D5_REFINEMENT.md) adds both D5 refinements, making
63/64 settled in all degrees. D4 alone remains. This note originally reduced
the all-degree problem to three base polynomials and exhibits an integral
invariant that obstructs a tempting polynomial-basis proof. No claim of
literature priority is made.

## 1. Exact statement

Use the [P7 note's](E7_ROOT_WEIGHT_COMPARISON.md) notation: G is simply
connected E7, X and Q are its weight and root lattices, K={1,2,3,4,5,6},
and I is any subset of K. For R=Z_(2), define the full unscaled images

\[
 M_I^d=\beta^{P_I}((\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathbb Z}^d Q)^{W_I}),\qquad
 N_I^d=\beta^{P_I}((\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathbb Z}^d X)^{W_I}).
\]

The previously proved common complement gives 2N_I contained in M_I.
Write N_I^d/M_I^d=(Z/2)^(m_(I,d)). These are relative image factors,
not the ambient cohomology cokernels. Degree d means cohomological
degree 2d. Let d_1,...,d_r be the fundamental degrees of the rank-r
reflection group W_I, including all its components, and put

\[
 H_I(q)=\frac1{(1-q)^{6-r}\prod_{j=1}^r(1-q^{d_j})}.
\]

**Theorem.** For every I contained in K and every 0<=d<=9,

\[
 \boxed{m_{I,d}=[q^{d-1}] (1-q^6)(1-q^8)H_I(q).}
 \tag{1}
\]

Coefficients of negative powers are zero. Equation (1) is asserted only
through degree nine; it is not an all-degree generating function.
For the empty retained set (the full flag), its coefficients are

\[
 (m_0,\ldots,m_9)=(0,1,6,21,56,126,252,461,786,1265).
\]

For I={1,3,4,5,6}, of type A5, they are

\[
 (0,1,1,2,3,5,7,10,13,17).
\]

For the normalized minimal and adjoint Springer maps, the literal-character
images are J56,I^d=12^d M_I^d and J133,I^d=36^d N_I^d. The previously
proved comparison identifies (J56+J133)/J56 with N_I/M_I degree by degree.
Thus (1) gives these Springer/Borel image-sum factors in the stated range.

The [independent table](experiments/e7-refinement-independent.json)
records all 64 cases. Full P7 multiplicities, in every degree, remain
those already proved in the P7 note.

## 2. The quadratic and the first two higher relations

Put V=R alpha1+...+R alpha6 and w=omega7. The actual lattices split as
X_R=V plus Rw and Q_R=V plus R(2w). Every W_I preserves V and fixes w.
If A_I=(Sym_R V)^(W_I), the two full sources are A_I[w] and A_I[2w].
No averaging by the order of W_I is involved.

Let C6 be the E6 Cartan matrix and a=(alpha1,...,alpha6)^t. The common
root polynomial

\[
 B=-\frac32 a^tC_6^{-1}a=-\frac{\psi_2}{144}
\]

is integral and has Borel image h^2, where h=beta(w). The monic relation
q2=w^2-B permits unique reduction into

\[
 \mathcal D=\operatorname{Sym}_R(V)[w]/(w^2-B)
           =\operatorname{Sym}_R(V)\oplus w\operatorname{Sym}_R(V).
 \tag{2}
\]

Its invariant part is exactly A_I plus w A_I. Before imposing the
higher relations, the root image corresponds to A_I plus 2w A_I.

The rational E7 invariant degrees are 2,6,8,10,12,14,18. Consequently,
below degree ten the remaining rational characteristic kernel is
generated by a sextic F6 and an octic F8 in D. Primitive integral
representatives can be obtained by reducing the sixth and eighth power
sums of the 56 minuscule weights modulo q2, then dividing coefficient
contents. The [primary certificate](experiments/e7-refinement-relations.json)
records all coefficients. They have the form

\[
 F_6=A_6+wC_5,\qquad F_8=A_8+wC_7,
 \quad C_5\in4\operatorname{Sym}_R^5(V),\quad
 C_7\in2\operatorname{Sym}_R^7(V),
 \tag{3}
\]

with A6 primitive. In the printed Kumar--Xie normalization one may take,
up to the sign of the primary sextic,

\[
 F_6=\frac{\psi_6+117504B^3-12w\psi_5}{3456}.
 \tag{4}
\]

The independent octic construction starts with
psi8+1568wB psi5-9796608B^4. After primitive coefficient normalization
and a possible integral half-sum with BF6, its span with BF6 agrees
with the primary octic span by a matrix invertible over R. The
[independent certificate](experiments/e7-refinement-independent.json)
records this exact transition.

For completeness, integrality of individual relations is insufficient:
their multiples must span the full integral kernel. In degrees 6,7,8,9,
the columns F6 times the monomial basis of D_(d-6), together with F8
times the basis of D_(d-8), have respective counts 1,7,28,84. Both
implementations find exactly those column ranks modulo 2. Thus each
span is primitive in the corresponding free R-module D_d. Rational
Borel identifies its rational span with the whole kernel. Primitivity
therefore proves that it is the whole integral kernel in these degrees.
Below degree six there are no further relations. Torsion-free target
cohomology identifies the integral kernel with this rational-kernel
intersection, even though the image need not be saturated in cohomology.

This is an exact finite coefficient certificate for a uniform theorem;
it is not an assumption that the rational invariant ideal remains
saturated at 2 in all degrees.

## 3. Taking invariants and counting the factors

The relation-multiple expressions above are unique through degree nine:
the same mod-2 ranks imply independence over R and over Q. Since F6
and F8 are W_I-invariant, uniqueness shows that the coefficients in an
invariant relation must themselves be W_I-invariant. This avoids the
failure of exactness of an averaging operator at 2.

Reduction of (3) modulo the root submodule A_I plus 2w A_I kills the
even-w components. The odd components of relation multiples are
precisely

\[
 \overline A_6\,(A_I^{d-7}/2A_I^{d-7})
 \; +\;
 \overline A_8\,(A_I^{d-9}/2A_I^{d-9})
 \quad\subseteq A_I^{d-1}/2A_I^{d-1}.
 \tag{5}
\]

Their sum is direct and both multiplication maps are injective in this
degree range, by the same primitive-column checks. The inclusion
A_I/2A_I into Sym_(F2)(V/2V) is injective: division of an invariant
polynomial by 2, when its coefficients permit it, preserves invariance.
Thus its dimensions are the R-ranks of A_I, and (5) gives

\[
 m_{I,d}=\operatorname{rank}_R A_I^{d-1}
       -\operatorname{rank}_R A_I^{d-7}
       -\operatorname{rank}_R A_I^{d-9},\qquad d\le9.
\]

Classical rational reflection invariant theory gives H_I as the Hilbert
series of A_I. The cross term q^14 cannot contribute in this range, so
this is (1). The quotient is 2-primary and killed by 2, hence the local
calculation recovers the original integral finite group.

## 4. Reduction of the remaining all-degree problem

For the 60 retained subsets whose induced diagrams are products of type
A, the usual common-fiber-basis argument is valid at 2. Indeed det(C6)=3
is a unit, so the common root lattice restricts onto the derived Levi
weight lattice at 2; its type A flag has torsion index one. The same
integral polynomial fiber lifts therefore give, for the full-flag
multiplicity polynomial T_empty and the Levi Weyl length polynomial,

\[
 T_{\varnothing}(q)=P_{W_I}(q)T_I(q).
 \tag{6}
\]

This is the classical characteristic-map/Leray--Hirsch consequence
spelled out in [the E6 proof, Section 6](E6_ROOT_WEIGHT_COMPARISON.md#6-good-levi-deconvolution-60-proper-parabolics).
It does not compute T_empty from the P7 answer.

The four other subsets are {2,3,4,5} (D4), {1,2,3,4,5} and
{2,3,4,5,6} (D5), and K (E6). The E6 case is already solved.
The longest E6 Weyl element sends alpha_i to -alpha_tau(i), where
tau=(1 6)(3 5), and fixes w. It preserves X and Q and intertwines the
characteristic maps. Hence the two D5 polynomials agree. The independent
Cartan calculation verifies both lattice matrices are unimodular and
preserve the full E7 quadratic form.

The [A6 intermediary theorem](E7_FULL_FLAG_COMPARISON.md) now determines
T_empty in all degrees. Equation (6) therefore settles all 60 type A
refinements, and the known P7 answer makes 61. The subsequent
[D5 completion](E7_D5_REFINEMENT.md) adds the two D5 refinements and
leaves only D4 as an all-degree target in this family.
This reduction is a deduction from known methods, not a new general
Leray--Hirsch theorem.

## 5. A concrete obstruction to a polynomial source-basis shortcut

Let psi_d be the 27-form E6 power sums used in the P7 catalogue. Define

\[
\begin{split}
 g_2&=\psi_2/144,\quad g_5=\psi_5/5760,\quad
 g_6=\psi_6/3456,\\
 g_8&=(\psi_8/9216+g_2^4)/2,\quad
 g_9=\psi_9/387072,\quad
 g_{12}=(\psi_{12}/663552+g_6^2)/2.
\end{split}
\]

All are integral root polynomials. In each of the six displayed degrees,
their weighted monomials span the entire invariant component over R:
their coefficient columns are integral, have full mod-2 rank, and span
the known rational invariant component. This last primitivity step is
essential; merely choosing primitive individual generators would not
justify a claim about all choices of six generators.

At a=(0,0,0,0,0,1), their values are respectively
(2,-4,88,954,-340,55780), all even. But if ell_1,...,ell_27 are the
catalogue's 27 linear forms divided by 2, each ell_j is an integer root
polynomial, and E6 permutes their set. Hence

\[
 c_{16}=e_{16}(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_{27})
\]

is an integral invariant. Exactly 16 of the ell_j have odd value at the
displayed point, so c16 has odd value there (in fact -696735). It cannot
belong to R[g2,g5,g6,g8,g9,g12].

In particular the full invariant algebra is not a polynomial R-algebra
on six homogeneous generators: after rationalization their degrees would
have to be 2,5,6,8,9,12, and component completeness forces any such choice
to generate the same displayed subalgebra, contradicting c16. This
obstructs the proposed six-generator polynomial-basis proof. It does
**not** disprove a multiplicity transfer formula for Borel images, since
the additional invariant could contribute compatibly to both images.
The [primary obstruction certificate](experiments/e7-refinement-invariant-obstruction.json)
and [plain-integer audit](experiments/e7-refinement-invariant-obstruction-audit.json)
verify the source completeness, six reflection permutations and witness.

## 6. Independent checks, published inputs and limits

The 56-weight orbit/Sage implementation and the 27-form/SymPy audit use
different relation constructions. Both certify the primitive kernel
columns through degree nine. The second implementation uses its own
sparse reduction and bit elimination. The invariant-ring obstruction is
also checked by plain Python integer multinomial expansions and sparse
convolution, with no Sage, SymPy or project imports.

A third route computes the A5 refinement directly from the full sources:
its six root permutation coordinates v_j and fixed u form a unimodular
root basis, while the same v_j and z=(u-sum(v_j))/2 form a unimodular
weight basis. Symmetric polynomials in the v_j and u or z give the entire
invariant rings. Their basis determinants were also checked in the
[earlier structural audit](experiments/e7-two-primary-ring-check.json). Fresh BGG, Hermite and Smith calculations check their
images in degrees 0..9; they do not obtain image columns from (1).
See [the report](experiments/e7-refinement-bgg-check.json).

Published inputs are the rational Borel kernel and reflection degrees,
the Kumar--Xie rational psi definitions/images, torsion-index facts and
the classical common-fiber-basis argument. Related integral presentations
are in [Kaji--Nakagawa, Theorems 2.2 and 3.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.3702),
which explicitly recalls Borel's rational theorem and attributes the
integral E7 flag presentation to Nakagawa. Their Chow ring of the group,
a quotient by the ideal of divisors, is a different object from N/M.
See also [Totaro's torsion-index paper](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~totaro/papers/public_html/torsione8.pdf).

The repository deductions are (1), the three-base reduction, and the
explicit obstruction certificate. The obstruction is not claimed to be
new invariant theory. This turn checked these primary sources and a
related [Baek--Neher--Zainoulline paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.4332);
none of these limited checks establishes novelty of the exact statements.
The bounded-degree argument here is separate from the subsequent
[A6 intermediary theorem](E7_FULL_FLAG_COMPARISON.md), which completes
the full flag and 61 of the 64 refinements in all degrees.
