# Complete E6/P1 and E7/P7 integral image checks

All degrees of both spaces have now been calculated for the original
catalogue generators and for their individually primitive images. Every
source monomial agrees between fixed-point interpolation and an ordinary
cohomology calculation using published integral presentations and Chevalley
multiplication. The [complete tables](EXCEPTIONAL_MINUSCULE_TABLES.md) retain
both normalizations; the [dataset](experiments/exceptional-minuscule-results.json)
also retains every matrix and its source monomials.

The primitive-generator image subalgebras have the following descriptions:

\[
J^{\mathrm{prim}}_{E_6/P_1}=\mathbb Z[h,16s],\qquad
J^{\mathrm{prim}}_{E_7/P_7}=\mathbb Z[h,81s,19683t].
\]

These denote subalgebras of the integral cohomology rings below, with their
inherited relations. They are not free polynomial rings. The integer changes
of generators proving these equalities are displayed below and checked by
[an exact verifier](experiments/exceptional_minuscule_structure.py).
Consequently the primitive-image saturation quotients are 2-primary and
3-primary, respectively. Individual primitivity does not imply that the
whole generated algebra is saturated.

## Map, source forms and connection to Part III

Let G be the simply connected complex group of the indicated type, T a
maximal torus, and P_i the standard maximal parabolic obtained by omitting
node i in the repository's Bourbaki numbering. Write beta for the Borel
characteristic map from W(P_i)-invariant rational polynomials on the Cartan
to H*(G/P_i,Q). Polynomial degree d maps to H^(2d).

The inputs are the corrected Levi-invariant Springer/Borel polynomials in
the [exceptional catalogue](EXCEPTIONAL.md), based on the E6 and E7 formulas
in [Kumar--Xie, Part III, Theorems 7.2 and 8.2](https://kumar.math.unc.edu/papers/kumar86.pdf).
The E6 normalization and the E7 unscaled journal power sums are preserved.
We calculate the Z-subalgebra generated by their Borel images. This extends
the published complex generator calculations to products in every degree
and to explicit integral sublattices; it does not change the Springer map.

For each input g, write beta(g)=c_g times a primitive integral vector in
the Schubert basis. Every original input here already has integral image,
so image-denominator clearing has multiplier 1. The second source form
replaces g by g/c_g. The contents are:

| Space | Generator contents c_g |
| --- | --- |
| E6/P1 | y1: 3; e1: 3; e2: 6; e3: 6; e4: 3; e5: 9; euler: 3 |
| E7/P7 | psi2: 144; psi5: 11520; psi6: 6912; psi8: 9216; psi9: 774144; psi12: 1327104; omega7: 1 |

These are two explicitly chosen source Z-forms. Neither is asserted to be
the intersection of the Springer polynomial subring with an integral
representation ring. A different source lattice can give different indices.

For each d, the columns are the Schubert expansions of all weighted-degree-d
monomials in the seven inputs. Smith factors a_1 | ... | a_r describe
H^(2d)(G/P_i,Z)/J_d as the direct sum of Z/a_j. Every recorded image has
full rational rank, and factors 1 are retained. Degrees above the complex
dimension have zero target. The original and primitive top-degree factors
are 54 and 2 for E6/P1, and 6 and 3 for E7/P7.

## Published integral rings and notation

Write sigma_[i1,...,il] for the Schubert class indexed by the displayed
reduced word. In E6/P1 set h=sigma_[1] and s=sigma_[2,4,3,1], of polynomial
degrees 1 and 4. The integral presentation is

\[
H^*(E_6/P_1,\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z[h,s]/
(2h^9+3hs^2-6h^5s,\ s^3-6h^4s^2+h^{12}).
\]

This is [Duan--Zhao, Theorem 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0511332),
transported from P6 by the diagram involution 1<->6, 3<->5, with 2 and 4
fixed. The space has complex dimension 16 and 27 Schubert classes.

For E7/P7 take h=sigma_[7], s=sigma_[2,4,5,6,7], and
t=sigma_[1,5,4,2,3,4,5,6,7], of degrees 1, 5 and 9. By
[Duan--Zhao, Theorem 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0511332),

\[
H^*(E_7/P_7,\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z[h,s,t]/
(s^2-2ht,\ 2st-9h^4s^2+6h^9s-h^{14},\
t^2+10h^3s^3-9h^8s^2+2h^{13}s).
\]

This space has complex dimension 27 and 56 Schubert classes. These integral
presentations, their Schubert generators, and the Schubert bases are
published input, not results claimed here.

## Integer changes of generators

In the following identities a barred catalogue name means its Borel image
divided by the content in the table above.

For E6 put H=h and S=16s. Direct substitution in the published ring gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\bar y_1&=H,&\bar e_1&=-H^2,\\
\bar e_2&=7H^4-S,&\bar e_3&=47H^6-7H^2S,\\
\bar e_4&=-457H^8+74H^4S-S^2,&
\bar e_5&=H^2(7H^4-S)^2,\\
\overline{\mathrm{euler}}&=-7H^5+HS.
\end{aligned}
\]

Conversely H=bar y1 and S=7(bar y1)^4-bar e2. Both inclusions are integer
polynomial identities, proving the E6 subalgebra equality.

For E7 put H=h, S=81s and T=19683t. Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
\bar\omega_7&=H,&\bar\psi_2&=-H^2,\\
\bar\psi_5&=35H^5-S,&\bar\psi_6&=683H^6-20HS,\\
\bar\psi_8&=-67537H^8+1960H^3S,\\
\bar\psi_9&=-1666H^9-3H^4S+T,\\
\bar\psi_{12}&=2311007H^{12}-222640H^7S
                   +9020H^3T-9020H^2S^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

Conversely H=bar omega7, S=35H^5-bar psi5, and
T=bar psi9+1666H^9+3H^4S. In particular the psi12 identity involves no
division: s^2=2ht implies H^3T-H^2S^2=3^8 h^3t.
This proves the E7 subalgebra equality over Z.

After inverting 2 in E6, or 3 in E7, the displayed scaled Schubert generators
generate the entire published ring. Since each cohomology group is a finite
free abelian group, its quotient by J^prim is finite and has only the
indicated prime as a possible divisor. The complete Smith lists further
determine the multiplicities and exponents; their largest individual
factors are 256 and 19683.

The [structure report](experiments/exceptional-minuscule-structure.json)
checks all 19 forward and reverse integer identities. Its rational
Groebner reductions certify equality in integral cohomology because that
cohomology is torsion-free. This argument would not be valid for an
arbitrary ring with additive torsion.

## Independent calculations and their dependencies

The [fixed-point engine](experiments/exceptional_localization.py) enumerates
the orbit of the omitted fundamental weight, hence just 27 or 56 minimal
coset representatives. It uses the reduced-subword restriction formula
explained in [Tymoczko's account of Billey's formula](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.0254).
It evaluates the exact triangular restriction matrix at a regular dominant
rational point and solves for the equivariant expansion. For a homogeneous
input of degree d, the coefficients on Schubert classes of length d have
degree zero in the torus parameters; they are precisely the ordinary
coefficients. Coefficients of shorter classes are discarded, and those of
longer classes are checked to vanish. A second regular point checks
specialization consistency; it is not a separate algorithm.

The [ordinary-cohomology engine](experiments/exceptional_published_rings.py)
uses the published presentations above and root/coroot Chevalley
multiplication by h. It propagates the anchors 1,s,s^2 for E6 and 1,s,t
for E7, and verifies that they span every degree and have the same linear
relations as their ring normal forms. The original BGG implementation
supplies the 14 starting catalogue images and one E6 s^2 anchor. The latter
is calculated from a polynomial lift of s. All subsequent products use
ordinary Chevalley columns and the published relations, independently of
fixed-point interpolation. The localization calculation checks those
starting generator images too. Both engines share root data and the
specified catalogue inputs, not their product matrices.

Rational normal forms are only intermediate coordinates. Every resulting
matrix is converted back to the actual integral Schubert basis before
Smith calculation. The Smith factors are checked a second way: Hermite
reduction gives a lattice basis, and gcds of all minors give the
determinantal divisors. The largest such basis matrix is only 3 by 3.

The complete run checks:

- 14 catalogue inputs reconstructed from the frozen audit fixture;
- 2372 unscaled source-monomial columns by both engines;
- 4744 columns across the two forms, using exact column rescaling and
  checking integrality and equality of both scaled matrices;
- 90 degree/form rows, including every degree through the top;
- all 1650 previous G2/F4 columns against the new localization engine.

All seven inputs are retained in each space. The computation avoids large
products in the fundamental-weight polynomial ring and never enumerates
the full E7 Weyl group. The dataset records elapsed time and normalized
source hashes. These are exact finite checks and displayed algebraic
deductions; no claim of novelty or classification of other E-parabolics is
made. See [proof status](PROOF_STATUS.md) for the broader distinction.

## API and reproduction

The existing public API handles individual degrees and explicit scales:

```python
from sage.all import QQ
from sage_borel import exceptional_borel
b = exceptional_borel("E6", 1)
original = b.generator_algebra()
contents = dict(y1=3, e1=3, e2=6, e3=6, e4=3, e5=9, euler=3)
primitive = b.generator_algebra(scales={n: QQ(1)/c for n,c in contents.items()})
assert original.integral_degree(4).smith_factors == (3, 288)
assert primitive.integral_degree(4).smith_factors == (1, 16)
```

For the complete high-degree calculation use the experimental backend:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src sage -python experiments/exceptional_minuscule_images.py
PYTHONPATH=src sage -python experiments/exceptional_minuscule_structure.py
python tools/render_exceptional_minuscule_tables.py
```

Run these commands in `borel/`. The fixed-point helper is currently an
experimental verifier, not a public equivariant-cohomology API.
