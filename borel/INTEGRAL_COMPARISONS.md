# Comparing and combining integral images

Version 0.9 compares the actual embedded lattices produced by selected
Springer/Borel inputs. Equal Smith lists alone do not imply equal images.
The new operations apply to any integral degree report on the same flag,
including exceptional groups; they do not require a general Smith formula.

## Mathematical objects

Fix a standard parabolic P of G and the integral Schubert basis of
H^(2d)(G/P; Z). For two lists of homogeneous Levi-invariant polynomials f
and g with integral Borel images, set

$$
L_d=\beta(\mathbb Z[f])_d,\qquad
M_d=\beta(\mathbb Z[g])_d,\qquad
J_d=\beta(\mathbb Z[f,g])_d.
$$

The degree is polynomial degree, half the cohomological degree. The
comparison returns L_d + M_d and L_d intersect M_d, with their embedded
integer bases, Smith factors and saturation indices. It tests both
containments and equality. All lattices retain their flag and degree.
Inputs with different flag contexts or degrees raise an error.

In general J_d contains more than L_d + M_d: it also includes products
involving both sets of generators. The joint-image experiment below
measures J_d / (L_d + M_d) separately. This quotient measures the contribution
of mixed products. Its torsion is distinct from the saturation quotient
of J_d in integral cohomology.

For K contained in L, `L.quotient_by(K)` returns the exact abelian group

$$
L/K\simeq\mathbb Z^r\oplus\bigoplus_i\mathbb Z/s_i\mathbb Z,
\quad s_i>1.
$$

`index` is the finite order when r=0 and `None` otherwise. The zero group
has index 1. Noncontainment raises `ValueError`; it is never reported as
a finite index. The saturation of L means (L tensor Q) intersect
H^(2d)(G/P; Z), so its index does not assert full ambient rank.

## API

```python
from sage_borel import BorelMap, GeneratorAlgebra, compare_integral_images

b = BorelMap("A2")
x, y = b.weights
left = GeneratorAlgebra(b, {"u": 2*x, "v": y}).integral_degree(1)
right = GeneratorAlgebra(b, {"u": x, "v": 2*y}).integral_degree(1)
comparison = compare_integral_images(left, right)  # also left.compare(right)
assert left.smith_factors == right.smith_factors == (1, 2)
assert not comparison.equal
assert not comparison.left_in_right and not comparison.right_in_left
assert comparison.same_rational_span
assert comparison.sum.smith_factors == (1, 1)
assert comparison.intersection.smith_factors == (2, 2)
quotient = comparison.sum.quotient_by(left)
assert quotient.free_rank == 0 and quotient.torsion_factors == (2,)
assert quotient.index == 2
```

| Entry | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `report.lattice()` | `IntegralImageLattice`, reusing the report's immutable bases |
| `report.compare(other)` or `lattice.compare(other)` | `IntegralImageComparison`; accepts a report or lattice |
| `comparison.left`, `.right`, `.sum`, `.intersection` | Composable embedded lattices |
| `comparison.left_in_right`, `.right_in_left`, `.equal` | Integral containment and equality |
| `comparison.same_rational_span` | Equality of spans over Q |
| `lattice.rank`, `.smith_factors`, `.saturation_index`, `.is_saturated` | Exact invariants in integral Schubert coordinates |
| `lattice.basis`, `.saturation_basis`, `.schubert_indices` | Immutable basis columns and their shared row labels |
| `lattice.contains(expansion)` | Integral membership of a homogeneous Schubert expansion |
| `lattice.quotient_by(smaller)` | `IntegralLatticeQuotient` with free rank, nonunit torsion factors and finite index or `None` |
| `.as_dict()` | JSON-safe output on lattices, comparisons and quotients |

Use `compare(...).equal` to compare lattices. A combined lattice has no
preferred source polynomial ring: polynomial preimages remain available
on the original `integral_degree` reports. For a class in an intersection,
each original report can independently produce its integer preimage.
No implicit scaling of generators or clearing of denominators occurs.

## Why the operations are integral

Align the two supports in the same Schubert basis and let A and B be
integer basis matrices with independent columns. The sum is the column
lattice of [A B]. Smith transformations UCV=D, with U and V unimodular,
give a basis for its image as well as its saturation.

For the intersection, apply Smith transformations to C=[A -B]. If its
rank is r, the last columns of V are a basis of ker(C: Z^(p+q) -> Z^m).
Indeed C v=0 is equivalent to D V^(-1) v=0, and unimodularity identifies
integer solutions in both coordinate systems. Write this kernel matrix
as (X,Y) in the two sets of column coordinates. Then A X = B Y generates
exactly the intersection. This uses the full integer kernel, which need
not be generated by independently clearing denominators in a rational
kernel basis.

Containment K contained in L is equivalent to every column of K having
integer coordinates in a basis of L. Smith factors of that coordinate
matrix give L/K, including its free part. Missing Schubert coordinates
are zero. Computing the unused ambient Schubert basis is unnecessary.

These are standard integer linear algebra constructions. Their addition
is a computational extension of the library, not a new mathematical
classification. The algorithms still require the existing Schubert
image matrices; they do not inherit the matrix-free rank scalability of
[orthogonal_smith](ORTHOGONAL_API.md).

## Two-step joint calculation

```python
from sage_borel import two_step_symplectic

flag = two_step_symplectic(n=3, b=1, k=1)
d = 3  # H^6
middle = flag.middle_algebra().integral_degree(d)
tail = flag.tail_algebra().integral_degree(d)
joint = flag.joint_algebra().integral_degree(d)
separate = middle.compare(tail)
assert separate.sum.rank == 1 and joint.rank == 2
assert separate.sum.compare(joint).left_in_right
new_classes = joint.lattice().quotient_by(separate.sum)
assert new_classes.free_rank == 1 and new_classes.torsion_factors == ()
assert joint.is_saturated

# The additional class is a mixed product of degrees 1 and 2.
c1 = flag.middle_generators()["c1"]
e1 = flag.tail_generators()["e1"]
mixed = flag.expand(c1*e1)
assert joint.contains(mixed) and not separate.sum.contains(mixed)
u, v = flag.joint_algebra().domain.gens()
assert joint.preimage(mixed) == u*v
```
