# Integral joint images on orthogonal two-step flags

Status: repository deduction from classical invariant and Schubert theory,
2026-10-19. The argument below is integral where required and includes a
dependency audit. It makes no originality claim and has not received external
peer review. The [finite verifier](experiments/orthogonal_joint_structure.py)
corroborates the argument; it is not its arbitrary-rank justification.

## 1. Statement and the difference from type C

Let a,b>=1, n=a+b+k. In type B let k>=1 and

$$X_B=\operatorname{SO}(2n+1)/(U(a)\times U(b)\times\operatorname{SO}(2k+1)).$$

In type D let k>=2 and

$$X_D=\operatorname{SO}(2n)/(U(a)\times U(b)\times\operatorname{SO}(2k)).$$

These compact quotients realize complex isotropic flags of dimensions a,a+b.
Split the Cartesian coordinates into blocks A,M,T of sizes a,b,k. Use the
fixed normalized inputs c_j=e_j(M) and q_i=e_i(T^2). In B include all q_i;
in D include q_1,...,q_(k-1) and p=product(T), with q_k=p^2. Set

$$L=\beta(\mathbb Z[c]),\qquad M_0=\beta(\mathbb Z[q])\quad\text{in B},
\qquad M_0=\beta(\mathbb Z[q_1,\ldots,q_{k-1},p])\quad\text{in D},$$

and let J be the subalgebra generated by L and M_0. All cohomology is
integral, and polynomial degree d denotes H^(2d).

**Theorem.** In both families and every degree:

1. L+M_0 is primitive in J: J/(L+M_0) is free abelian.
2. L intersect M_0=Z, with zero intersection in positive degree.
3. J is saturated after inverting 2. Thus every nonzero ambient Smith
   factor of J is a power of 2, including 1.

Unlike type C, J need not be primitive in ambient integral cohomology.
For B3 with (a,b,k)=(1,1,1), its degree-three factors are (1,2).
For D4 with (1,1,2), its degree-four factors are (1,1,2,2,2).
These [existing exact examples](TWO_STEP_INTEGRAL.md) rule out a verbatim
orthogonal extension of type C joint saturation. They also rule out ambient
primitivity of the separate-image sum in general: in the B3 example at
degree three, the sum has Smith list (2), as recorded in the original table.

The theorem gives explicit integral image presentations and quotient ranks
below. It does not give the multiplicities of all powers of 2 in the
ambient joint-image Smith lists. The earlier single-tail B/D formulas
do not automatically supply those multiplicities.

## 2. Type B transfers the integral source algebra from type C

The rational Weyl-invariant ideals in types B and C are identical in the
same Cartesian variables: they are generated by the positive elementary
functions in x_1^2,...,x_n^2. Consequently the restrictions of the rational
Borel maps to the same joint polynomial source have identical kernels.

Both integral cohomology groups are torsion-free. For a polynomial with
integer coefficients, vanishing is therefore equivalent to vanishing after
tensoring with Q. The integral kernels are equal as well. Thus there is a
generator-preserving integral algebra isomorphism

$$J_B\cong J_C,$$

which also identifies their middle and tail subalgebras. The
[type C theorem](TYPE_C_JOINT_PRIMITIVITY.md) immediately transfers the
primitive-sum and constant-intersection assertions. Its common basis
is a basis of the abstract B image, though it need not be primitive in
the ambient B Schubert lattice.

In particular, with Z=M^2 union T^2,

$$J_B\cong\mathbb Z[c_1,\ldots,c_b,q_1,\ldots,q_k]/
                    (h_{a+1}(Z),\ldots,h_n(Z)).\tag{1}$$

The isomorphism is not an identification of the ambient integral B and C
Schubert lattices. It supplies no equality of their Smith lists.

## 3. A shared auxiliary flag ring

Put

$$R=\mathbb Z[u_1,\ldots,u_a,v_1,\ldots,v_b,w_1,\ldots,w_k]/
             (\text{positive coefficients of }U(z)V(z)W(z)-1),\tag{2}$$

where U(z)=1+sum u_i z^i, and similarly for V,W. These variables represent
the squared elementary functions of the three blocks. R is the integral
quaternionic partial-flag ring; it also identifies with a complex
partial-flag cohomology ring with degrees doubled. Write
R_M=Z[v_1,...,v_b] and R_T=Z[w_1,...,w_k] for the image subrings in R.
Set u=u_a, v=v_b, w=w_k. The top relation gives uvw=0.

We will use four elementary integral properties of (2):

(i) R_M and R_T occupy subsets of a common integral basis, after ordering
the blocks M,A,T. They are the two Grassmannian pullbacks for
Fl(b,n-k;n;C); their permitted right descents are {b} and {n-k}.
Thus R/(R_M+R_T) and R/R_M are free, and R_M intersect R_T=Z.

(ii) By integral Grassmannian Leray–Hirsch, R is free over R_M with a
homogeneous basis including 1 and with fiber rank polynomial
Gaussian(a+k,k). It is free over R_T with such a basis of fiber rank
polynomial Gaussian(a+b,b). These are module decompositions, not
tensor-product ring assertions.

(iii) Whitney inversion gives

$$uw=(-1)^{n-b}h_{n-b}(M^2)\in R_M,\qquad
uv=(-1)^{n-k}h_{n-k}(T^2)\in R_T.\tag{3}$$

Quotienting R_M by its displayed complete function gives the integral
Grassmann ring Gr(b,n-1), with doubled degrees; quotienting R_T gives
Gr(k,n-1). This follows directly from their consecutive complete-function
presentations. By (ii), R/(uw) is free over R_M/(uw), and R/(uv) is free
over R_T/(uv), with 1 among the respective module bases. In particular
both quotients are free abelian and both indicated base subgroups are
primitive.

(iv) R/(u) is the same flag presentation with block sizes (a-1,b,k) and
total size n-1: the last coefficient relation becomes automatic when
u_a=0. It is free abelian. A zero head here simply means omitting that
block, so this also applies when a=1.

These properties concern ordinary integral flag rings. They do not assert
that an orthogonal characteristic subring fills integral cohomology.

## 4. The type D integral presentation

Let D_b=Z[c_1,...,c_b], with v_i=e_i(M^2) expressed by

$$C(z)C(-z)=\sum_{i=0}^b(-1)^iv_i z^{2i}.$$

Put P=R tensor_(Z[v_1,...,v_b]) D_b and s=c_b, so s^2=v. Let

$$\mathcal J=P[p]/(p^2-w,\;u s p).\tag{4}$$

**Claim.** The natural characteristic map identifies this formal ring
integrally with J_D.

First compute the rational kernel. Use the type D head-invariant subring
Q[u_1,...,u_(a-1),r], where r=product(A) and r^2=u. It is a subring of
the ordinary symmetric head polynomials, not an enlargement of that ring.
Averaging over W(D_a) over Q contracts the full invariant ideal to this
intermediate ring. For a=1 this is the trivial group and r is the sole
head coordinate.
The global D Euler relation is r s p=0. One can include all squared
elementary relations in (2), since the last one is the square of the global
Euler relation. The intermediate quotient is therefore

$$P[r,p]/(r^2-u,\ p^2-w,\ r s p).\tag{5}$$

Before imposing r s p, the ring is free on 1,r over P[p]/(p^2-w).
Multiplication of r s p by r gives u s p; hence its even-r intersection
is exactly the ideal (u s p). This proves (4) rationally. The averaging
is used only over Q, not over Z.

To upgrade it integrally, we now prove that (4) is torsion-free, without
assuming the desired image theorem. The integral Q-tilde identities give

$$D_b=\bigoplus_{\lambda\text{ strict},\lambda_1<b}
 \widetilde Q_\lambda(c)\,
 \mathbb Z[v_1,\ldots,v_b,s]/(s^2-v_b).\tag{6}$$

Indeed, the usual free basis over Z[v] is indexed by strict partitions
with parts at most b. Since c_j=0 for j>b, the Pfaffian definition gives
Q-tilde_(b,lambda)=c_b Q-tilde_lambda when lambda has no part b.
Pair-removal gives c_b^2=v_b. This groups the usual basis into pairs;
there is no division by 2.

The quotient (4) thus has the following direct-sum decomposition of graded
abelian groups, with z_lambda=Q-tilde_lambda(c):

$$\boxed{\quad
\mathcal J=\bigoplus_{\lambda\text{ strict},\lambda_1<b}z_\lambda
 \left(R\ \oplus\ s\,R/(uw)\ \oplus\ p\,R/(uv)
                        \oplus\ sp\,R/(u)\right).\quad}\tag{7}$$

Here s,p,sp label components and degree shifts; they do not mean a
polynomial-ring direct product. To verify (7), work first in
R[s,p]/(s^2-v,p^2-w), free on 1,s,p,sp. The relation u s p and its
products by s,p,sp impose respectively u in the sp component, uv in the
p component, uw in the s component, and uvw=0 in the unit component.
These are all multiples because 1,s,p,sp span the extension over R.

Every coefficient ring in (7) is free by Section 3. Thus the proposed
integral source quotient has no torsion. Its relations vanish in actual
cohomology, and its rational characteristic map is injective by (5).
It therefore injects integrally and has image exactly the algebra generated
by c and p,q. This proves the claim. Head variables u_i are eliminated by
Whitney inversion, so (4) introduces no additional image generators.

Equivalently, eliminate u_i from (2), and substitute q_k=p^2. With
Z=M^2 union T^2 interpreted through these elementary functions,

$$\boxed{\quad J_D\cong
\frac{\mathbb Z[c_1,\ldots,c_b,q_1,\ldots,q_{k-1},p]}
 {(h_{a+1}(Z),\ldots,h_n(Z),\ c_b p h_a(Z))}.\quad}\tag{8}$$

The generating list need not be minimal. The elimination of the squared
head variables is triangular with coefficients +/-1, as in the type C
proof. The extra relation has polynomial degree 2a+b+k.

## 5. Locate both separate images in the D decomposition

The middle polynomial algebra in (7) is exactly

$$L_D=\bigoplus_{\lambda\text{ strict},\lambda_1<b}z_\lambda
 \left(R_M\ \oplus\ s\,R_M/(uw)\right).\tag{9}$$

This follows by expanding D_b using (6). There are no further relations
in its two components: R_M embeds in R, and R_M/(uw) embeds in R/(uw)
by the free module decomposition in Section 3(ii)–(iii).

The tail algebra has no z_lambda except z_empty=1, and is

$$ (M_0)_D=R_T\ \oplus\ p\,R_T/(uv).\tag{10}$$

Again Section 3 proves the exactness of these embedded descriptions.
In the unit component with lambda empty, the sum has free quotient
R/(R_M+R_T). For nonempty lambda, its quotient is R/R_M. In every
s component its quotient is (R/(uw))/(R_M/(uw)), which is free by the
module basis including 1. In the p component with lambda empty it is
(R/(uv))/(R_T/(uv)), also free. All other p components and all sp
components are entire free coefficient rings from (7).

This explicitly decomposes J_D/(L_D+(M_0)_D) into free groups, proving
primitivity. The only possible intersection is in the unit component with
lambda empty, where R_M intersect R_T=Z. This proves the intersection
assertion. Choosing the stated bases componentwise also gives a simultaneous
integral basis of J_D containing bases for both separate images as subsets.

Notice that the splitting in (7) is internal to the normalized image
algebra. It makes no assertion about integral splitting of the saturated
Euler and square sectors in ambient orthogonal cohomology.

## 6. Why ambient torsion can only be 2-primary

Here is the polynomial-module argument needed to exclude odd primes.
For r>=1, Z[x_1,...,x_r] is free, with a homogeneous basis including 1,
over each of its type A, B and D invariant polynomial subrings, using
Cartesian integer coordinates. For D the generators are
e_1(x^2),...,e_(r-1)(x^2),product(x); for B they are e_1(x^2),...,e_r(x^2).

One integral justification is as follows. Over every field these generators
form a homogeneous system of parameters: their common zero is the origin,
including in characteristic two. The quotient is a complete intersection
with a characteristic-independent Hilbert series. The integral quotient
is finite over Z, since elementary relations imply nilpotence of each
coordinate. Equality of dimensions over Q and every prime field excludes
torsion. Lift a homogeneous integral basis, including 1; degree induction
gives generation over the invariant polynomial subring, and equality of
rational Hilbert series proves independence. For type A the same argument
uses ordinary elementary generators.

Tensor the block bases and then impose the global invariant relations.
This shows that the intermediate block-characteristic quotient is a direct
module summand of the full Cartesian coinvariant quotient. In B the block
ring with head squared, middle unsquared, and tail squared becomes exactly
J_B after eliminating the head classes. In D the analogous ring with head
Euler retained is (5). Its even-r component is J_D and is an additive
direct summand: the relation r s p is homogeneous for head parity.
This is a parity decomposition, not division by 2 or Weyl averaging.

After inverting 2, the full Cartesian coinvariant quotient is the
cohomology of the full orthogonal flag by the classical characteristic
presentation: orthogonal torsion primes and the SO/Spin character-lattice
index introduce only the prime 2. Hence J is primitive there over Z[1/2].
Pullback from a partial flag to the full flag is a primitive integral
injection (the Schubert basis is preserved), so the same conclusion holds
in the original partial flag. Thus sat(J)/J has only 2-primary torsion.
This argument also implies that ambient torsion for L+M_0 is 2-primary,
since it is primitive in J.

## 7. Closed ranks

Let z=t^2, with t recording polynomial degree. Define

$$G(N,r)={N\brack r}_{z},\qquad F_j(t)=\prod_{i=1}^j(1+t^i),\quad F_0=1.$$

In type B the three rank polynomials are the type C ones:

$$P_L=F_bG(n,b),\qquad P_{M_0}=G(n,k),\qquad
P_J=F_bG(n,b)G(n-b,k).\tag{11}$$

In type D put

$$Q=G(n,b)G(n-b,k),\quad Q_s=G(n-1,b)G(n-b,k),$$
$$Q_p=G(n-1,k)G(n-k,b),\quad Q_{sp}=G(n-1,b)G(n-1-b,k).$$

Equations (7), (9), (10) give

$$\boxed{P_J=F_{b-1}\left(Q+t^bQ_s+t^kQ_p+t^{b+k}Q_{sp}\right),}\tag{12}$$
$$P_L=F_{b-1}\left(G(n,b)+t^bG(n-1,b)\right),\qquad
P_{M_0}=G(n,k)+t^kG(n-1,k).\tag{13}$$

In either type,

$$J_d/(L_d+(M_0)_d)\cong\mathbb Z^{r_d},\qquad
r_d=\operatorname{coeff}_{t^d}(P_J-P_L-P_{M_0}+1).\tag{14}$$

The +1 corrects the shared constant. These ranks describe the abstract
image and its separate-image quotient, not unit multiplicities in ambient
cohomology. For D4 with (a,b,k)=(1,1,2), (12) gives total joint rank 30;
in degrees four and eight it gives ranks five and three, respectively.

## 8. Sources, audit and remaining work

The published inputs are Borel's rational and prime-local characteristic
presentations and integral complex/quaternionic flag presentations
([Borel](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/mth/sites/doug-ravenel/otherpapers/Borel-Sur.pdf),
§§26–31); the integral Q-tilde identities
([Pragacz–Ratajski](https://arxiv.org/pdf/alg-geom/9602019), Props. 4.2–4.3,
4.7 and Appendix A); and Schubert compatibility with parabolic projections
([Brion](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0410240), §§1.2–1.3).
Classical orthogonal flag presentations are also discussed in
[Tamvakis](https://www.math.umd.edu/~harryt/papers/giamdegcl.pdf).

Parts [II](https://kumar.math.unc.edu/papers/kumar81.pdf) and
[III](https://kumar.math.unc.edu/papers/kumar86.pdf) provide the Springer
normalization and the single-tail framework. The joint statements above
are deductions about the specified two-factor source; they are not quoted
as theorems of those papers. No claim that the abstract kernel is new is
made. The type C literature audit remains applicable to its classical
inputs, but no separate exhaustive B/D priority search is claimed here.

| Possible error | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Same rational B/C kernel mistaken for equal ambient lattices | Only the integral source kernels and abstract image algebras are identified. |
| D global Euler relation omitted or squared away | The intermediate ring retains r s p=0, producing u s p in the joint ring. |
| Rational averaging used over Z | Averaging establishes only the rational contraction; the four-piece free decomposition proves the integral upgrade. |
| Q-tilde top-part multiplication assumed without checking | Terms c_(b+i) vanish for i>0, so the Pfaffian factors by c_b; pair-removal gives its square. |
| Torsion hidden in R/(uw), R/(uv), or R/(u) | The first two are free base changes over smaller Grassmann rings, and the third is a smaller flag ring. |
| Separate images identified only up to rational span | Equations (6), (9), (10) and the unit module bases identify their actual Z-spans. |
| A primitive sum in J confused with ambient primitivity | Explicit nonunit joint factors remain; the ambient conclusion only excludes odd primes. |
| Type D end nodes or a=0 silently included | The theorem assumes a,b>=1 and k>=2 in D. |

The [verification report](experiments/orthogonal_joint_structure_verification.json)
records exact finite coverage and source hashes. It compares (11)–(14) to
Schubert image matrices, checks both separate images and their quotient,
and checks that multiples of the displayed relations form the entire
integer kernel in every tested degree: they vanish, have the correct rank,
and have only unit Smith factors in the source lattice. Selected matrices
are independently reconstructed by Cartesian BGG.

The remaining problem is a closed formula for the ambient joint-image
Smith multiplicities (including powers higher than two). The primitive-sum,
constant-intersection, integral kernel, and rank questions are settled by
the argument above for the stated two-step families. More flag blocks and
other character normalizations require separate statements.

This proof does not depend on the earlier single-tail B Smith multiplicity
theorem or the D square-comparison and even-tail gluing proofs. Its
dependencies are the type C common-basis argument and the classical
polynomial and flag-ring facts stated here. In particular, the absence of
torsion in J/(L+M_0) is not inferred from a conjectural comparison of ambient
Smith factors.
