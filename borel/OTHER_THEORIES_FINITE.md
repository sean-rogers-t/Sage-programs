# Springer-character images in K-theory and oriented theories

Exploration, 22 October 2026. The constructions and arguments below concern
the **full literal integral polynomial-character source** for the normalized
trace Springer morphism on a finite simply connected simple complex group.
They do not automatically apply to the smaller rationally normalized Z-forms
used for some generators in Parts II and III. Published inputs, deductions,
finite computations and remaining questions are identified separately.
No priority claim is made for the deductions.

## 1. A direct K-theory map already exists

Let G,T,P,L,W,W_L,X have the meaning used in the Kumar papers. Fix rho
with faithful differential and normalized theta=theta_rho, so theta(1)=0
and d theta_1=id. The existing integral source theorem gives

\[
 A_{\rho,L}=\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathbb Z}(\Lambda_\rho)^{W_L},\qquad
 \theta^*A_{\rho,L}\subseteq R(L)=\mathbb Z[X]^{W_L}.
\]

The cohomological map and its image are

\[
 \xi_\rho^P(f)=\beta^P(f),\qquad J_H=\beta^P(A_{\rho,L}).
\]

Here f is the transported polynomial; the corresponding input character
is theta^*f. Let kappa_P:R(L)->K^0(G/P) be the associated-bundle map.
Then there is a canonical integral map

\[
 \eta_\rho^P:A_{\rho,L}\longrightarrow K^0(G/P),\qquad
                 f\longmapsto\kappa_P(\theta^*f),\quad J_K=\operatorname{im}\eta.
                                                                    \tag{1}
\]

The construction in (1) is a restriction of the published characteristic
map, not a new definition of ambient K-theory. Kumar's polynomial-character
source is already a subring of the representation ring. Its published
definition is [Kumar, Part I, Definition 4 and Theorem 5](https://kumar.math.unc.edu/papers/kumar76.pdf).
The associated-bundle and Demazure frameworks are classical; see
[Griffeth--Ram, introduction and Sections 1--2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0405333).

The literal-source qualification matters. On Sp4/P1=CP3, the rationally
normalized coordinate epsilon_1 has ordinary image h, but its formal
associated K-class is (L-L^(-1))/2=v-v^2/2+v^3/2 in
K^0(CP3)_Q, where L=1+v and v^4=0. It is not integral. The literal
generator 2epsilon_1 gives the integral class 2v-v^2+v^3. Consequently
ordinary integrality of a selected normalized generator cannot replace
integrality of its actual virtual character in (1).

Use the filtration F^d K^0 by codimension of support (equivalently the
even skeletal filtration on this complex cellular space). Its associated
graded is CH^*(G/P)=H^(2*)(G/P;Z). This is the **topological/support
filtration**, not the gamma filtration; the two need not agree. The ambient
Chow-to-graded-K comparison and its connective version are published inputs;
see [Karpenko, Theorem 2.2 and the split case in Theorem 3.1](https://sites.ualberta.ca/~karpenko/publ/connective.pdf).

## 2. Common kernel and a strict filtered comparison

**Theorem.** For the source and normalization above, the maps beta^P and
eta_rho^P have the same integral source kernel. Consequently

\[
 \mathcal F_\rho^P:J_H\xrightarrow{\sim}J_K,\qquad
                    \beta^P(f)\longmapsto\kappa_P(\theta^*f)          \tag{2}
\]

is an isomorphism of Z-algebras. It is strict for the descending polynomial
degree filtration on J_H and the filtration induced from F^*K^0 on J_K.
The associated-graded embedding is exactly the original one:

\[
                  \operatorname{gr}_F J_K=J_H\subseteq CH^*(G/P).    \tag{3}
\]

This statement compares the **images** and the support filtration. It
does not identify the augmentation or gamma filtration of a representation
ring with this filtration.

### Proof

Work first over Q on the full flag. Write I_W for the ideal in
Sym_Q(X_Q) generated by the positive-degree W-invariant polynomials.
The rational Borel theorem identifies its quotient with H^(2*)(G/B;Q).
If f is a positive-degree W-invariant polynomial, theta^*f is a rational
virtual G-character. Its dimension is f(theta(1))=f(0)=0. Its associated
G-bundle is trivial of virtual rank zero, so kappa_B(theta^*f)=0.
Multiplicativity therefore kills the whole ideal I_W.

The Chern character of kappa_B(theta^*ell), for a linear ell, begins with
ell in polynomial degree one: theta(1)=0 kills the rank and d theta_1=id
gives the first jet. A homogeneous polynomial f of degree d therefore
has leading support-filtration class beta^B(f) in degree d. The induced
map H^(2*)(G/B;Q)->K^0(G/B)_Q is triangular with identity diagonal on
its associated graded. The filtration is finite, so this map is an
isomorphism. Thus its source kernel is exactly I_W.

For P, pullback to G/B is injective in both cohomology and K-theory.
For K-theory this also follows from pi_* pi^*=id, since the fibers have
pi_*O=O and higher O-cohomology zero. Restriction to W_L-invariants gives
the same rational-kernel assertion on G/P. Both integral targets are
torsion-free, so their kernels on A_(rho,L) are the intersection with this
common rational kernel. They are therefore equal.

To see strictness, choose a Z-basis of each homogeneous image J_H^d and
homogeneous source lifts. The corresponding classes in J_K have exactly
those independent leading classes. A nonzero combination with smallest
source degree d has a nonzero leading class in degree d. Thus it cannot
enter F^(d+1). This proves strictness and (3). QED.

### What follows, and what does not

The total lattice index is determined by the old graded image:

\[
 [K^0(G/P):J_K]=\prod_d[H^{2d}(G/P;\mathbb Z):J_H^d].               \tag{4}
\]

The finite cokernel K^0/J_K has a filtration whose graded quotients are
the old cohomological cokernels. Its **total Smith factors can change**,
because those finite groups can be joined by nontrivial extensions.
Equation (4) determines the order, rather than the extension classes.

If two integral source subalgebras are compared using the **same** theta,
the single map (2) identifies their image sums, intersections and relative
quotient groups. A change of representation changes F_rho; containment
between two cohomological images therefore need not persist in K-theory.
These distinctions prevent treating all previous Smith tables as ambient
K-theory tables.

### A finite representation-theoretic description of the K-map

There is an efficient refinement of the old lattice classification.
Write R(G)=Z[chi1,...,chir] in the fundamental characters, put
di=dim(V(omega_i)), and let m_G be its augmentation ideal. Define

\[
 \tau_\rho=\frac{[\chi_\rho-\dim\rho]}{I_\rho}
       \in(m_G/m_G^2)\otimes\mathbb Q,                              \tag{11}
\]

where I_rho is the Dynkin trace index with a single fixed invariant-form
normalization. If chi_rho=P_rho(chi1,...,chir), its coordinates are
a_i/I_rho, where a_i=(partial P_rho/partial chi_i)(d1,...,dr).

**Proposition.** The pair (Lambda_rho,tau_rho) determines the embedded
literal K-image for every parabolic. More explicitly, on linear weights,

\[
 \eta_\rho^B(\ell)=\frac1{I_\rho}
     \sum_i a_i I_{\omega_i}\eta_{\omega_i}^B(\ell)\quad\text{in }K^0(G/B)_\mathbb Q.
                                                                    \tag{12}
\]

The individual linear inputs in (12) may be rational characters; the
full source Lambda_rho is what makes the final image integral. If two
representations have equal normalized cotangent classes tau and nested
source lattices, a common rational map transports their ordinary image
comparison to K-theory. Equality of the lattices alone is insufficient.

**Proof.** The normalized trace formula is the invariant-form gradient
of chi_rho divided by I_rho. Differentiate the character polynomial
P_rho. Under the associated-bundle map each global fundamental character
chi_i becomes the constant di. The chain rule therefore gives exactly
(12). Polynomial substitution and the source lattice give the assertion
for the entire source and then for each Levi. QED.

This is a deduction from the trace formula, the classical polynomial
representation ring and the characteristic map. The cotangent space
m_G/m_G^2 and higher representation indices are established objects;
no claim is made to invent them here. Rationally the same description
can be expressed in basic invariant polynomials: differentiating
chi_rho(exp x) modulo the positive invariant ideal retains only the
indecomposable invariant terms. Their degrees are the basic invariant
degrees, and the linear-coordinate corrections have degrees one less.
Thus G2 has one higher correction (degree five), F4 has degrees 5,7,11,
E6 has 4,5,7,8,11, and E7 has 5,7,9,11,13,17. This supplies a finite
data target for a K-theory API, rather than requiring arbitrarily high
Laurent expansions in every calculation.

## 3. A connective K-theory image and its Fitting ideals

Put R=Z[beta], deg beta=-1 in codimension notation. For a split complex
flag, the published Chow/support comparison identifies CK^*(G/P) with
the extended Rees ring

\[
 \mathcal R_F K=\sum_d \beta^{-d}F^dK^0\ \subseteq K^0[\beta,\beta^{-1}].
\]

For homogeneous f of degree d define

\[
 \eta_{\rho,CK}^P(f)=\beta^{-d}\kappa_P(\theta^*f).                  \tag{5}
\]

Membership in the Rees ring follows from the first-jet argument. The
common homogeneous kernel proves that (5) extends multiplicatively and
that its image is isomorphic, as a graded Z[beta]-algebra, to

\[
                         J_H\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Z[\beta].    \tag{6}
\]

Its specializations beta=0 and beta=1 embed as J_H and J_K respectively.
Thus this specified connective image is flat, with faithful additive and
multiplicative specializations. The **embedding and its cokernel** can
still vary; (6) alone does not make their Smith groups constant.

For example, take G=SL3, P=P1, rho the adjoint representation. Its full
linear source is 6X. With K^0(P2)=Z[v]/v^3, v=[O(1)]-1, the source
has image Z[b] where b=3(L-L^(-1))=6v-3v^2. The cohomological image is
Z[6h]. On the positive-rank part the connective inclusion matrix is

\[
 M_\beta=\begin{pmatrix}6&0\\-3\beta&36\end{pmatrix}.               \tag{7}
\]

Its determinant ideal is (216), while its ideal of entries is
3(2,beta). At beta=0 the nontrivial Smith factors are (6,36); at beta=1
they are (3,72). A Fitting-ideal calculation over Z[beta] captures the
change that a single ordinary Smith list misses. This is an elementary
worked example, not a general Fitting-ideal classification.

## 4. A uniform oriented lift with explicit hypotheses

Let E be a multiplicative complex-oriented topological cohomology theory
whose coefficient ring R=E^*(pt) is even and torsion-free as a Z-module.
Use ordinary cohomology, periodic complex K-theory, connective complex
K-theory, or complex cobordism as examples. For an algebraic oriented
theory one must additionally verify the corresponding cellular comparison;
the topological statement does not assume it without proof.

For ell in Lambda_rho write its integral virtual character

\[
        b_\ell=\theta^*\ell=\sum_\mu a_\mu e^\mu,
        \quad \sum a_\mu=0,\quad\sum a_\mu\mu=\ell.
\]

Use the even skeletal filtration for E; in K-theory it agrees with the
support filtration used above. No algebraic support theory is assumed
for an arbitrary topological E.

On G/B define the **trace-coordinate lift**

\[
 B_{\rho,E}(\ell)=-\sum_\mu a_\mu c_1^E(\mathcal L_{-\mu}).         \tag{8}
\]

It is Z-linear in ell, W-equivariant, and has first support-filtration
term ell. Substitution in polynomials gives an R-algebra map. In additive
cohomology (8) is ell. For multiplicative formal group law
u+v-beta*u*v, it is beta^(-1) kappa(b_ell), agreeing with (5).
This construction is determined by rho and the chosen orientation; it
is not an assertion of an affine trace morphism or of uniqueness among
every possible generalized source construction.

**Theorem.** With the stated even/torsion-free hypotheses, substitution
(8) on A_(rho,L) tensor R descends uniquely from G/B to G/P. Its image
is a strict filtered copy of J_H tensor R, with associated-graded
embedding equal to the original cohomological embedding tensored with R.

**Proof.** The Atiyah-Hirzebruch spectral sequence for a finite flag has
only even cells and even coefficient degrees, so all differentials
vanish. Its finite multiplicative filtration has associated graded
H^(2*)(G/P;Z) tensor R. In particular E^*(G/P) is R-free and Z-torsion-free.
Ordinary Schubert pullback to G/B is primitive and injective. The same
associated-graded statement makes E-pullback strict and injective, with
Z-torsion-free cokernel: the graded cokernel is a free R-module, and
the finite filtration splits as R-modules.

The usual right W-action on K/T acts on E^*(G/B) and on (8).
Over Q, taking invariants is exact, so its associated graded commutes
with W-invariants. Ordinary positive-degree cohomology has no W-invariants.
A positive-filtration W-invariant E-class must therefore vanish over Q,
and then integrally by torsion-freeness. Thus substitution (8) kills
every positive-degree W-invariant source polynomial and its ideal.
For W_L the same filtration argument identifies rational invariants
with rational pullback from G/P. The primitive E-pullback just proved
then gives integral descent for each W_L-invariant source element.

The rational Borel presentation and the identity first jet now prove
that the source kernel is exactly the ordinary Borel kernel after flat
base change to R. The homogeneous image bases used in Section 2 remain
independent after tensoring with R, and their lifts have the same leading
terms. They give an isomorphism from J_H tensor R to the image, and
prove strictness. QED.

The source here is the base change of the **integral** invariant ring;
it is not an enlarged ring of new modular invariants. The coefficient
hypotheses exclude Morava K-theory and mod-p coefficients from this
integral-image conclusion. Existing formal-group and oriented Schubert
frameworks are published; see
[Calmes--Petrov--Zainoulline](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.1341) and
[Calmes--Zainoulline--Zhong](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.7111).
The deduction above specifies one representation-dependent source/image
inside those frameworks. It does not claim a new ambient presentation.

## 5. The linear lattice does not classify the K-image

Let V be the standard SL3 module and compare

\[
 \rho_+=4V\oplus\operatorname{Sym}^2V,\qquad
 \rho_-=4V\oplus\operatorname{Sym}^2(V^*).
\]

Both have dimension 18, Dynkin index 9 (standard module index one),
weighted coefficient lattice D=X, and full linear Springer source 9Q.
For P1 the source is Z[E1,E2], with
E1=27omega1 and E2=81(2omega1-omega2)(omega1+omega2). Both ordinary
images are Z direct-sum 27Z h direct-sum 243Z h^2.

The normalized standard and symmetric-square character identities, after
their associated G-bundles become trivial, give

\[
 \eta_+(E1)=27v+3v^2,\qquad
 \eta_-(E1)=27v-18v^2,\qquad \eta_\pm(E2)=243v^2.                   \tag{9}
\]

Indeed the standard coordinate maps to v, the Sym^2(V) coordinate to
v+v^2/5, and the Sym^2(V*) coordinate to v-6v^2/5. Taking their
Dynkin-index-weighted averages yields (9). Their common relations are
E1^2=3E2 and all products of source degree greater than two equal zero.
Thus (1,E1,E2) is a complete image basis. The ambient Smith factors are

\[
 \begin{array}{c|c}
 \text{ordinary cohomology, either representation}&(1,27,243)\\
 \text{K-theory for }\rho_+&(1,3,2187)\\
 \text{K-theory for }\rho_-&(1,9,729).
 \end{array}                                                       \tag{10}
\]

All total indices are 3^8. This exact example shows that higher trace
moments can change the embedded K-image beyond the information in
Lambda_rho. The cohomological source classification remains valid;
the new ambient K-arithmetic retains additional representation data.

In fact this is an infinite family. For a=6k+4, k>=0, put I=a+5 and
replace 4V by aV in the two representations. Both weighted coefficient
lattices remain X, both full sources are IQ, and the two positive-degree
K Smith lists are respectively

\[
                    (3,3I^3),\qquad (9,I^3).                       \tag{13}
\]

Their common ordinary list is (3I,3I^2); all total indices are 9I^3.
To verify the full source, the plus representation has weights a*epsilon_i,
2epsilon_i, and -epsilon_i with the indicated weighted coefficients,
and the minus representation has coefficients (a+1)epsilon_i and
-2epsilon_i. Since a is even, both generate the full weight lattice
in the coefficient-lattice calculation. Their trace Gram matrices are
I times the standard one. The same projective calculation gives
eta_+(E1)=3Iv+3v^2, eta_-(E1)=3Iv-18v^2 and eta(E2)=3I^2v^2.
The gcd of the entries and the determinant give (13), since I is odd
and divisible by three. This proves the family, not just the first
18-dimensional example. In cotangent terms Sym^2(V)=chi_V^2-chi_(V*),
so the normalized data differ even though Lambda agrees.

## 6. Verification, limits and next work

The [Sage pilot](experiments/other_theories_k_pilot.py) uses integer
Laurent Demazure operators and the Euler pairing with Schubert varieties,
independently of the ordinary BGG matrices. The Euler pairing supplies
integral coordinates by its dual ideal-sheaf basis; see
[Brion, Theorem 3.4.1](https://impan.pl/images/Notatki-z-wykadw/IMPANGA/brion.pdf).
It checks complete full-flag source kernels, total indices and ambient
Smith factors for A2 standard/adjoint, C2 standard, G2 minimal/adjoint
and A3 standard, and checks (9)--(10) by direct virtual characters.

The [native independent audit](experiments/audit_other_theories_k.py)
uses weight moments and the Riemann-Roch identity
D_i(f)=partial_i(Todd(alpha_i)*f), rather than Laurent Demazure strings.
It reproduces every matrix and Smith list with SymPy, and reconstructs
the SL3 pair's coefficient lattice from its elementary representation
weights. Reports: [primary](experiments/other-theories-k-pilot.json),
[audit](experiments/other-theories-k-audit.json).
For both G2 representations it also reconstructs the full weights,
moment matrix, literal source lattice and every linear Laurent pullback
from explicit short/long roots, without using Sage representation input.
The remaining cases share the certified root/character input but use
independent evaluation and Smith arithmetic.

The G2 audit also finds that the adjoint K-image is not contained in the
minimal K-image, although their cohomological images have the old graded
4^d containment. It records a nonintegral image-basis coordinate. This is
a complete finite comparison, not an exceptional-group classification.

### Specialization defects have a single algebraic description

Let R=MU^*(pt), H=MU^*(G/P), J the specified trace-coordinate image,
and C=H/J. Both H and J are finite free R-modules by the even-cell
argument and the theorem above. For a complex-oriented theory with even
coefficients S, the cellular comparison identifies
H tensor_R S with E^*(G/P): the finite cell filtrations split over R
and the natural comparison is an isomorphism on their graded pieces.
The source here is A_(rho,L) tensor S, not an enlarged modular invariant
ring. Tensoring 0 -> J -> H -> C -> 0 gives

\[
 0\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(C,S)
 \longrightarrow J\otimes_R S\longrightarrow E^*(G/P)
 \longrightarrow C\otimes_R S\longrightarrow0.                    \tag{14}
\]

Thus extra modular source relations are exactly the specialization
kernel Tor_1^R(C,S), after the already-known ordinary kernel has been
factored out. Torsion-free S gives no such defect by the theorem; a
characteristic-p specialization can have one. The explicit
[symplectic height formula](OTHER_THEORIES_HEIGHT.md) computes that
phenomenon in a complete family. This is standard homological algebra
applied to the specified embedding, not a new Tor theorem.

There is also a useful uniform prime-support conclusion. Choose
homogeneous image bases and ambient MU bases lifting ordinary Schubert
bases, ordered by support codimension. The universal inclusion matrix
is block triangular; its diagonal blocks are the old integer image
matrices. Thus, putting

\[
 D_\rho^P=\prod_d[H^{2d}(G/P;\mathbb Z):J_H^d],
\]

the determinant and zeroth Fitting ideal are

\[
                \det M=\pm D_\rho^P,\qquad
                \operatorname{Fitt}_0(C)=(D_\rho^P).              \tag{15}
\]

The adjugate identity shows D*C=0, and after any even-coefficient
orientation specialization the same determinant holds. In particular,
after inverting D the specified image is the entire ambient theory.
No new bad primes appear in a finite-flag oriented lift; the old full
literal-source bound confines them to primes dividing t_G*e_rho.
This does not keep the old **exponents**: on Sp6/P1=CP5 the largest
ordinary factor is 2^5, whereas the lifted K-image has largest factor
2^7. The [arbitrary-rank calculation](OTHER_THEORIES_HEIGHT.md#5-an-arbitrary-rank-integral-k-theory-consequence)
proves that difference structurally.

The same observation applies to the equivariant pilot: with
S=H_T^*(pt), I its augmentation ideal, H_T free over S and
C_T=H_T/J_T, the kernel of J_T/IJ_T -> J_H is
Tor_1^S(C_T,Z). Ambient specialization being correct does not force
image specialization to be faithful.

The next substantial questions are the Fitting ideals of the connective
embedding, the extension classes joining the old graded Smith quotients,
and comparisons between different representations. The abstract filtered
comparison itself follows from the common kernel and the first jet.
An equivariant or quantum analogue needs further work because global
positive invariants no longer vanish in the same way.
