# Audit of degree-one generation for trace Springer sources

Audit date: 2026-10-20. This is a proof and source audit of
[the general theorem](TRACE_SOURCE_THEOREM.md), not external peer review
or formal verification. No gap was found in the argument under its stated
hypotheses. The exact integral statement was not located in the sources
reviewed below; its literature priority remains unresolved.

## Exact statement and scope

Let G be simply connected, simple and complex, with maximal torus T,
character lattice X=X(T), and V=X tensor Q. Use the normalized trace
Springer morphism theta_rho associated to a representation with faithful
differential. Define

\[
\mathcal C_\rho=\{f\in\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathbb Q}(V):
                         \theta_\rho^*f\in\mathbb Z[X]\},\qquad
\Lambda_\rho=\mathcal C_\rho\cap V.
\]

Then C_rho=Sym_Z(Lambda_rho), including mixed-degree polynomials.
Degree means degree on the symmetric-algebra side. On the character side
this is the grading transported through the injective Springer pullback;
it is not Laurent total degree.

For a standard Levi L the literal integral polynomial-character source is
Sym_Z(Lambda_rho)^(W_L). This need not be generated by its linear invariant
elements. For instance, the full Weyl invariants for SL2 have a quadratic
generator and no nonzero linear invariant. The theorem also does not say
that a Borel image is saturated, surjective over Z, or generated by divisor
classes after taking invariants.

The proof concerns this characteristic-zero representation construction
and reductions of its integral Laurent polynomials. It does not claim
existence of trace projection for a group over every finite field, nor
invertibility of its trace form in every characteristic. Faithful
differential suffices; a finite central kernel is allowed. Broader
reductive groups or other Springer maps are not additional claims here.

## Proof dependency audit

### 1. Coordinates, normalization, and existence of the lattice

Use fundamental weights as an integral basis of X. For weight columns mu
and positive multiplicities m_mu put

\[
M=\sum_\mu m_\mu\mu\mu^t,\qquad
q=\theta_\rho^*(\omega)=M^{-1}\sum_\mu m_\mu\mu e^\mu.
\]

The weights span V, so M is positive definite over R and invertible over
Q. Differentiating gives dq_1=M^(-1)M=I. In particular the pullback is
injective: its differential has full rank at the identity, so the map
has dense image in the affine space of the same dimension.

If A is the finite rational coefficient matrix of q, it has rank r.
Clearing denominators puts d Z^r inside A^(-1)Z^N for some positive d;
a nonsingular r-row submatrix bounds this group inside a rational
lattice. Thus Lambda_rho=A^(-1)Z^N is a full, finitely generated lattice,
and choosing its integral basis B is justified.

Verdict: valid. No nondegeneracy assumption modulo a prime is used.

### 2. Full linear integrality gives primitive coefficient columns

Set b_i=theta_rho^*(ell_i) for the basis columns ell_i of B and C=AB.
For z in Q^r, Cz integral implies Bz in Lambda_rho, hence z in Z^r.
Therefore

\[
C\mathbb Z^r=C\mathbb Q^r\cap\mathbb Z^N.
\]

The columns are primitive; the nonzero Smith factors are all one and
the maximal minors have gcd one. Consequently rank(C mod p)=r for
every prime p. Fullness of Lambda_rho is essential. Merely clearing
denominators in a convenient rational basis would not establish it.

Verdict: valid. The runtime checks this hypothesis, rather than assuming
that any integral linear basis is the full lattice.

### 3. The trace factorization transfers independence to exponents

Let K=B^t M^(-1). The coefficient row at a nonzero support exponent is

\[
C_{\mu,*}=m_\mu\mu^tK^t.
\]

For any r selected rows, its rational determinant is the product of the
nonzero multiplicities, det(K), and the exponent determinant. A
coefficient minor nonzero over Q therefore selects Q-independent
integer exponents. The zero weight has zero trace moment and is omitted.

Verdict: valid, and essential. This extra structure is what an arbitrary
primitive collection of Laurent polynomials lacks.

### 4. Prime-dependent row reduction supplies leading exponents

Fix p. List the finite support in descending lexicographic order.
Reduced row echelon form of C^t over F_p is U C^t for some U in GL_r(F_p).
Its pivot columns select an invertible coefficient minor modulo p and
are exactly the leading exponents of the transformed polynomials U b.
That integer minor is also nonzero over Q, so Step 3 makes the selected
exponents mu_1,...,mu_r independent over Q.

Only the coefficient minor must be invertible modulo p. The exponent
determinant may be divisible by p, and K may have p in its denominator.
The factorization is applied over Q, never reduced modulo p. Also U may
depend on p; an integral unimodular lift or a single basis for all primes
is unnecessary.

Verdict: valid. Conflating these coefficient and exponent determinants
would produce an unjustified restriction to good primes.

### 5. Leading terms prove algebraic independence even when inseparable

For an additive lexicographic order, the leading exponent of
g_1^(a_1)...g_r^(a_r), where g=U b, is sum(a_i mu_i). Two different
nonnegative integer exponent tuples cannot give the same sum. In any
finite polynomial relation in the g_i, the largest of these distinct
leading terms consequently cannot cancel. This proves algebraic
independence over F_p in all degrees, including relations with constants
and mixed degrees. Invertibility of U gives the same conclusion for b.

This argument uses integer exponents, not exponents reduced modulo p.
For example, t1^2,t2^2 are independent in characteristic two despite
their zero Jacobian. A global well-order on Z^r is not required because
every polynomial and every relation has finite support.

Verdict: valid. Characteristic-p inseparability is harmless here.

### 6. The denominator step proves the entire integral intersection

Write f=F(ell)/D with integral F and D>0 minimal among coefficient
denominators. If p divides D, then F mod p is nonzero. Integrality of
F(b)/D forces F(b) to vanish modulo p, contradicting Step 5. Thus D=1.
The other inclusion is immediate from the integrality of the b_i.

Verdict: valid. This proves all degrees at once, rather than only the
degrees covered by computation. It also proves that the intersection
is graded; gradedness is not assumed at the start.

### 7. Descent, Levi invariants, and integral Borel images

An integral character in the complex polynomial image has a polynomial
preimage of some finite degree. The coefficient matrix on that finite
space is rational and injective, so its unique preimage has rational
coordinates. Thus the rational formulation loses no integral characters.

Equivariance makes Lambda_rho Weyl-stable. Injectivity identifies the
W_L-fixed part of C_rho with the integral character intersection for L.
Taking invariants of an equality needs no division by |W_L|.

Finally, differentiating an integral Laurent pullback at 1 shows
Lambda_rho is contained in X, using d(theta_rho)_1=id. Its weight
polynomials have integral full-flag Borel images. Integral Schubert
pullback H*(G/P,Z) -> H*(G/B,Z) is a primitive inclusion: the classes
indexed by minimal coset representatives form a subset of the full-flag
Schubert basis. Hence a rational partial-flag image whose pullback is
integral is integral as well. This establishes integrality, not
primitivity or saturation of the image of our source.

Verdict: valid with these distinctions.

## Adversarial controls and computational limits

Primitive coefficients alone do not prove the theorem. The Laurent
polynomials b1=t, b2=t^2+2v have a primitive linear coefficient lattice,
but (b2-b1^2)/2=v is integral. Modulo two they satisfy b2=b1^2. Their
independent coefficient rows at t and t^2 correspond to dependent
exponents, violating Step 3. The trace certificate rejects this example.
Conversely, a proper doubled sublattice retains the trace factorization
but fails the primitive-column test and is also rejected.

The [saved survey](experiments/trace-source-survey-results.json) covers
59 representation cases, 354 prime-adapted checks, and 18 searches
through degree three. The [separate Python audit](experiments/audit_trace_source_survey.py)
reconstructs weights and multiplicities from root strings/Freudenthal,
checks Weyl dimensions, and rebuilds the trace and lattices. Saturation
is verified by small exact minors; a different coordinate order checks
leading exponents. It imports neither Sage nor the runtime library.
Freudenthal is a shared mathematical formula with a separate
implementation, not a distinct weight-multiplicity theorem.

These computations test the implementation and controls. Neither their
finite prime list nor their degree bound replaces Steps 1-6. The previous
128-test regression report still applies to the unchanged runtime; this
audit makes explanatory and attribution changes only.

The elementary components (primitive lattices, row reduction, leading
monomials and denominator clearing) are standard. Their use here is not
an originality claim. Nor do the cited authors explicitly pose our
integral-generation question in the passages checked.

## Search record and conclusion

Web and source checks on 2026-10-20 combined author-hosted papers and
arXiv texts with searches for the following formulations and variants:

- Springer morphism / generalized Cayley map with integral, character
  lattice, polynomial characters, integral form, and degree-one.
- Integral polynomial character ring, symmetric algebra / group ring,
  and saturated Laurent subalgebra.
- Logarithmic gradient / logarithmic derivatives, Laurent polynomial,
  positive characteristic, algebraic independence, and logarithmic Hesse.
- Gauss lemma / valuation and algebraically independent reductions.

Representative exact queries included `"Springer morphism" "degree-one"`,
`"integral form" "Springer morphism"`,
`"polynomial characters" "lattice"`, and
`"logarithmic derivatives" "Laurent" "algebraic independence"`.
Terminology varies; irrelevant hits about nilpotent Springer
isomorphisms, Cayley birationality, quantum integral forms, or saturation
of monomial semigroups were not treated as this result.

**Conclusion:** the proof passes this audit under the stated trace-map
hypotheses. The exact integral equality was not found in the reviewed
literature. This is a proved deduction recorded here with unresolved
priority, not an established claim of a new theorem. A specialist may
know an equivalent statement under other terminology.
