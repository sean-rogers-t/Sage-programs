# The full specialization annihilator: reduction and remaining gap

**Historical note, superseded on 28 October:** the gap recorded here is
now closed by [the integral difference-trace recurrence](TYPE_A_K_INTEGRAL_RECURRENCE.md).
It proves m Delta_m=0 in every rank. The earlier finite checks and failed
shortcuts below remain as provenance; their negative general-proof flags
describe this earlier attempt, not the current theorem status.

27 October 2026. Continuation of IMG-37/38, not a proof of the conjecture.
The representation is the defining representation of SL_m, the parabolic
is P1, and the source is the full literal root-polynomial character source
of [the specialization note](TYPE_A_K_SPECIALIZATION.md). No source is
replaced by a coefficient slice in the statements below.

## 1. The precise target and present answer

Use S=Z[c_1,...,c_(m-1)], A=S[L]/F(L), and J=S[f_1,...,f_(m-1)]
as in that note. Put a_k=c_k-binomial(m,k), I=(a_1,...,a_(m-1)).
The conjecture is

    m Delta_m=0,  Delta_m=(J intersect I A)/(I J).

Follow-up: [homogeneous descent and full-source certificates](TYPE_A_K_GRADED_LIFTS.md)
now prove 7 Delta_7=0, including every overflow product, without a complete
image-module calculation. Combined with the lower bound this gives exact
exponent 7. It does not supply uniform integral lifts or multiplicities.

**Status: unproved in arbitrary rank.** The complete presentations through
m=6 prove it in those ranks. The prime-power family gives a quotient of
exponent m for m=p^s>=3, so the proposed bound would be sharp there.
Neither that quotient nor the reduction below proves the upper bound.

This attempt gives an explicit, complete list of multiplicative relations
whose lifts would suffice. For prime-power m the list consists entirely
of quadratic relations in normalized ORIGINAL source generators. It also
explains why checking only first-order coefficient deformations cannot
prove the conjecture. We found the two uniform lifts (7)-(8), but not the required complete
family of lifts.

## 2. Ordinary inputs already established in the program

At augmentation put v=L-1. Then A_0=Z[v]/v^m and

    f_j(0)=b_j v^j,  b_j=binomial(m,j).

IMG-11, [section 6 of the earlier image theorem](LOCAL_IMAGE_BOUND.md),
already proved that the full ordinary image has basis

    1, d_1 v,...,d_(m-1) v^(m-1),  d_j=m/gcd(m,j).

The same calculation applies here because the displayed K-generators
have exactly these coefficients. This is not a new ordinary Smith theorem.
In particular m A_0 is contained in J_0.

For completeness, fix p|m, s=v_p(m), b=p^s, and work over Z_(p).
Set w_j=max(0,s-v_p(j)) and w_0=0. The p-local ordinary basis is

    e_0=1, e_j=p^w_j v^j,  1<=j<m.

Its COMPLETE algebra presentation has generators X_1,...,X_(m-1) and
relations, for 1<=i<=j<m,

    X_i X_j - p^(w_i+w_j-w_(i+j)) X_(i+j) = 0  if i+j<m,
    X_i X_j = 0                              if i+j>=m.       (1)

The exponent in (1) is nonnegative: v_p(i+j)>=min(v_p(i),v_p(j)),
so w_(i+j)<=max(w_i,w_j)<=w_i+w_j. These relations reduce any monomial
to a linear combination of 1,X_1,...,X_(m-1). Their images in A_0 are
linearly independent over Z_(p), proving that there are no missing
relations. This argument proves completeness, not just product identities.

## 3. Explicit lifts of the ordinary basis in the full source

Write j=q b+r with 0<=r<b. Define the following monomial in the original
source variables z_1,...,z_(m-1):

    M_j=z_b^q z_r if r>0, and M_j=z_b^q if r=0.

When b=m, q=0 for every index under consideration, so M_j=z_j and
the unavailable z_m is never used. Put

    B_j=b_b^q b_r if r>0, and B_j=b_b^q if r=0,
    u_j=B_j/p^w_j,   g_j=M_j/u_j.

Again factors with q=0 are omitted. Every u_j is a p-local unit.
Indeed b_b is a unit when b<m, while for 0<r<b,

    binomial(m,r)=(m/r) binomial(m-1,r-1)

has valuation s-v_p(r): the lower s base-p digits of m-1 are all p-1.
These are the same valuation facts used in IMG-11. Thus g_j(f) belongs
to the FULL p-local source image and specializes to e_j.

Put beta_j=b_j/p^w_j, a p-local integer. In the polynomial ring
P=Z_(p)[z_1,...,z_(m-1)] the entire ordinary kernel K_0 is generated by

    z_j-beta_j g_j,                                      (2)
    g_i g_j-p^(w_i+w_j-w_(i+j))g_(i+j),  i+j<m,           (3)
    g_i g_j,                              i+j>=m.         (4)

Proof: all displayed expressions vanish ordinarily. Modulo them, (2)
makes the quotient generated by the g_j, and (3)-(4) reduce every
polynomial to the span of 1,g_1,...,g_(m-1). Their ordinary images are
independent. Hence any polynomial in K_0 reduces to zero. This proves
that the list is complete, including relations of arbitrarily high degree.

For m=p^s, M_j=z_j, u_j=beta_j is a unit, so (2) is identically zero.
Only the m(m-1)/2 quadratic relations (3)-(4) remain. Normalizing these
generators uses only p-local units, never division by p.

## 4. Exact lifting criterion, not an accomplished lift

Here write S_p=S tensor_Z Z_(p), A_p=A tensor_Z Z_(p), and
J_p=J tensor_Z Z_(p). This localizes integer coefficients only, NOT
at the height-one ideal (p) of S used in the earlier determinant proof.
Let K_sp be the kernel of P -> J_p/I J_p, z_j -> [f_j].
There is an exact sequence of ideals

    0 -> K_sp -> K_0 -> (Delta_m)_(p) -> 0.

Here Delta is an ideal in the specialized image algebra; it need not be
generated as an ABELIAN GROUP by the displayed finite list. Ideal
generation suffices for the annihilator question because p^s is central.
Consequently the following are equivalent:

* p^s kills (Delta_m)_(p);
* p^s times each relation (2)-(4) belongs to K_sp;
* after evaluating z_j=f_j(c,L), p^s times each such relation belongs
  to I J_p, inside A_p.

Thus the missing uniform identity is quite specific. For every i,j one
must express the p^s multiple of (3) or (4), and in general also (2), as

    sum_(k=1)^(m-1) (c_k-binomial(m,k)) H_k(c,f),           (5)

with H_k in Z_(p)[c_1,...,c_(m-1),f_1,...,f_(m-1)], modulo F(L).
An expression with H_k merely in A_p does NOT suffice. Neither does
a rational identity whose denominator contains p.

This is a finite all-rank reduction of the conjecture to full-source
relations. It is not a proof of those relations and does not improve the
known general upper bound m^(m-1).

### Two uniform lifts that do work

There is a small genuine partial result within this reduction. For a
prime power m>=3, the quadratic relation involving f_1^2 already vanishes
in the specialized full image. For prime-power m>=4, the relation involving
f_1 f_2 is killed by m. These statements concern two families of relations,
not every quadratic relation or the whole defect.

Set C_2=(m-1)c_1^2-2m c_2. Direct polynomial expansion gives

    f_1^2-[2m/(m-1)]f_2 = C_2/(m-1).                    (7)

The denominator m-1 is a p-local unit and C_2 is in I. Thus (7) is
already in IJ, without multiplication by m. Next set

    D_3=-c_1 c_2+[3m/(m-2)]c_3.

For m=p^s>=4, 3m/(m-2) is p-integral: m-2 is a unit for odd p, and
has valuation one for p=2 (where s>=2). Polynomial expansion gives

    f_1 f_2-[3m/(m-2)]f_3 = C_2 L+D_3,
    m(f_1 f_2-[3m/(m-2)]f_3)
       = C_2 f_1+(C_2 c_1+m D_3).                      (8)

Both C_2 and D_3 augment to zero. Thus the last line is an explicit
expression of form (5), with coefficients in the full coefficient ring
and source. The constants C_2 c_1+mD_3 are in I, and 1 is in J.
Division by the p-local units u_1 u_2 identifies (8) with m times the
normalized relation (3); the analogous division by u_1^2 applies to (7).
No coefficient slice, rationalization at p, or source enlargement occurs.
The argument for (8) does not apply to m=3 because f_3 is unavailable.
It also does not cover i+j>=m or arbitrary i,j.

## 5. Why the immediate multiplicative and first-order arguments stop

The identity mL=f_1+c_1 says mL is in J. It does not imply m L^j is
in J for all j. Already for m=3 the full module from IMG-37 has basis
generators 1,3L,3L^2-2c_1 L,c_1^2 L,c_1^2 L^2. If 3L^2 belonged to
this span, comparison of L^2 coefficients and then L coefficients would
give 2c_1 in the ideal (3,c_1^2), which is false. This remains false
locally at (3,c_1-3,c_2-3). Thus m(A/J)=0 is false even where
m Delta_m=0 is true.

There is nevertheless a uniform FIRST-ORDER cancellation. More generally,
for any k>=1 and x in J intersect I^k A, the ordinary containment
m A_0 subset J_0 gives y in I^k J with

    m x-y in J intersect I^(k+1) A.                       (6)

Proof: A is free over the polynomial coefficient ring S, so each degree-k
coefficient of x modulo I^(k+1) is in A_0. Multiply it by m and lift its
ordinary image to J. Multiply these lifts by their degree-k parameter
monomials and add them. This gives y and proves (6).

Let D^k be the image of J intersect I^k A in Delta_m. Then

    m D^k subset D^(k+1).

This raises the parameter order; it does NOT make the result zero.
The issue is real, even in the already solved cubic case. Put a=c_1-3,
u=3L, x=c_1^2 L. Then

    z=x-(3+2a)u=a^2 L

belongs to J intersect I^2 A and represents a NONZERO class of Delta_3.
Its class equals [x-3u], one of the order-three classes from IMG-37.
Also 3z=a^2u is in IJ. Thus D^2 can be nonzero. Vanishing to second
order is not an annihilation certificate. Iterating (6) multiplies by
additional powers of m, so it cannot establish the proposed single m.

## 6. Independent checks and what remains

The finite certificate builder uses the existing COMPLETE full-image
presentations for m=2,...,6. If B_0 is the augmented generator matrix and
R_0 its augmented relation matrix, it produces an integral basis K for
ker(B_0) and an integer matrix W satisfying

    R_0 W=m K.

A separate native SymPy audit reconstructs B_0 and R_0 from the saved
polynomial presentations, verifies B_0 K=0, checks that K is primitive
and has the full kernel rank, and verifies R_0 W=m K exactly. Thus it
checks explicit annihilation witnesses rather than just rereading Smith
factors. Completeness of the full image presentations remains supported
by the earlier integral closure and syzygy calculations; this is not an
independent reimplementation of that entire standard-basis engine.

The native audit separately checks the ordinary content recurrence,
p-local monomial lifts and multiplication tables through m=64, and the
cubic second-order identity. It checks (7)-(8) as symbolic identities
in the rank parameter m, not merely at finitely many ranks. The proofs above, rather than that finite
range, establish the reduction in every rank. Additional one-coefficient
probes at m=8,9 found no counterexample, but remain quotient tests and
are not promoted to full-image certificates or a proof of any bound.

Published/general tools: elementary binomial valuations, finite algebra
presentations, exact sequences and Smith normal form. The ordinary
content formula is prior repository result IMG-11. The lifting reduction
and explicit witness audit are deductions here, with no external-review
or novelty claim. A limited search on Hasse derivatives and translation
invariants did not supply a theorem proving (5); this is not a literature
classification of the conjecture.
