# Integral trace recurrence for the full type A specialization defect

28 October 2026. Continuation of IMG-37/38. This note concerns the defining
SL_m/P1 Springer--Borel K-image, with its full literal root-polynomial
source. It does not concern the shelved Cayley-fiber problem.

## 1. The theorem

The all-rank conclusion is:

    m Delta_m = 0, for every m>=2.                         (1)

The bound is sharp for EVERY m>=3: Delta_m has exponent exactly m.
Delta_2=0. A separate degree-three order detector below proves the
sharpness, using IMG-37's nonzero 2-primary part when m=2 modulo 4.

Here Delta_m is the ACTUAL specialization kernel defined in
[IMG-37](TYPE_A_K_SPECIALIZATION.md), not a coefficient-family quotient,
the ordinary cokernel, or a rationalized image. The written proof below gives
an integral recurrence constructing witnesses for all homogeneous
relations. It includes overflow products and the mixed-prime replacement
relations; it does not require a new rank calculation.

Explicitly, G=SL_m(C), rho is the defining representation, and P=P1
stabilizes a line. Here m is the defining dimension; the Lie rank is
m-1. Put

    R=Z[t_1^+-1,...,t_m^+-1]/(product t_i-1), I_T=(t_i-1),
    A_T=K_T^0(G/P)=R[L]/product_i(L-t_i),
    f_j=[u^(m-j)] product_i(L+u-t_i),
    J_T=R[f_1,...,f_(m-1)] subset A_T.

The normalized Springer pullback sends x_i-x_j to t_i-t_j, so these
are the associated-bundle images of the full literal root-polynomial
source Sym_Z(Q_root)^(S_(m-1)). If J_0 is its ordinary K-image, then

    Delta_m=ker(J_T/I_T J_T -> J_0).

Thus (1) says that specializing the equivariant IMAGE can create extra
classes, but multiplying each such class by m kills it. It does not say
the ordinary image equals the whole ordinary K-ring.

The ingredients are a difference-trace operator, integral Hasse
translation, and the already established binomial-content formula.
The last formula is not a new result: compare IMG-11 and Karpenko,
*Partially splitting PGO-torsors*, Lemma 5.2 (reference below).
The deduction here is their application to the full specialization kernel.
No literature-priority or external-review claim is made.

## 2. A more general coefficient-family theorem

Let S be a commutative Z-algebra with an augmentation epsilon:S->Z,
I=ker(epsilon). Let F(X) be monic of degree m and suppose

    epsilon(F(X))=(X-a)^m, with a in Z.

Put A=S[L]/F(L). Define all the jets by

    F(L+t)=sum_(j=0)^m f_j t^(m-j),
    f_0=1, f_m=0 in A,
    J=S[f_1,...,f_(m-1)] subset A.

Then the kernel of J/IJ -> A/IA is finite and annihilated by m. Equivalently
for the annihilator assertion,

    m (J intersect IA) subset IJ.                        (2)

Only the embedded source generated by ALL these jets is asserted here.
Deleting generators or replacing the source by its linear span is not
covered. The assertion does not say that m(A/J)=0; that stronger claim
already fails in the cubic example.

Translate X and L by a. This does not change the jets, and the coefficients
of the translated polynomial augment to zero. It therefore suffices to
construct universal identities with

    S^u=Z[c_1,...,c_m], I^u=(c_1,...,c_m),
    F(Y)=sum_(k=0)^m (-1)^k c_k Y^(m-k), c_0=1,
    f_j(Y)=sum_(k=0)^j (-1)^k binomial(m-k,j-k)c_k Y^(j-k).

Every universal identity then maps to S and to its actual image J. This
use of identities requires no assumption that images commute with base
change. In particular it gives a direct route back to the original
determinant-one coefficient family, independent of the earlier
homogeneous nonzerodivisor descent.

There is also a direct cohomological upper-bound corollary for this SAME
root-polynomial source. For SL_m take

    S_H=Sym_Z(X(T))=Z[t_1,...,t_m]/(sum_i t_i),
    A_H=H_T^*(P^(m-1);Z)=S_H[L]/product_i(L-t_i),
    J_H=S_H[f_1,...,f_(m-1)], epsilon(t_i)=0.

The standard equivariant projective-bundle presentation identifies this
with the root-polynomial Borel image. Section 2 applies with a=0 and
proves m*ker(J_H/I_H J_H -> ordinary image)=0. This deduction does NOT
identify its defect with the K-theory defect or prove a sharp exponent
there. In particular c_1=sum_i t_i=0, so the degree-three K-theory order
detector below vanishes after this coefficient change. An arbitrary
coefficient-family quotient can remove a sharp-order class.

## 3. The difference trace stays in the source

Let B=S^u[X]/F(X), a free S^u-module of rank m. With L in A^u, define
the S^u-linear operator

    T(H)=Tr_(B tensor A^u / A^u)(H(L-X)), H in S^u[Y].   (3)

This is a LINEAR operator, not a ring homomorphism. Write p_r(c) for
the power sums of F's roots and P_r(f) for the power sums with elementary
coefficients f_1,...,f_(m-1),f_m=0. In both cases the zeroth power sum
is m; the zero root is included in the latter convention. The
characteristic polynomial of multiplication by L-X is

    (-1)^m F(L-Y)=sum_(j=0)^m (-1)^j f_j Y^(m-j).

The domain in (3) is S^u[Y], not its root quotient: T need not factor
through F(Y)=0. In particular we never infer T(FG)=0 from F(L)=0.
Congruence (6) below, applied to the full jet polynomial FG, is what
places T(FG) in I^u J^u. This distinction is essential to the lifting step.

Consequently the integral Newton identities give

    T(Y^r)=P_r(f) in J^u, for every r>=0.               (4)

The precise recurrence, with f_m=0 only after evaluation at L, is

    P_0=m,
    P_r=sum_(j=1)^min(r-1,m) (-1)^(j-1) f_j P_(r-j)
          + 1_(r<=m) (-1)^(r-1) r f_r,    r>=1.

Use the same formula with c_j in place of f_j for p_r(c). The integer
r MULTIPLIES f_r; there is no Newton-identity division by r.

Thus T(S^u[Y]) is contained in J^u, and
T(I^u S^u[Y]) is contained in I^u J^u. No division by a discriminant,
a factorial, or a prime occurs. Formula (4) can alternatively be proved
by splitting F into formal roots x_i: it is the identity
sum_i (L-x_i)^r=P_r(f), including the zero difference.

The companion-matrix argument does not require diagonalizability or
distinct roots. In particular no reduced-fiber replacement or deletion
of nilpotents is involved. The root calculation is an alternative check
of a universal polynomial identity, not a justification by point values.

For a polynomial H in the jets f_j(Y), INCLUDING f_m(Y)=F(Y), its
Hasse derivatives at L belong to J^u. Indeed

    f_j(Y+t)=sum_(r=0)^j binomial(m-j+r,r) f_(j-r)(Y)t^r. (5)

For completeness, comparing the coefficient of c_k Y^(j-k-r)t^r in
(5) gives the integer identity

    binomial(m-k,j-k) binomial(j-k,r)
      = binomial(m-j+r,r) binomial(m-k,j-k-r).

Both sides count two disjoint subsets of sizes r and j-k-r in a set
of size m-k. Thus (5) holds in every rank, including j=m.
Products are expanded by the integral Hasse product rule. Taking the
trace of H(L-X) now gives

    T(H)=m H(L)+sum_(r>=1) (-1)^r p_r(c) H^{[r]}(L).

Every p_r(c), r>0, belongs to I^u. Therefore

    T(H) = m H(L) modulo I^u J^u.                      (6)

The distinction from the unsuccessful ordinary trace shortcut is
essential: (3) traces DIFFERENCES L-X, so all its moments lie in J^u.

## 4. Integral binomial-content recurrence

Put b_j=binomial(m,j), 0<=j<=m, and

    B_m=Z[b_1 y,...,b_(m-1)y^(m-1),y^m] subset Z[y].

For d>=0 put d_d=m/gcd(m,d), with gcd(m,0)=m. Its degree-d part is

    (B_m)_d=d_d Z y^d.                                 (7)

Here is an elementary proof, including the needed periodicity. For p|m,
write s=v_p(m). Each b_j is divisible by
p^max(0,s-v_p(j)); for j=m this bound is zero. In a product of total
weight d, the sum of these valuations is at least
max(0,s-v_p(d)): when v_p(d)<s, at least one index j has
v_p(j)<=v_p(d), since otherwise their sum would be divisible by
p^(v_p(d)+1). Conversely, first reduce d modulo m using y^m.
For the residue r<m, write r=q p^s+r_0, 0<=r_0<p^s.
If p^s<m, b_(p^s) is a p-unit; for r_0>0,
v_p(b_(r_0))=s-v_p(r_0), by the lower s base-p digits of m-1.
The product b_(p^s)^q b_(r_0), with absent factors omitted, attains
the required p-valuation. If p^s=m then q=0. Primes not dividing m
cannot divide the content, since b_1^d=m^d is one of the coefficients.
Taking the gcd proves (7).

For an explicit polynomial U_d(z_1,...,z_m) realizing the gcd, start
with U_0=1 and a_0=1. For 1<=d<m compute integers v_(d,j) by extended
Euclid such that

    a_d=gcd_(1<=j<=d)(b_j a_(d-j))
       =sum_(j=1)^d v_(d,j) b_j a_(d-j),
    U_d=sum_(j=1)^d v_(d,j) z_j U_(d-j).                (8)

Then U_d(b_1 y,...,b_m y^m)=a_d y^d=d_d y^d.
For arbitrary d=qm+r, 0<=r<m, put U_d=z_m^q U_r.
This is a finite integral recurrence, not a rational division recipe.

The crucial consequence of (7) is

    B_m intersect y^m Z[y] = y^m B_m.                  (9)

Both sides have degree-d coefficient d_d for d>=m, since
d_d=d_(d-m). Equality (9) is stronger than merely knowing that the
ordinary image has finite index.

## 5. Construction of every homogeneous lift

Let R(z_1,...,z_(m-1)) be integral and homogeneous of weight d, with
weight(z_j)=j. Suppose its ordinary image in Z[y]/y^m is zero.
Set H(Y)=R(f_1(Y),...,f_(m-1)(Y)). Its coefficient specialization is

    H(0,Y)=a Y^d.

If d<m, then a=0; set G=0. If d>=m, (7) shows that a/d_d is an
integer. Set

    G(z)=(a/d_d) U_(d-m)(z),
    K(Y)=F(Y)G(f_1(Y),...,f_m(Y)).

By (9), K(0,Y)=aY^d. Thus E=H-K is in I^u S^u[Y]. Also K(L)=0,
and K is a polynomial in the full jets, so (6) applies to K as well
as to H. We conclude

    m R(f(L)) = T(H) = T(K)+T(E) = 0 modulo I^u J^u.   (10)

This is already a proof of existence of the desired identities. To
exhibit their coefficients, write E(Y)=sum_r E_r(c)Y^r, with each
E_r in I^u, and put V(z)=z_m G(z)-R(z). Let V_r(z_1,...,z_(m-1))
be the coefficient of t^r after the substitution (5), setting z_m=0
afterwards. Then the exact identity in A^u is

    m R(f) = sum_r E_r(c) P_r(f)
             +sum_(r>=1) (-1)^r p_r(c) V_r(f).          (11)

All factors P_r and V_r are integer source polynomials. All their
displayed coefficient multipliers E_r and p_r lie in I^u. Newton's
recurrence computes P_r and p_r; (5), (8), and (11) finish the
construction. Splitting a coefficient monomial off by its first
nonzero c_k gives a literal expression sum_k c_k W_k(c,f) if desired.

For overflow R=z_i z_j, d=i+j>=m, this proves

    m f_i f_j in I^u J^u,                              (12)

in EVERY rank. For lower-degree homogeneous relations it proves the
same assertion without requiring a choice of G. The construction also
works for arbitrary homogeneous words and integer linear combinations;
it is not restricted to prime powers or quadratic presentations.

### A finite integral relation list in every rank

The same U_j also removes the earlier need to work prime by prime when
listing the ordinary kernel. In Z[z_1,...,z_(m-1)], that kernel is
generated by

    z_j-(b_j/d_j) U_j,                                  1<=j<m,
    U_i U_j-(d_i d_j/d_(i+j)) U_(i+j),                   i+j<m,
    U_i U_j,                                           i+j>=m,

where 1<=i<=j<m in the last two lines. All coefficients are integers.
For the second line, this follows either from (7) and multiplication
in B_m, or from the valuation inequality used in its proof.

To prove completeness, the first line makes the quotient generated
by the U_j. The last two lines reduce products to the span of
1,U_1,...,U_(m-1). Their images 1,d_1 y,...,d_(m-1)y^(m-1) are
Z-linearly independent. Thus the quotient maps isomorphically to the
ordinary image. This proves generation of the entire ordinary ideal,
not merely validity of the displayed relations.

All these generators have weight at most 2m-2, for mixed m as well as
prime powers. Applying (11) to this finite list gives a complete
constructive annihilation certificate in arbitrary rank. The list can
have zero or redundant entries. It is a list of ideal generators, not
a bound on the weights or number of abelian-group generators of Delta_m.

## 6. Passage to the entire actual specialization kernel

Any x in J intersect IA is a polynomial in the f_j with coefficients
in S. Reducing those coefficients by epsilon changes x by an element
of IJ, leaving an integral polynomial R(f). Its image in
Z[y]/y^m is zero. Decompose R into homogeneous parts. Each part is
an ordinary kernel relation, so (10), transferred to S, kills it by m
modulo IJ. This proves (2) and therefore (1).

Finiteness does not require S to be Noetherian. Multiplication by f_j
on the rank-m free algebra A has a monic characteristic polynomial.
After augmentation its matrix is multiplication by binomial(m,j)y^j
on Z[y]/y^m, a nilpotent triangular matrix. Thus every nonleading
coefficient of that characteristic polynomial lies in I. The integral
Cayley--Hamilton identity gives

    f_j^m in IJ,  1<=j<m.

Consequently J/IJ is generated as an abelian group by the at most
m^(m-1) monomials product_j f_j^a_j with 0<=a_j<m. Its kernel in the
ordinary image is finitely generated; the m-annihilator makes it finite.
This is a standard characteristic-polynomial argument, not another
claimed new input. It also gives a justified, albeit coarse, homogeneous
support bound in the universal model:

    (Delta_m)_d=0 for d>m*(m-1)^2/2.

Here the grading is the universal grading transported by the earlier
homogeneous descent; K-theory is not being declared intrinsically graded.
The bound is separate from the weight 2m-2 bound on ordinary ideal
generators. No sharp support or multiplicity formula follows from it.

Moreover the universal specialized image surjects onto the specialized
image of every coefficient family in section 2. Both have the same
ordinary image generated by binomial(m,j)y^j, so their kernels also
surject. Thus every such family defect is a QUOTIENT of the universal
Delta_m. This uses the elementary image-quotient argument of IMG-38,
not injectivity of an arbitrary base change.

For the defining SL_m/P1 family, IMG-37 already proves nonvanishing
for m>=3 and exact prime support. IMG-38 supplies a quotient of exponent
m when m=p^s>=3. The following additional argument now proves sharpness
also for every mixed m. Full Smith multiplicities remain undetermined.

### A uniform degree-three class proves exact exponent

For m>=3 put g_3=gcd(m-2,6) and use f_3=F(L)=0 when m=3. The INTEGRAL
source element

    R_m=((m-2)/g_3) f_1 f_2-(3m/g_3) f_3

is an ordinary kernel relation. Both coefficients are integers, since
g_3 divides m-2 and 3m=3(m-2)+6. Direct expansion gives

    g_3 R_m=(m-2)(m-1)c_1^2 L-2m(m-2)c_2 L
                   -(m-2)c_1 c_2+3m c_3.

As m c_2 L=c_2 f_1+c_1 c_2 is in IJ, this implies, in A/IJ,

    [R_m]=((m-2)(m-1)/g_3) [c_1^2 L].                (12a)

The ambient class e=[c_1^2 L] has order EXACTLY m. The upper bound
follows from m c_1^2 L=c_1^2 f_1+c_1^3 in IJ. For the lower bound,
use the following additive functional on weight-three ambient polynomials
with values in Q/Z:

    ell(L^3)=ell(c_1 L^2)=2/m^2,
    ell(c_1^2 L)=1/m,
    ell(c_2 L)=ell(c_3)=ell(c_1 c_2)=ell(c_1^3)=0.

These seven monomials span the weight-three ambient piece. For m>3
there is no root relation in that weight; for m=3 the relation
L^3-c_1 L^2+c_2 L-c_3 has functional value zero. Thus ell is defined
on the actual ambient piece in all m>=3.

The COMPLETE weight-three coefficient-ideal piece is generated by

    c_3, c_1 c_2, c_1^3,
    c_1 f_2, c_1 f_1^2, c_2 f_1, c_1^2 f_1.

Indeed it is c_1 J_2+c_2 J_1+c_3 J_0, and listing the source monomials
of weights 0,1,2 gives exactly these elements. Their functional values
are 0,0,0,0,0,0,1 respectively, hence all zero in Q/Z. Homogeneity
prevents higher-weight relations from changing this calculation.
Consequently ell descends modulo IJ and detects order m for e.
Rational values are used only to DETECT integral torsion, not to justify
source membership by division. The class e need not itself lie in J;
the element R_m in (12a) does, and J/IJ injects into A/IJ.

It follows that R_m has exact order

    m/gcd(m,(m-2)/g_3)
      = m,   if m is odd or divisible by 4;
      = m/2, if m=2 modulo 4.                          (12b)

For the gcd calculation, any common divisor of m and (m-2)/g_3 divides
2. It is 2 precisely when m=2 modulo 4: for even m, g_3 has exactly
one factor 2. This proves (12b) in arbitrary rank.

Combining (12b) with (1) proves exact exponent m except initially in
the m=2 modulo 4 case. There m>=6, R_m has odd order m/2, and IMG-37
independently proves a nonzero 2-primary part. The upper bound (1)
makes that part have exponent 2. Thus the full exponent is again m.
This settles the remaining mixed-prime exponent question. By the
structure theorem for finite abelian groups, Delta_m has a noncanonical
Z/m direct summand for every m>=3.

Sharpness is asserted for the universal model and, by the proved
homogeneous descent, the original defining SL_m/P1 K-image. An arbitrary
coefficient family in section 2 may kill this class: for S=Z and
F(X)=X^m its augmentation ideal is zero and its defect is zero.

The [independent symbolic audit](tools/audit_type_a_k_degree_three.py)
checks the rank-variable identity and every coefficient-ideal generator
against ell, retains the cubic root relation, and checks the gcd reduction
in symbolic residue families. A second check computes the maximal minors
of the coefficient-ideal lattice in coordinates c_1 L^2,c_2 L,c_1^2 L.
Its index is m^2*gcd(binomial(m,2),m^2); adjoining c_1^2 L changes it to
m*gcd(binomial(m,2),m^2), independently giving order m. The audit
uses neither the trace builder nor
finite Smith tables. The generator-completeness argument and the
geometric identification of Delta_m remain written proofs, not a
machine-formalized or externally reviewed proof. Its
[saved report](type-a-k-degree-three-audit.json) records both order detectors.

One part DOES split after the new upper bound: for m=p^s>=3, the
quotient in IMG-38 has a factor (Z/m)^phi(m), phi(m)=(p-1)p^(s-1).
Composing with projection onto that factor gives a surjection from
Delta_m, now a Z/m-module, onto a free Z/m-module. Lifting its basis
gives a section. Hence Delta_m has a noncanonical direct summand
(Z/m)^phi(m). This proves a lower bound on the multiplicity of the
largest factor, not its exact multiplicity, and does not split the
whole mixed-exponent quotient in IMG-38.

Whenever p divides m exactly once, the p-primary defect
is an F_p-vector space. Its dimension is not determined here.

There is a useful explicit form of the conclusion. Let J_0 be the
ordinary image and write its basis as e_d=d_d y^d, 0<=d<m. Define a
Z-linear map

    s:J_0 -> J/IJ,    s(e_d)=d_d [P_d(f)].              (13)

If pi:J/IJ->J_0 is specialization, then

    pi s=m id_(J_0),       s pi=m id_(J/IJ).           (14)

For the first equality, P_d(f) specializes to m y^d for d<m.
For the second, expand a source polynomial ordinarily as sum a_d y^d.
Equations (6)--(10) remove every term of weight at least m and give
m[H]=sum_(d<m) a_d[P_d(f)]. Since d_d divides a_d, this is (14).
The map s is linear, not multiplicative, and sends 1 to m.
It is a transfer on the SPECIALIZED image; it is not an S-linear map
A->J and does not imply the false containment mA subset J.

## 7. Transfer to the full flag without averaging

There is an integral full-flag corollary for the SAME defining
representation and literal root-polynomial source. Let Delta_m^B denote
the specialization defect on SL_m/B. As abelian groups,

    Delta_m^B is isomorphic to (Delta_m)^((m-1)!).       (15)

In particular m Delta_m^B=0. Its exponent is the same as the projective
defect's exponent; its ungraded cyclic multiplicities are multiplied by
(m-1)!. This does not supply the still unknown projective multiplicities.
Together with IMG-37, full-flag faithful specialization also holds
exactly for m=2, and the nonzero defect has exactly the primes dividing m.

Here is the integral base-change argument, including injectivity. Put

    D=Z[f_1,...,f_(m-1)],
    E=Z[d_2,...,d_m],  f_j=e_j(d_2,...,d_m).

The integral Artin basis makes E free over D, of rank (m-1)!, with a
basis containing 1. One proof successively adjoins the ordered roots of
the generic monic polynomial with coefficients f_j: the successive
monic degrees are m-1,m-2,...,1. No group averaging or factorial
inversion is used.

In the universal coefficient model A^u=D[L]. The inverse coefficient
formulas are

    c_k=sum_(j=0)^k (-1)^j binomial(m-j,k-j) f_j L^(k-j),
    f_0=1, f_m=0.

After tensoring with E these become the elementary coefficients of
the m roots L,L-d_2,...,L-d_m. Consequently

    A^u tensor_D E = Z[L,d_2,...,d_m],
    J^u tensor_D E = S^u[E] inside that ambient ring.    (16)

The second equality is an equality of actual embedded images: flatness
of E over D preserves J^u -> A^u, and both sides are generated by S^u
and E. The right side is precisely the full root-polynomial flag source.
Imposing c_m=1 first gives the same statement for the determinant-one
model: the previously proved injection J^u/(c_m-1)J^u into
A^u/(c_m-1)A^u remains injective after this free D-extension.

Now specialize the coefficient ring. Flatness over D preserves the
kernel of the specialized image map and its ordinary embedded image.
Thus, also for the original K-model,

    Delta_m^B = Delta_m tensor_D E.                    (17)

The torus coefficient extension and its augmentation are the same
integral symmetric-coefficient reduction as in IMG-37. Choosing the
Artin basis proves (15). Under the homogeneous grading from the earlier
descent, (17) is a direct sum of degree shifts indexed by S_(m-1), with
shift polynomial product_(j=1)^(m-1)(1+q+...+q^(j-1)).

This corollary uses the standard integral splitting/Artin basis and
exactness of flat base change. It does not identify projective and
full-flag varieties or change the representation.

An [independent full-flag control](tools/verify_type_a_k_flag_transfer.py)
starts from x-y and x-z in the actual rank-six determinant-one SL3 flag
algebra. Complete integral multiplication closure and syzygies give
(Z/3)^4, as predicted by (15), and ordinary factors 1,1,3,3,3,3.
The projective presentation is not imported. The
[saved presentation](type-a-k-flag-transfer-verification.json) is
replayed over Z by the separate SymPy audit. This one control checks
the transfer's interpretation; the general proof is (16)--(17).

### A precise parabolic extension of the transfer

Let P have Levi block sizes n_1,...,n_s, with sum n_i=m, and retain
the full literal defining root source Sym(Q_root)^(W_P). Put
g=gcd(n_1,...,n_s). For every prime p not dividing g, there is a
p-local graded direct-sum decomposition

    (Delta_m^B)_(p) = direct_sum_(w in W_P)
                         (Delta_m^P)_(p)[length(w)].   (18)

In particular the p-primary projective and parabolic defects have the
same exponent and are simultaneously zero. If g=1, (18) holds
integrally, and m Delta_m^P=0. For proper P and m>=3 it then has exact
prime support {p:p divides m} and exponent exactly m. In particular every proper parabolic
is covered when m is prime. The case m=2 still has zero defect.

In fact, when g=1 the entire ungraded finite abelian group is determined
by the projective one:

    Delta_m^P is isomorphic to (Delta_m)^k,
    k=(m-1)!/(n_1! ... n_s!).                          (18a)

The integer k counts cyclic orbits of words with n_i letters of color i:
a stabilizer order divides every n_i, so g=1 makes that action free.
To prove (18a), combine (15) and (18). Each cyclic p-power multiplicity
in |H| copies of Delta_m^P equals that in (m-1)! copies of Delta_m.
The uniqueness of finite abelian-group decomposition gives (18a).
No canonical splitting or ring isomorphism is asserted. This computes
relative multiplicities between these flags, not the unknown projective
multiplicities themselves. At p not dividing g in general, the same
argument gives the rational ratio (m-1)!/|H| between multiplicities;
their integrality follows from (18), not from rounding that ratio.

Here is a proof of the cofree-source hypothesis, rather than an
assumption about invariants. Work over R=Z_(p), or R=Z if g=1. In the
permutation lattice with basis x_1,...,x_m, choose an H=W_P-invariant
linear combination w with coefficient sum 1. Such a choice exists
because the block sizes generate the unit ideal in R. If Q is the
kernel of coefficient sum, then

    V=Q direct_sum R*w, P_poly=Sym_R(V)=D[w],
    D=Sym_R(Q), P_poly^H=D^H[w].

The ordinary block Artin monomials form a basis of P_poly over
P_poly^H. Replace each x_i in those monomials by x_i-w, which belongs
to Q. Expanding powers gives a triangular change of basis with diagonal
1 and coefficients in R[w] subset P_poly^H: all lower exponent vectors
still satisfy the Artin bounds. Therefore the translated monomials are
also a basis. Setting w=0 proves that D is free over D^H on this same
root-polynomial basis, of rank |H| and graded lengths indexed by H.

For the determinant-one K-model, e_m(x)-1 is monic in w with
coefficients in D^H. The quotient has a unique w-remainder of degree
less than m. Taking H-invariants coefficientwise therefore gives

    (P_poly/(e_m-1))^H = D^H[w]/(e_m-1).

This equality does NOT invoke exactness of taking modular invariants.
The full ambient ring is its free base change from D^H to D. The
partial source image S[D^H] base-changes injectively to S[D], exactly
as in (16). Specializing coefficients and taking the image kernel
commutes with this free source extension. This proves (18).

The restrictions matter: when p divides every Levi block size, the
required invariant w with coefficient sum 1 does not exist. No claim
about the m-annihilator at that prime follows from (18). One cannot
divide by |H| to extend this argument.
This family transfer is an application of the standard integral Artin
basis, not a new general theorem that modular invariants are exact.

### An integral bound even at the excluded parabolic primes

The unnormalized relative group trace supplies the weaker bound

    m*g Delta_m^P=0,  g=gcd(n_1,...,n_s),               (19)

for EVERY type A parabolic with this same literal defining source.
These defects are finite: their source images are finite modules over
the Noetherian symmetric coefficient ring, and (19) kills their
finitely generated specialization kernels by a nonzero integer.
This does not assert that m*g is sharp. At the primes excluded from
(18), their exponent is bounded by p^(v_p(m)+v_p(g)); neither equality
nor nonvanishing is asserted there. For g=1 this independently recovers
the m-annihilator, without the cofree-source decomposition.

Here is the exact integral trace argument. Write H=W_P and let H_i
stabilize one chosen letter in the block of size n_i. Its block
decomposition has a singleton, so (18) implies m Delta^(H_i)=0.
The index [H:H_i] is n_i. There are S-linear maps of actual source images

    J_H -> J_(H_i),
    tau_i:J_(H_i) -> J_H,  tau_i(x)=sum_(h in H/H_i) h(x),

with tau_i composed with inclusion equal to n_i times identity.
Indeed S is fixed by H, and relative trace sends a polynomial in
H_i-invariant root polynomials to an H-invariant root polynomial.
These assertions are about sums and products in the actual ambient
character ring, so they do not assume invariant functors are exact.
The same maps exist on ambient rings A_(H_i)->A_H, preserve coefficient
augmentation ideals, and commute with the maps from source to ambient.
They therefore induce maps on the actual specialization kernels, with
the same composite n_i. Applying the known m-annihilator upstairs gives
m*n_i Delta^H=0 for every i. An integer Bezout combination of the n_i
proves (19). The improper parabolic G/G has zero defect, consistently.

This is an application of the usual integral restriction/relative-trace
identity, after the new projective annihilator theorem. The trace is
not divided by its index and is not a ring homomorphism. The main open
parabolic issue is whether one can remove the extra factor g at its
prime divisors, not the existence of any integral bound.

## 8. Independent audit and limits

The [recurrence builder](experiments/type_a_k_integral_recurrence.py)
uses only extended Euclid, Hasse substitution and Newton recurrence;
it uses no Hermite normal form or ideal-membership solver. Eighteen
implementation controls include lower relations, overflow products,
mixed-prime ranks and words of degree greater than 2m. A
[separate native auditor](tools/audit_type_a_k_integral_recurrence.py)
replays their exact integer coefficient-ideal identities, without
importing Sage, SymPy, or the builder. Seven are also replayed in formal
root variables using products of elementary symmetric functions rather
than the coefficient formula. The [report](type-a-k-integral-recurrence-audit.json)
also checks 60,297 content-recurrence steps and 135,746 binomial identities.

A [separate companion-matrix check](tools/verify_type_a_k_trace.py)
verifies 176 trace moments over Z[L,c_1,...,c_m], retaining f_m until
the root quotient, and 286 Hasse identities with the rank itself
symbolic. Its [report](type-a-k-trace-verification.json) also checks
the invariant-ideal obstruction below. These are independent
implementations and proof audits in this session, not external review.
The all-rank proof is sections 3--6, not extrapolation from the checks.

For a short example, the recurrence produces, for m=3,

    3 f_2^2 = c_1^2 f_2 - c_1 c_2 f_1 - 9 c_1 c_3
                + c_1 f_1 f_2 + 3 c_2^2 - c_2 f_1^2 + 9 c_3 f_1.

Every term visibly has a positive coefficient parameter and a source
factor. Formula (11) supplies such identities in arbitrary rank.

The characteristic map from the full weight-lattice source is different
from the restricted root-polynomial K-source here. The general
characteristic-map theorem with the torsion index inverted does not by
itself compute this integral specialization kernel; localizing at m
would remove the very defect at issue. We are not identifying m with
the torsion index of simply connected SL_m. Nor can the integral ideal of positive Weyl
invariants alone supply the bound. For A2, with root coordinates a,b,

    q=a^2-ab+b^2,
    r=(a+b)(2a-b)(a-2b),

the degree-four quotient by positive invariants has, after reducing by
q, relation matrix [[-3,6],[-6,3]] on a*b^3,b^4. Its Smith factors are
3 and 9. Degree-four invariants add only a multiple of q^2. This
does not contradict (1): our coefficient-dependent source relations
are stronger than that invariant ideal.

Still unresolved: the full defect multiplicities in arbitrary rank,
and sharp bounds for other Springer sources, representations, or the
parabolic primes excluded after (18). Bound (19) is not claimed sharp.
The recurrence proves containment m K_0 subset K_sp for the ordinary
and specialized-source relation ideals. It does not prove equality:
some source relations already vanish without the factor m. A complete
presentation still requires the additional relations between these
two ideals. The next justified task is
to study the graded defect using (11), now that its exponent is known,
rather than continue a rank-by-rank search for annihilation witnesses.
More precisely, for p|m and 1<=r<=v_p(m), seek a uniform formula for

    H_(p,r)(q)=sum_d dim_Fp(p^(r-1)(Delta_m)_d /
                                      p^r(Delta_m)_d) q^d.

The coefficient of q^d in H_(p,r)-H_(p,r+1) is the multiplicity of
Z/p^r in degree d, and H_(p,v_p(m)+1)=0. These layer polynomials would
therefore determine the full Smith factors. The degree-three order
detector provides a constraint on the top layer, not that formula.
