# Faithful specialization of defining type A projective Springer K-images

27 October 2026. IMG-37. Written all-rank deduction, with independent exact
identities and complete finite module computations. No external review or
literature-priority claim. The Cayley-fiber program is shelved for this phase.

## 1. The theorem and its connection to the Springer--Borel map

Let G=SL_m(C), m>=2, rho the defining representation, P=P1, and
G/P=projective (m-1)-space. Use the normalized trace Springer morphism and
the FULL literal integral polynomial-character source, transported to
polynomial coordinates:

    A_source = Sym_Z(Q_root)^(S_(m-1)) = Z[E_1,...,E_(m-1)],
    E_j = e_j(x_1-x_2,...,x_1-x_m).

The character pullback satisfies theta^*(x_i-x_j)=t_i-t_j. Thus this is
the same polynomial-character source as the earlier Springer--Borel
construction, now mapped by the associated-bundle characteristic map
to K-theory. It is not the full representation ring of the Levi, nor
an arbitrary source whose cohomological images happen to be integral.

Let R=Z[t_1^+-1,...,t_m^+-1]/(product t_i-1), I=(t_i-1), and

    A_T=K_T^0(G/P)=R[L]/product_i(L-t_i),
    J_T=R[ kappa_T(theta^*E_1),...,kappa_T(theta^*E_(m-1)) ].

Let J be the corresponding ordinary image in K^0(G/P). Coefficients
are included in J_T. The specialization defect is the abelian group

    Delta_m = ker(J_T/I J_T -> J)
            = (J_T intersect I A_T)/(I J_T).

**Theorem.** Delta_2=0. For every m>=3, Delta_m is finite and nonzero,
and its prime support is EXACTLY the set of primes dividing m:

    (Delta_m)_(p) != 0  if and only if  p divides m.

Furthermore m^(m-1) annihilates Delta_m. Hence faithful specialization
holds in this defining projective type A family exactly when m=2.
The bound is not claimed sharp. The theorem does not give all p-primary
exponents or multiplicities in arbitrary rank.

This contrasts with the defining Sp_(2n)/P1 K-image, which specializes
faithfully in every rank. It is compatible with the existing ordinary
type A result: agreement of ordinary H and K image Smith factors does
not imply that their EQUIVARIANT image modules specialize faithfully.

## 2. Reduction to symmetric coefficients

Put S=Z[c_1,...,c_(m-1)], c_0=c_m=1, and

    F(L)=sum_(k=0)^m (-1)^k c_k L^(m-k),
    A_S=S[L]/F(L),
    f_j(L)=sum_(k=0)^j (-1)^k binomial(m-k,j-k)c_k L^(j-k),
    J_S=S[f_1,...,f_(m-1)] subset A_S.

Here c_k=e_k(t_1,...,t_m). Comparing coefficients in F(L+u), and using
F(L)=0, identifies f_j with the elementary symmetric function of the
m-1 differences from the selected root L. In particular

    f_1=mL-c_1,
    f_2=binomial(m,2)L^2-(m-1)c_1 L+c_2.

The actual torus ring R is finite free over S, of rank m!: start with
the usual free symmetric-polynomial extension, invert the product of
the variables, and set that product equal to one. Equivalently use
the integral Artin monomial basis of the symmetric-group extension.
Flatness preserves the inclusion J_S in A_S, and its base change is
exactly J_T, since both are generated by the same polynomials.

Augmentation sends c_j to binomial(m,j). Therefore

    J_T/I J_T = Z tensor_S J_S.

We may compute the defect over S without confusing symmetric coefficient
specialization with specialization of arbitrary Weyl-invariant modules.

The S-module J_S is finite because it is a submodule of the finite free
S-module A_S over a Noetherian ring. More explicitly mL=f_1+c_1 lies
in J_S, so m^(m-1) A_S is contained in J_S. Put C=A_S/J_S. The exact
sequence and freeness of A_S give

    Delta_m = Tor_1^S(C,Z).

It follows that Delta_m is finitely generated over Z and killed by
m^(m-1). In particular it is finite and has no primes outside m.
These homological facts are standard; the nonvanishing proof is below.

## 3. General local-freeness and determinant obstruction

The following argument applies to finite full-rank image modules, not
just this representation. Let S be a polynomial ring over Z, equipped
with an integer-valued augmentation, A a finite free S-module of rank r,
and J a submodule containing N A for some nonzero integer N. Assume its
ordinary image J_0 in A_0 is full rank. For a prime p let

    m_p=(p, augmentation parameters).

**Local-freeness criterion.** The p-local specialization kernel vanishes
if and only if J_(m_p) is free of rank r over S_(m_p).

Proof. If specialization is faithful, its localization is J_0 tensor
Z_(p), a free rank-r module. Reducing further modulo p shows that r
elements generate J_(m_p), by Nakayama. The resulting surjection from
S_(m_p)^r has zero kernel: the kernel is a rank-zero submodule of a free
module over a domain. Conversely a free rank-r J_(m_p) specializes to
a free rank-r Z_(p)-module, and its surjection onto the full-rank ordinary
image has zero kernel. QED.

**Determinant obstruction.** Suppose, in addition,

    J_(p)=A_(p), but (J_0)_(p) is a proper submodule of (A_0)_(p).

Then the p-local specialization kernel is nonzero. Here (p) is the
height-one generic characteristic-p prime, NOT the augmentation maximal
ideal m_p.

Proof. Otherwise the criterion gives a square inclusion matrix for
J_(m_p) in A_(m_p). Its determinant D is nonzero. Since N kills the
cokernel, the square inclusion matrix M has a matrix B with MB=N times
the identity. Hence D divides N^r. In the local UFD S_(m_p),
all prime factors of D must therefore be p. Thus D is a unit times a
power of p. Equality at the height-one prime (p) forces that power to
be zero. The inclusion is an isomorphism at m_p, contradicting the
proper ordinary image. QED.

This is a deduction from standard Nakayama, localization and determinant
arguments. It is not a new general theorem about flatness. Its useful
content here is a test using generic characteristic-p image recovery,
rather than computing every integral syzygy in every rank.

## 4. Generic characteristic-p image recovery in every rank

Fix p dividing m. Work first in A_S tensor Frac(S/p), where c_1 and
every other nonzero coefficient polynomial are units. It suffices to
recover L from the f_j. This makes the entire image equal to A modulo
p at (p); Nakayama applied to the finite cokernel then gives J_(p)=A_(p).

If p is odd, or if p=2 and 4 divides m, the displayed formula for f_2
reduces to

    f_2 = c_1 L+c_2 (mod p).

The coefficient of L^2 is zero, and -(m-1)=1 modulo p. Thus L is in
the generic characteristic-p image.

It remains to handle p=2, m=2 mod 4, m>=6. Put
delta=binomial(m,4) mod 2. The binomial coefficients, or their binary
digits, give

    f_2=L^2+c_1 L+c_2,
    f_4=delta L^4+c_3 L+c_4                   (mod 2).

Indeed binomial(m-1,3) and binomial(m-2,2) are even. Therefore

    f_4+delta(f_2^2+c_1^2 f_2)
      =(c_3+delta c_1^3)L+c_4+delta(c_2^2+c_1^2 c_2).

The coefficient c_3+delta c_1^3 is a nonzero polynomial in S/2, so it
is invertible at its generic point. Again L is recovered. The generator
f_4 is available because m>=6. For m=2 it is absent, which is essential.

At ordinary augmentation, write v=L-1. Then A_0=Z[v]/v^m and
f_j=binomial(m,j)v^j. Every element of the ordinary image has its
v coefficient divisible by m. Hence J_0 is proper at every p dividing m.
Apply the determinant obstruction for each such p. This proves the
nonvanishing and exact prime support for all m>=3, without extrapolating
from the finite computations.

For m=2, J_S has the free basis 1,2L: the relation L^2=c_1 L-1 gives
(2L)^2=2c_1(2L)-4. Its inclusion matrix specializes with determinant 2,
which is nonzero over Z. Thus Delta_2=0, completing the classification.

## 5. Explicit complete SL3 module and defect

For m=3 write a=c_1, b=c_2, so L^3-aL^2+bL-1=0. Put

    u=3L, v=3L^2-2aL, x=a^2L, y=a^2L^2.

Then the FULL image is the S-module generated by 1,u,v,x,y. First the
original generators are u-a and v+b. Also

    2x=uv-av+3bu-9,      3x=a^2u,
    y=v^2+3bv-3u+3a+ab*u.

Thus all five elements belong to the image. Direct reduction using the
monic cubic proves their span is closed under multiplication; the native
audit verifies all fifteen unordered pair products with integer polynomial
coefficients. This proves generation of the entire algebra, not just a
bounded source-degree calculation.

The full relation module is free on

    3x-a^2u=0,       3y-a^2v-2ax=0.

To see completeness, compare coefficients of L^2 in a relation, obtaining
3b_v+a^2 b_y=0. Since S/3 is a domain and a is nonzero there, b_y=3k
and b_v=-a^2 k. The L coefficient then gives b_x=-2ak+3l and
b_u=-a^2 l. The constant coefficient is independently zero. This proves
exactness of the two-relation presentation.

At augmentation a=b=3, the two nonzero Smith factors of the relation
matrix are 3,3. Consequently

    J_T/I J_T = Z^3 direct-sum (Z/3)^2,
    Delta_3 = (Z/3)^2.

For explicit kernel generators one can use the classes of x-3u and
y-3v-6u. The ordinary image is free of rank three, so the entire torsion
subgroup above is exactly the specialization kernel. This does not put
torsion in the ambient K^0(projective plane), which remains torsion-free.

## 6. Complete finite computations and independent controls

The [integral closure calculation](experiments/type_a_k_specialization.py)
starts with 1 and all f_j, forms a strong module standard basis over
Z[c_1,...,c_(m-1)], and repeatedly adjoins products by the f_j until every
product reduces to zero. Closure proves the resulting module is J_S.
Syzygies are then computed over the same integral ring and augmented;
the torsion in their integer cokernel is Delta_m. Rational Groebner bases
would lose exactly the information at issue and are not used.

| m | Complete finite specialization kernel |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 0 |
| 3 | (Z/3)^2 |
| 4 | Z/2 direct-sum (Z/4)^2 |
| 5 | (Z/5)^20 |
| 6 | (Z/3)^10 direct-sum (Z/6)^6 |

Reports are stored as experiments/type-a-k-specialization-m.json. They
include the full generating matrix and syzygies. A second monomial order
checks the finite answers; this is a separate computation in the same
engine, not an independent implementation. The original ordinary image
Smith lists are also recovered independently by the binomial gcd recurrence.

The [native SymPy audit](tools/audit_type_a_k_specialization.py) imports
neither Sage nor the closure calculation. It checks the SL3 full module,
the two specialized relations, the modular recovery identities in ranks
3 through 128, and the Sp augmentation determinant in ranks 1 through 8.
The all-rank proof of the modular identities is section 4, not this sweep.
An additional Sage check compares the inputs with the existing character
API's fixed-point restrictions and opposite-Schubert expansions for
m=3,...,6 (68 restrictions and 14 expansions). The existing Sage 9.5
SL2 API path raises a one-variable Laurent coercion error; it is recorded
in the verification report, not silently counted as a passed API check.
The SL2 mathematical control is verified by the direct two-by-two
presentation. The public API is unchanged in this research phase.

Controls: the previously established SL4 COHOMOLOGICAL defect is Z/2 in
polynomial degree five. The present SL4 K defect is a different, ungraded
abelian group. For the defining symplectic projective family, the free
image basis specializes with nonzero determinant 2^(n(2n-1)), so its kernel
is zero. Ordinary H/K agreement in defining type A and faithful Sp
specialization therefore cannot be promoted to automatic faithfulness.

## 7. Published inputs, limits and next step

Published inputs: Kumar's polynomial-character construction in
[Part I, Definition 4 and Theorem 5](https://kumar.math.unc.edu/papers/kumar76.pdf);
the associated-bundle/equivariant characteristic framework, for example
[Griffeth--Ram](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0405333); the projective bundle
presentation; the integral symmetric-polynomial free extension; and
[Nakayama's lemma](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00DV), elementary
Tor and determinant arguments. The source identification and ordinary
type A comparison are prior repository inputs. The all-rank faithful-
specialization classification and its exact prime support are the written
deductions made here. They do not alter the maps in the Kumar papers.

Targeted searches on 27 October for polynomial Springer characters,
equivariant K-images and characteristic-map specialization did not locate
this precise statement. Much search material concerns nilpotent Springer
VARIETIES, a different problem. This was a limited literature check, not
an exhaustive novelty audit or external mathematical review.

Remaining: arbitrary-rank p-primary exponents and multiplicities of
Delta_m; a compact uniform presentation of the image and its relations;
nonprojective parabolics; and other representations. The local-freeness
and determinant criteria apply more generally, but the modular L-recovery
proof uses the defining projective type A source. No transfer to arbitrary
flags is claimed. No public API or PDF change is included.

The next theorem target is the p-primary size of Delta_m, starting by
deriving a uniform presentation near the augmentation prime. This now
addresses a known nonzero obstruction, rather than testing faithfulness
rank by rank. A finite list of more Smith computations is not a substitute.
