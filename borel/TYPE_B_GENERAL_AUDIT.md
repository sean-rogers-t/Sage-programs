## Dependency-by-dependency review

1. **Chosen lattice, B/C comparison, and block duality.** The source is the
   integer span of monomials in the fixed normalized squared-tail inputs,
   not the entire representation-ring intersection or ambient cohomology.
   The head/tail change is integral: invert the squared elementary series
   and transpose Schur partitions. The C image is primitive by the
   quaternionic integral Schur basis and fibration. The published B/C
   comparison scales the coefficient rows by `2^nu`; it does not change
   source coordinates. Interchanging blocks preserves the full Smith
   multiset, without asserting preservation of individual prefixes.
2. **Integral prefix reduction.** Signed Schubert cells with `nu <= t`
   form the stated closed incidence locus. The resolution has explicit
   sections over those cells, so its homology pushforward is surjective
   integrally. The relative quaternionic fibration has a free even
   cohomology base and fiber; its integral Serre spectral sequence gives
   a second primitive pullback. Both maps have integral left inverses,
   preserving row lattices. The quaternionic fiber width is exactly `A`.
   The compact bundle is topological; its globally defined Borel classes
   and integral Leray-Hirsch basis provide the needed argument.
   Panin-Walter supplies an algebraic analogue, not an algebraic structure
   on that compact bundle.
   Littlewood's two-quotient identity, with the empty-core bead counts,
   gives precisely the three stated Schur rectangles. There is no
   unrecorded fiber-basis reduction or Schubert-pairing reversal.
3. **Defect-stratum ranks.** For defect `e` and intersection parameter `j`,
   the two graph-lift dimensions are `e(ell-e-2j)` and
   `(e+j)(c-ell+e+j)`, summing to `ec+j(c-ell+j)`.
   Filter first by defect and then, within the defect stratum, by
   `rank(N|U)=e+j`. The rank loci are relatively closed. Grassmann cell
   refinements and locally trivial affine torsors give the claimed free
   even integral homology, generated by algebraic cycle closures.
   Projectivity identifies compact-support and ordinary Betti ranks.
4. **Balanced incidence and the upper bound.** The exact sequence
   `0 -> K/F -> N^{-1}F/F -> F -> 0` proves total Chern class one.
   Out-of-width middle Schur classes vanish. Top Schur factors already
   fit width `A`. Branching therefore gives exactly the prefix rows.
   The pullback factors through the defect locus, proving the rank bound
   without assuming ambient Chern generation.
5. **The integral bridge.** An invariant `b`-plane and the auxiliary
   balanced `F` can be chosen by vector-space operations over every
   extension of C, including each subvariety's function field. Closing
   the graph yields a degree-one cycle lift. This is stronger than
   surjectivity on complex points and proves integral Chow surjectivity.
   On an unbalanced smooth Grassmann-bundle incidence variety, integral
   dual Chow cycles exist by the Grassmann-bundle formula and Poincare
   duality. Lift their pushforwards through the envelope and apply the
   proper projection formula. This imports the unbalanced coefficient
   rows over Z without using a cohomology pushforward on a singular
   target.
6. **Primitive minor.** Complete-symmetric products in the rectangle
   form an integral basis. Bounded spectator changes are unimodular.
   Every nonidentity Jacobi--Trudi term strictly increases the sum of
   squared indices; negative or out-of-width terms vanish. Projecting
   the selected rows onto their selected columns gives a triangular
   matrix with diagonal one. Thus their span is a direct summand, not
   merely a full-rank rational subspace.
7. **Partition count.** The corner criterion is equivalent to the
   displayed beta-number statistic. The proof covers `b=0`, `b=1`, an
   empty middle interval, both parities of `n`, and `e=0,ell`.
   The weighted three-interval count and the even/odd Gaussian identities
   give the stated polynomials with the grading `q^m` in polynomial
   degree `2m`. The statistic counts multiplicities and does not identify
   each original Schur generator with an individual Smith vector.
8. **Conclusion and absence of circularity.** A primitive sublattice and
   the prefix have the same rank by the Betti bound and partition count,
   so they are equal. The nested primitive prefixes admit an adapted
   integral basis; their rank increments give the powers of two.
   Ambient integral Chern generation follows afterward. It is not used
   to establish the integral bridge or the rank bound.

The review separately checked the prefix dependencies, the geometric
cycle argument, and the symmetric-function/combinatorial argument.
The conclusion rests on the displayed mathematical steps, not on the
number of reviewers or finite checks.

## Published inputs and limits

The [status map](PROOF_STATUS.md) records precise source locators.
Parts II and III give the maps, generator images, chosen integral forms,
and stable complex injectivity; they are not relabeled Smith
classification theorems. BKT, Pragacz--Ratajski, Panin--Walter and
Littlewood/SXP provide the Schubert and quaternionic inputs.

The primary text of Chari--Schneider--Shereen--Wand,
[*Modules with Demazure Flags and Character Formulae*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.5191),
SIGMA 10 (2014), 032, was checked at Section 2.1, Theorem 3.3(ii)--(iii),
and Remark 3.4. Its complex Demazure-character formulas give the same
polynomial expression; they do not themselves specify the integral
Springer-image lattice. Classical invariant-subspace/profile counting
also predates this comparison. Novelty of the additional integral
identification is unestablished.

The existing [general verification report](experiments/type_b_general_smith_verification.json)
records 585,243 corner/bead comparisons in 72 parameter cases and 21 exact
integer LR module comparisons through smaller-block size eight. The
[high-layer report](experiments/type_b_high_layers_verification.json)
is an independent check of the top layers. Those reports retain their
original computational scopes and hashes; the proof and verifier were
not changed merely to refresh an audit date. Finite verification does
not establish the arbitrary-rank theorem.

A fresh [standalone pure-Python checker](experiments/verify_type_b_general_audit.py)
imports neither Sage, the library, nor earlier verifiers. Its
[recorded run](experiments/type_b_general_audit_verification.json) checks
nine balanced/near-balanced cases from `n=14, ell=7` to `n=22, ell=11`:
1,430,949 partitions, 16,200,912 corner-membership comparisons, 88 exact
weighted polynomial comparisons, and nine full rectangle Gaussian
identities. All pass. The report records script/proof byte hashes,
confirms the proof stayed unchanged during the run, and records Python
3.12.14 and 44.541786 seconds. This extends finite corroboration, not the
logical scope of the proof.
