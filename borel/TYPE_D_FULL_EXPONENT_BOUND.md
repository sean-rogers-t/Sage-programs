# A general bound on the full type-D Smith exponents

**Status: internally audited repository deduction, 19 October 2026.**
This note proves a bound for the full normalized image, including
square/Euler gluing. It is independent of the subsequent
[general gluing classification](TYPE_D_EVEN_GLUING_THEOREM.md).
It is unpublished, with no originality claim or external expert certification.

## Statement

Let \(a\geq1\), \(k\geq2\), and
\(X_D=\operatorname{OG}(a,2(a+k))\). For the fixed normalized
tail generators
\[
q_i=e_i(x_{\rm tail}^2),\qquad
p=\prod x_{\rm tail},\qquad p^2=q_k,
\]
every full-image Smith factor divides
\[
                              2^{\min(a,k)}.
\tag{1}
\]
Indices concern saturation in the selected rational image, and units
are retained. The same argument applies to the \(SO(2)\)-tail boundary
\(k=1\), with its two-fork parabolic interpretation.

## The bound from the original head

Let \(A\) be the subring of integral cohomology generated by the head
Chern classes and the specified tail Euler class \(p\). The published
ordinary eta formula expresses every Schubert class with denominator
at most \(2^a\). A type-zero class uses \(2^{-\nu}\), where
\(\nu\) is the number of parts greater than \(k\). A positive-type
class has at most one additional half, and its part equal to \(k\)
ensures \(\nu+1\leq a\). The other factors are integral Chern
polynomials and \(p\). Hence
\[
                              2^aH^*(X_D;\mathbb Z)\subseteq A.
\tag{2}
\]
This is a denominator consequence of
[Buch--Kresch--Tamvakis, Theorem 2](https://math.umd.edu/~harryt/papers/oggiam.pdf).

There is an integral additive retraction from \(A\) to the selected
full image \(L=\mathbb Z[q,p]\): in an expression
\(F_0(x_{\rm head})+pF_1(x_{\rm head})\), delete every monomial
with an odd exponent in any head root. Reduction of \(p^2\) and the
total squared elementary relations express the answer in \(L\).

To justify descent, over \(\mathbb Q\) this coefficient projection is
averaging over all head sign changes while leaving the tail roots
fixed. Every squared invariant in the complete-flag characteristic
ideal is fixed, and the global Euler generator is taken to itself or
its negative. Thus the rational ideal is preserved. Coefficient
deletion is integral and commutes with head permutations, so it maps
integral Chern polynomials to integral Chern polynomials. Torsion-free
cohomology and injective complete-flag pullback then give integral
descent to \(A\).

The retraction fixes the selected rational span. If
\(z\in\operatorname{Sat}_H(L)\), (2) places \(2^az\) in \(A\),
and applying the retraction places it in \(L\). Every factor therefore
divides \(2^a\).

## The bound from the specified tail

Pass to a complete type-D flag and permute the original \(k\) tail
roots to the head of
\[
                         X'=\operatorname{OG}(k,2(a+k)).
\]
The complete-flag pullbacks from both partial flag manifolds are
primitive by integral Schubert Leray--Hirsch. Root permutations act by
integral cohomology automorphisms on the compact complete flag. Thus
they preserve the selected Smith factors, as explained explicitly in
[the two-tail proof](TYPE_D_TWO_TAIL_GLUING.md#2-moving-the-two-specified-roots-to-the-head).

On \(X'\) the source has become the head invariant algebra
\[
L'=\mathbb Z[e_1(x^2),\ldots,e_{k-1}(x^2),e_k(x)],
\tag{3}
\]
which is contained in the head Chern subring \(C'\). Here the Euler
generator is the head top Chern class. It is not the Euler generator
of the new complementary tail.

Define a coefficient projection on integral head-symmetric
polynomials by retaining precisely the two exponent parity patterns
\[
                          (0,\ldots,0),\qquad(1,\ldots,1).
\]
Its image is
\(\mathbb Z[x^2]^{S_k}\oplus e_k(x)\mathbb Z[x^2]^{S_k}\),
namely (3). Over \(\mathbb Q\) this is the Reynolds projection for
the group of even head sign changes. Those changes fix both the
squared invariants and the global Euler invariant in the rational
complete-flag ideal. The same integral descent argument as above
therefore gives an additive retraction
\[
                                  C'\longrightarrow L'.
\tag{4}
\]
In particular \(C'\cap(L'\otimes\mathbb Q)=L'\).

All head Chern polynomials are fixed by the diagram involution for
the new complementary tail. Consequently the selected rational span
lies in its plus eigenspace. That integral eigenspace has the basis
\(Y_\lambda\) consisting of type-zero Schubert classes and sums of
the two typed classes. Its Giambelli formula has only the denominator
\(2^{\nu(\lambda)}\), with \(\nu(\lambda)\leq k\). There is no
additional typed half for a sum. Therefore
\[
                             2^kH^*(X';\mathbb Z)^+\subseteq C'.
\tag{5}
\]
Every integral class in the selected rational span is in this
eigenlattice. Applying (4) to (5) proves that its \(2^k\)-multiple
lies in \(L'\). The original Smith factors thus divide \(2^k\)
as well as \(2^a\), proving (1).

This uses a change of the selected algebra after permuting roots. It
does not assert symmetry of the two full tail-image problems.

## Consequence for gluing

In the [two-flag gluing rule](ORTHOGONAL_SMITH.md#lattice-lemma), a
gluing pair at sector exponents \((i,j)\) produces
\((\min(i,j),\max(i,j)+1)\). Write \(\ell=\min(a,k)\).
Equation (1) rules out every gluing pair with \(\max(i,j)=\ell\).
This bound alone does not rule out positive-level gluing below that
top level. The separate
[general gluing theorem](TYPE_D_EVEN_GLUING_THEOREM.md) now excludes
all such gluing by proving the full multiplicity formula.

For literal defining/vector polynomial-character inputs, the fixed
normalization scales each degree-\(d\) factor by \(2^d\); the
corresponding bound is \(2^{d+\min(a,k)}\).
