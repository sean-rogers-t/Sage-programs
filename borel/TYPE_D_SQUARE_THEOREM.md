# The general type-D square-sector comparison

**Status: internally audited complete repository deduction, 19 October 2026.**
This note supplies an integral argument in every rank. Its relative
lemmas have received fresh adversarial review within this session.
The Smith conclusion is unpublished, without an originality claim or
external expert certification. This theorem concerns the square sector;
it does not settle its integral gluing with odd Euler multiples in the
full D image.

## 1. Statement and the existing one-sided comparison

Let \(a,k\geq1\), \(n=a+k\), and put
\[
X_D=\operatorname{OG}(a,2n),\qquad
X_B=\operatorname{OG}(a,2n+1).
\]
Use the fixed normalized inputs
\[
q_i=e_i(x_{a+1}^2,\ldots,x_n^2),\qquad 1\leq i\leq k.
\]
In D, \(q_k=p^2\), where \(p=x_{a+1}\cdots x_n\). Only
polynomials in the \(q_i\) are included. Polynomial degree \(d\) means
cohomological degree \(2d\). Smith lists retain units and concern
saturation in the selected rational image.

**Theorem.** The normalized D square-sector Smith list equals the
corresponding normalized B Smith list in every degree. More precisely,
in a common integral square-input Schur basis, the coefficient-row
lattices are equal.

The common kernel and rectangle basis, integral block duality, and
\[
                        \mathcal R_D\subseteq\mathcal R_B
\tag{1}
\]
are proved in [TYPE_D_SQUARE_COMPARISON.md](TYPE_D_SQUARE_COMPARISON.md).
The inclusion follows from integral restriction along
\(X_D\hookrightarrow X_B\). The new work is the reverse inclusion.

By the proved integral square-sector block dualities in both types, we
may assume \(a\leq k\). All type-B prefix primitivity statements below
are used in this smaller-head convention. A result for the original
block order follows from those same dualities.

This reduction transports the stronger row-lattice assertion as well.
Partial-to-full flag pullback is primitive and therefore preserves
the coefficient-row module exactly. On a complete flag, a permutation
of the coordinate roots is an integral cohomology automorphism.
Inverting the two squared elementary series then interchanges head
and tail Schur sources by the same conjugation and degree signs in B
and D. Thus block exchange changes both row lattices by the same
unimodular source-coordinate map. Their equality in the smaller-head
convention implies their equality in the original common coordinates.

Use head Schur inputs
\[
f_\lambda=s_\lambda(x_1^2,\ldots,x_a^2),\qquad
\lambda\subseteq k^a.
\tag{2}
\]
The change from the original tail Schur basis is conjugation and
the sign \((-1)^{|\lambda|}\), by inversion of the total squared
elementary series. It is a signed permutation of integral bases.
Fix a polynomial degree \(2m\). Odd-degree square images vanish.

## 2. The exact integral type-B input

For \(0\leq t\leq a\), put \(b=a-t\). Let
\(B_t=\operatorname{Gr}(b,L_0)\), where \(L_0\) is a complex
\(n\)-plane. Write \(S\) for its tautological bundle and
\(W=L_0/S\), of rank \(N=n-b=k+t\). Let
\[
Q_t=\operatorname{HGr}(t,\mathbb H(W))\longrightarrow B_t.
\tag{3}
\]
Here \(\mathbb H(W)\) is the quaternionic bundle whose underlying
complex bundle is \(W\oplus W^*\), after choosing a compatible metric.
It also identifies with the quaternionic complement of \(\mathbb H(S)\)
in the trivial quaternionic \(n\)-plane. The source class on (3) is
the Borel-Schur class of \(\mathbb H(S)\oplus U_Q\), where \(U_Q\)
is the quaternionic tautological \(t\)-plane.

The following integral facts are proved in
[TYPE_B_FILTRATION.md](TYPE_B_FILTRATION.md) and
[TYPE_B_GENERAL_SMITH.md](TYPE_B_GENERAL_SMITH.md):

1. The coefficient-row lattice of (2) on \(Q_t\) is the symplectic
   prefix \(P_t\), generated by rows with at most \(t\) negative entries.
2. Every \(P_t\) is primitive in the common source coordinate lattice.
   Equivalently, the source image in \(H^*(Q_t;\mathbb Z)\) is
   primitive in its rational span, with any kernel retained.
3. Exact B/C row rescaling gives
   \[
                         \mathcal R_B=\sum_{t=0}^a2^tP_t.
   \tag{4}
   \]

Item 2 is the integral triangular squeeze in the general B proof,
not an inference from rational rank. These inputs are independent of D.
Primitivity of this particular symmetric source image is essential;
primitive restriction of arbitrary all-even full-flag images would be
a false stronger assertion.

We will also use the ordinary complete flag bundle
\(F_t=\operatorname{Fl}(W)\to B_t\), and write
\[
                         Q'_t=Q_t\times_{B_t}F_t.
\]
The pullback \(H^*(Q_t;\mathbb Z)\to H^*(Q'_t;\mathbb Z)\)
is primitive: its complete-flag fiber has free even cohomology and
the integral Serre spectral sequence collapses. Consequently the
source image on \(Q'_t\) is primitive and its row lattice is still
exactly \(P_t\). Composition with a primitive injection preserves
a coefficient-row lattice, since it has an integral left inverse.

## 3. A flagged even orthogonal incidence bundle

Now let \(L_0\subset\mathbb C^{2n}\) be maximal isotropic for a
nondegenerate symmetric form. Consider
\[
Y_t=\{(S,\Sigma): S\subset L_0,\ \dim S=b,\
                         S\subset\Sigma\in X_D\}.
\]
Its projection to \(B_t\) has fiber \(\operatorname{OG}(t,2N)\).
Pull back to \(F_t=\operatorname{Fl}(W)\), and denote the result by
\[
                 Z_t=Y_t\times_{B_t}F_t,\qquad j_t:Z_t\longrightarrow X_D.
\tag{5}
\]
Only integral restriction along \(j_t\) will be needed. There is no
need to identify a D Schubert union or its typed pivot conventions.

The quadratic bundle \(S^\perp/S\) has maximal isotropic subbundle
\(W\). Topologically, with compatible metrics and compact forms, it
is the hyperbolic bundle
\[
                              E=W\oplus W^*.
\]
On \(Z_t\), let \(U=\Sigma/S\) be the isotropic \(t\)-plane, and
let \(V=U^\perp/U\), an oriented orthogonal bundle of complex rank
\(2k\). Denote by \(p=e(V)\) the Euler class of its compact real
oriented \(2k\)-plane bundle, whose complexification is \(V\).
The same notation \(W,S\) denotes their pullbacks to \(F_t\).

If \(z_1,\ldots,z_t\) are the formal Chern roots of \(U^*\), the
Euler identities give
\[
p^2=q_k(V),\qquad
c_t(U^*)p=Z,\qquad Z=\pm c_N(W)\in H^{2N}(F_t;\mathbb Z).
\tag{6}
\]
Indeed the compact real bundle for \(E\) decomposes into the real
bundle underlying \(U^*\) and the compact bundle for \(V\);
its Euler class is \(\pm c_N(W)\). A fixed choice of Euler signs
fixes the sign in (6).

Let \(A_t\subset H^*(Z_t;\mathbb Z)\) be the subring generated by
base cohomology \(H^*(F_t;\mathbb Z)\), the Chern classes of \(U^*\),
and \(p\). Both the base and the orthogonal Grassmann fiber have free
even cohomology. Their integral Serre spectral sequence collapses, so
\(H^*(Z_t;\mathbb Z)\) is free and even. The same observations apply
to its relative complete flag spaces.

## 4. The relative eta denominator bound

**Lemma 1.**
\[
                             2^tH^*(Z_t;\mathbb Z)\subseteq A_t.
\tag{7}
\]

The published input is [Tamvakis, *Double eta polynomials and equivariant
Giambelli formulas*](https://math.umd.edu/~harryt/papers/dbleta.pdf):
Definition 1 and equation (4) on PDF pages 2--3, Theorem 1 on page 5,
and the geometrization formulas of Section 4.1, pages 19--20.

Here is the denominator check. A finite typed \(k\)-strict partition
\(\lambda\) has length at most \(t\). Put
\(\nu=\#\{i:\lambda_i>k\}\). Its double eta formula has the outside
factor \(2^{-\nu}\). Every modified Chern factor is an integral
polynomial in Chern classes, the flag line Chern roots, and \(p\):
the apparent expressions \(2b_k-c_k\) and
\(2\widetilde b_k-c_k\) geometrize to \(\pm p\).
For positive type, the distinguished central factor contributes
at most one further factor \(1/2\), through \(b_k=(c_k+p)/2\),
its mate, or \(a_j^s=c_j/2+\cdots\). Positive type requires a part
equal to \(k\), so \(\nu+1\leq\operatorname{length}(\lambda)\leq t\).
For type zero, \(\nu\leq t\). Raising-operator terms create no
additional denominators. Thus \(2^t\) times every finite equivariant
Schubert class is an integral polynomial of the claimed kind.

The geometrization in that source sends \(c_j\) to
\(c_j(E-U-F_N)\), with \(F_N\) the fixed maximal isotropic bundle,
and its Euler expression to \(c_k(E_N-U)=p\). In our bundle
\(F_N=W\). Therefore these Chern classes are integral polynomials
in the base classes and \(c_i(U^*)\).

To apply the equivariant statement integrally, the complete flag of
\(W\) on \(F_t\), split with a Hermitian metric, reduces its structure
group to the diagonal torus of \(U(N)\subset SO(2N)\). The bundle
\(Z_t\) is a pullback of the universal torus mixing space.
Equivariant Schubert classes pull back to global classes restricting
to the integral Schubert basis on every fiber. Integral
Leray--Hirsch therefore makes them a basis over
\(H^*(F_t;\mathbb Z)\). The preceding denominator check proves (7).

No averaging over the ordinary flag Weyl group, division by \(N!\),
or descent from flag-root polynomials to unflagged base classes is
used. The auxiliary ordinary flag remains part of \(Z_t\).

## 5. Compensated parity gives an integral retraction on \(A_t\)

Let \(R'_t\subset H^*(Z_t;\mathbb Z)\) be the image of base
cohomology and symmetric polynomials in \(z_1^2,\ldots,z_t^2\).

**Lemma 2.** There is an integral base-linear additive retraction
\[
                                \rho:A_t\longrightarrow R'_t.
\tag{8}
\]
This is asserted only on the polynomial subring \(A_t\), not on every
integral cohomology class, and is not claimed to be a ring map.

The total squared characteristic series expresses \(q_k(V)\) as an
integral polynomial in base classes and the head squares. Reduce
powers of \(p\) by \(p^2=q_k(V)\). A representative in \(A_t\) then has
the form \(F_0(z)+pF_1(z)\), with \(F_i\) symmetric in the \(t\)
head roots and with coefficients in integral base cohomology.

Keep the monomials of \(F_0\) even in every \(z_i\), and the
monomials of \(F_1\) odd in every \(z_i\). The latter part equals
\((z_1\cdots z_t)G(z_1^2,\ldots,z_t^2)\), so its product with
\(p\) becomes \(ZG\) by (6). This coefficient operation is integral,
commutes with head permutations, takes values in \(R'_t\), and
fixes every element of \(R'_t\).

For independence of representatives, pass to the relative complete
orthogonal flag space refining \(U\). Choose one tail root, possible
because \(k\geq1\). For each head root, reverse that root and the
chosen tail root simultaneously. These commuting double sign changes
belong to \(W(D_N)\); they fix base classes and send \(p\mapsto-p\).
Their rational averaging is precisely the coefficient operation above.

The relative rational full-flag characteristic kernel consists of the
squared characteristic relations of \(E\) and
\(\prod z_{\mathrm{all}}=e(E)\). Each double sign change preserves all
these relations, including the nonzero base Euler class.
Thus the projection preserves the rational kernel. Equivalently,
fiberwise compact full-flag Weyl actions realize the same root
substitutions and fix base classes.

For the latter justification, let \(P(E)\) be the compact oriented
orthonormal frame bundle. Its full flag bundle is \(P(E)/T\).
Right multiplication by a normalizer representative gives a
well-defined map of this bundle, commuting with the frame group's
left action. On its root line Chern classes this is the usual Weyl
substitution. Hence each double sign change preserves every
cohomological relation even for the nontrivial bundle \(E\); the
kernel argument does not depend on identifying the invariant
subgroup of the whole integral cohomology.

Partial-to-complete flag pullback is injective by its collapsing
integral Serre spectral sequence with free even flag fiber. A projected
difference between two equal representatives is zero rationally on
the full flag, hence rationally on \(Z_t\), and then integrally by
torsion-freeness. This proves (8).

The Euler relation is preserved: \(\rho((z_1\cdots z_t)p)=Z\).
An uncompensated projection killing every odd Euler term would fail
when \(Z\ne0\).

## 6. The square ring is the relative quaternionic ring

**Lemma 3.** Sending the Borel classes of \(U_Q\) to the head squared
classes on \(Z_t\) identifies
\[
                           H^*(Q'_t;\mathbb Z)\cong R'_t
\tag{9}
\]
as rings over \(H^*(F_t;\mathbb Z)\).

The relative quaternionic Grassmann presentation and integral Schur
bundle basis are the published inputs used in the B proof: see
[Panin--Walter, Theorems 11.2--11.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.0649)
and [Pragacz--Ratajski, Appendix A](https://www.impan.pl/~pragacz/download/comp3868.pdf).

The underlying complex bundles of the symplectic and orthogonal
hyperbolic bundles are both \(W\oplus W^*\). On \(Z_t\),
\[
                              [E]=[U]+[U^*]+[V].
\]
This identity and \(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb C}V=2k\) give exactly
the squared characteristic relations in the quaternionic bundle
presentation. Odd Chern classes of the self-dual \(V\) vanish here:
they are 2-torsion universally and \(H^*(Z_t;\mathbb Z)\) is
torsion-free. The presentation therefore gives a well-defined
surjection from the left side of (9) to \(R'_t\).

For injectivity, the left side is free over base cohomology on the
Schur classes indexed by \(\mu\subseteq k^t\). Their images restrict
on every D fiber to the square-input Schur classes with precisely
that rectangle index set. Those are rationally independent by the
common square-input kernel proved in
[TYPE_D_SQUARE_COMPARISON.md](TYPE_D_SQUARE_COMPARISON.md).
The Serre filtration now gives independence over base cohomology
over \(\mathbb Q\). Explicitly, the lowest nonzero base filtration
of a purported relation has these independent fiber classes as its
coefficients and cannot vanish. The source of (9) is torsion-free,
so rational injectivity proves integral injectivity.

One can equivalently use rational Leray--Hirsch directly: the same
global double eta classes used in Lemma 1 supply a full rational
fiber basis. The selected Schur classes are an independent constant
linear combination of that basis on each fiber, so their products
with base classes are independent over rational base cohomology.

This is an isomorphism of characteristic-class rings; no geometric map
\(Z_t\to Q'_t\) is asserted.

The exact sequence \(0\to S\to\Sigma\to U\to0\) and the Schur
addition formula identify \(j_t^*f_\lambda\) under (9) with the source
class on \(Q'_t\) associated to \(\mathbb H(S)\oplus U_Q\).
The integral type-B input in Section 2 therefore says that their
image \(L_t\subset R'_t\) is primitive in its rational span, and that
the source row lattice computed in \(R'_t\) is \(P_t\).

## 7. Integral row containment proves the theorem

Let \(T_t\) be the coefficient-row lattice of
\(f_\lambda\mapsto j_t^*f_\lambda\) on \(Z_t\), using any integral
basis of its free cohomology in the fixed degree. By (9), its rational
source kernel equals that on \(Q'_t\). Since \(P_t\) is primitive,
\[
                               \operatorname{Sat}(T_t)=P_t.
\tag{10}
\]

If \(h\) is an integral class in the rational span of \(L_t\), Lemma 1
gives \(2^th\in A_t\). The retraction of Lemma 2 fixes its rational
span, so \(2^th\in R'_t\). Primitivity of \(L_t\subset R'_t\) gives
\(2^th\in L_t\). Every nonzero Smith factor of the source map on
\(Z_t\) thus divides \(2^t\). Transposition preserves nonzero Smith
factors, so (10) implies
\[
                                2^tP_t\subseteq T_t.
\tag{11}
\]
This uses the integral primitivity and denominator lemmas, not just
agreement of rational ranks.

Integral restriction along \(j_t\) expresses each row on \(Z_t\)
as an integer combination of rows on \(X_D\). Consequently
\[
                         2^tP_t\subseteq T_t\subseteq\mathcal R_D.
\]
Summing over \(t=0,\ldots,a\) and using (4) yields
\[
                         \mathcal R_B\subseteq\mathcal R_D.
\]
Together with (1), this proves equality of the integral row lattices
and hence of their nonzero Smith factors. The common source basis
makes these the selected-image factors in the statement.

At \(t=0\), the orthogonal Grassmann fiber is a point; the relative
square ring is simply base cohomology and the bound is \(2^0=1\).
Here \(p=Z\) is a base class, the head sign group is trivial, and
\(A_0=R'_0=H^*(F_0;\mathbb Z)\).
At \(t=a\), the unflagged incidence is \(X_D\); the auxiliary ordinary
flag over the trivial maximal isotropic bundle is retained.
For \(k=1\), the D Grassmannian is the parabolic omitting both fork
nodes. These incidence bundles and Euler classes keep their geometric
meaning, including the low-rank quadric interpretation; no single-node
maximal-parabolic assertion is used there. Block duality then covers
the original ordering of the two block sizes.

## 8. Multiplicities and the three-row case

All closed type-B square-image multiplicity formulas consequently apply
to the D square sector. In particular
[TYPE_B_THREE_ROW_SMITH.md](TYPE_B_THREE_ROW_SMITH.md) gives every
multiplicity \(1,2,4,8\) when \(\min(a,k)=3\), and
[TYPE_B_GENERAL_SMITH.md](TYPE_B_GENERAL_SMITH.md) gives every rank.

With \(\ell=\min(a,k)\), \(n=a+k\),
\(r=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), and \(c=\lceil n/2\rceil\), the
multiplicity polynomial for \(2^e\) is
\[
q^{ec}{r\brack e}_q\,U_{n-2e,\ell-e}(q),\qquad 0\leq e\leq\ell.
\tag{12}
\]
Here \(U\) is the polynomial defined in the general B note, and
\(q^m\) records polynomial degree \(2m\). This counts factors; it
does not assign individual original Schur inputs as Smith vectors.

For the requested three-row case, put \(A=\max(a,k)\geq3\),
\(n=A+3\), \(r=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), and \(c=\lceil n/2\rceil\).
Write \({v\brack1}_q=1+q+\cdots+q^{v-1}\), and interpret a
Gaussian coefficient outside its range as zero. Specializing (12)
gives four positive expressions:
\[
\begin{aligned}
D_0(q)&={c\brack3}_q+
 q^{c-2}{r\brack1}_q{c-1\brack1}_q,\\
D_1(q)&=q^c{r\brack1}_q
 \left({c-1\brack2}_q+q^{c-2}{r-1\brack1}_q\right),\\
D_2(q)&=q^{2c}{r\brack2}_q{c-2\brack1}_q,\\
D_3(q)&=q^{3c}{r\brack3}_q.
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
The coefficient of \(q^m\) in \(D_e\) is exactly the number of
D square-sector factors \(2^e\) in polynomial degree \(2m\).
There are no higher factors in this family. These are direct
specializations of the already proved general B multiplicity
polynomials, transported by the integral comparison above.

The proof does not identify the full D lattice combining the square
image with \(p\) times that image. The additional integral gluing
question remains in [TYPE_D_CANDIDATE.md](TYPE_D_CANDIDATE.md).
For the literal defining/vector character form, multiply every
normalized degree-\(d\) factor by \(2^d\), as explained in
[TYPE_D_SMITH.md](TYPE_D_SMITH.md#literal-polynomial-character-form).

## 9. Scope of the independent algebra check

The ordinary-Python script
[verify_type_d_incidence_projection.py](experiments/verify_type_d_incidence_projection.py)
and its
[report](experiments/type_d_incidence_projection_verification.json)
check the compensated-parity operation independently of Sage, a Borel
engine, or divided differences. They compare actual double-sign group
averaging with coefficient deletion and reduction using
\(p^2=q\), \((z_1\cdots z_t)p=Z\), and
\((z_1^2\cdots z_t^2)q=Z^2\). The base Euler symbol \(Z\) is retained.
They also detect the failure of the uncompensated projection that
would discard its nonzero contribution.

These are finite formal algebra checks. They do not computationally
prove the equivariant eta theorem, its denominator bound, bundle
Leray--Hirsch, or the previously proved type-B prefix primitivity.
Those remain the explicitly identified mathematical dependencies of
the written proof.
