# How to read and challenge the final E8 certificate

This guide accompanies [the proof](../../E8_LAST_ADJOINT_FIBER.md).
It distinguishes explicit identities, replay-dependent memberships,
standard algebraic implications, and the remaining human review work.

## 1. The precise calculation being certified

In P=Q[x,y,w,a,A,B,C], set

    p=xy+wa, q=xw+ya, r=xa+yw,
    f=x^2+y^2+w^2+a^2-p^2-q^2-r^2+xywa
      -Aqr-Bpr-Cpq-BCp-ACq-ABr-ABC-4,
    H0=A^2+B^2+C^2-ABC-4,
    g=(f_x,f_y,f_w,H0).

The actual relative critical scheme is Y=V(g). Let I be generated by
g and every 4-by-4 minor of its FULL 4-by-7 Jacobian, with differentiation
in all seven variables. Removing duplicates and zeros leaves 38
generators. No radical or source-parameter fraction field is substituted.

The needed conclusion is

    dim_C V(I) <= 2.

The central target has dimension three. Pulling this bad locus back to
the original torus along the finite trace map preserves its dimension
bound. Its image under the ORIGINAL central target map is therefore
nondense. After the separately justified ramification exclusions, the
actual remaining incidence is smooth. Generic smoothness and the regular
quotient reduction then prove the group-fiber assertion.

This computation does not give the generic degree of the Springer map.
Nor is it a calculation of the integral Borel image or its Smith factors.

## 2. What is in the compressed files?

The two files
[32003](../../e8-last-32003-certificate.json.gz) and
[32009](../../e8-last-32009-certificate.json.gz) are gzip-compressed JSON,
not opaque binary executables. Polynomials are finite lists of exponent
vectors and exact coefficients. Each records the original affine
generators, 59 selected homogeneous ideal members, their basis indices
in the replay, and their leading exponent vectors. The schema can be
inspected directly with Python's standard `gzip` and `json` modules.

Homogenization adds h (called tau in the note). The replay works on the
ideal generated by the homogenizations of all 38 generators, NOT its
saturation or an assumed projective closure. It stops an exact F4
calculation after degree 20, having produced 15,488 members. It retains
the selected 59. The proof only requires ideal members; it does not
claim the truncated output is a Groebner basis.

The replay uses msolve v0.4.9, source commit
`c547cb83ac3e876a2d72a2cc8ee191b543287d85`, with a small degree-stop patch
and [driver](../../tools/e8_last_f4_driver.c). Its exact sparse linear
algebra mode is selected. The two prime runs use the same engine and
therefore are not two independent implementations of ideal membership.

## 3. The rational identity and the finite-field bound

The written proof supplies a degree-30 polynomial R explicitly as a
polynomial combination of the original homogenized derived equations
and the homogenized H0. No denominator is introduced. The checker
constructs it using the adjugate of the normal linear matrix M.

For weighted degree reverse lexicographic order with weights

    (2,2,2,2,1,1,1,1) on (x,y,w,a,A,B,C,h),

R has leading monomial x^10 y^10 w^6 a h^3 with coefficient 48.
This leading coefficient survives at both chosen primes. The 59
replayed leading monomials together with this one generate a monomial
ideal of dimension four and degree 21. Sage/Singular computes these
invariants; a separate native program checks them by coordinate-support
enumeration and localized standard-monomial counts.

Why is this a characteristic-zero argument? All generators are homogeneous
in the ORDINARY total grading, even though the leading terms use a weighted
order. In each total degree, the ideal component is the image of a finite
integer matrix. Its rank cannot increase modulo a prime. Consequently

    HF_Q(P[h]/J,n) <= HF_Fp(P[h]/J_p,n)
                   <= HF_Fp(P[h]/M_p,n).

Here M_p is the certified leading-monomial ideal, contained in in(J_p).
Thus the homogeneous scheme over Q has dimension at most four and, once
the known dimension-four components are included, degree at most 21.
A single admissible prime suffices for this implication; the second is
a corroborating replay. An affine finite-field dimension calculation
without this homogeneous argument would not justify the conclusion.

## 4. The geometric half of the certificate argument

The proof identifies four distinct dimension-four components at h=0:

    L   = V(A,B,C,h),
    X_A = V(h,A,p,Br+Cq),
    X_B = V(h,B,q,Ar+Cp),
    X_C = V(h,C,r,Aq+Bp).

The three X components are integral complete intersections of degree six.
L has degree one and generic scheme multiplicity at least three. Their
contributions already total 21. Additivity of top-dimensional degree
therefore leaves no room for another dimension-four component away from
h=0. This remains valid with embedded structure: such structure cannot
cancel a positive top-dimensional contribution.

After h is inverted, the homogenized scheme is V(I) times G_m. Its
dimension is at most three, giving dim V(I)<=2. This extra subtraction
of one is essential. The argument does not claim that the whole
homogeneous ideal has been explicitly decomposed, or that the affine
scheme was reduced before removing the bad target image.

The software checks the component containments, normal-order identities,
the determinant 61600 at a rational test point (proving it is nonzero),
and the polynomial height inputs. A reviewer must still assess the
integrality, local multiplicity and degree-exhaustion arguments in the
written proof. A nonzero test value proves that a polynomial is not
identically zero; it is not a claim that it is invertible everywhere.

## 5. Trust and independence

| Part | What is independently checked | Remaining dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restricted character | Root reconstruction and unit elimination in the earlier linked audit; compact residual re-expanded by the native last-case auditor | Earlier root/character audit is a bound input to this replay |
| Rational member R | Exact construction in Sage, separate native normal-jet and divided-difference identities | Correctness of exact arithmetic and written interpretation |
| 59 memberships per prime | Recomputed from the original ideal and compared coefficient by coefficient | Same pinned F4 engine at both primes; no saved multiplier vectors for these 59 members |
| Monomial dimension and degree | Sage/Singular and separate native combinatorial counts | Interpretation as an upper bound on the original ideal |
| Infinity components and transfer | Explicit equations and checked identities | Written algebraic geometry, schemes, ramification and target exclusions |
| Case coverage | Fresh integer roots and all 136 standard cubic transfers | The case proofs themselves, not just their recorded success flags |

One useful strengthening would be to store explicit multiplier vectors
for the 59 memberships, or reproduce them in a different exact engine.
That would reduce the computation's trust requirements. It is not
currently part of the supplied certificate, and we do not describe the
native monomial counter as if it performed that verification.

The manifest detects changed files; it does not prove a mathematical
statement. The runner verifies specific computations; it is not a proof
assistant. The reviewer is being asked to assess the combined argument.
